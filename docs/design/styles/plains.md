# Style brief: plains

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/plains.json`. Biomes: plains,
sunflower plains, meadow, forest, flower forest, birch forest (and cherry
grove, beach by fallback).

## Precedents

Rural farmhouses and cottages: English and French countryside, the American
farmhouse and the vanilla plains village. Light wood walls, shutters, brick
or tile roofs, a porch or a stoop, gardens.

## Materials

Birch plank walls (a stripped birch variant, calm per DS-2), oak and spruce
log posts, cobblestone and andesite plinth, brick and red terracotta roofs.
Interiors oak with spruce accents, cobblestone, yellow, white and light blue
cloth, flowers in pots.

## Proportions and massing

1-2 storeys (3 rarely), box or L plan, broad rather than tall, corner posts.
No jetties, no timber frame (that is medieval).

## Roof

Gable mostly, some hip, 1:1, 1-block overhang, dormers on a quarter of
roofs.

## Windows and doors

1x1 or 1x2 windows with shutters, symmetric fronts, a door with a surround
and a step or porch. Open-plan kitchen and living room usual (DS-10).

## Avoid

Speckled wall mixes (John's "splotchy"); white terracotta and smooth stone in
the wall mix; tall town houses; flat roofs.
