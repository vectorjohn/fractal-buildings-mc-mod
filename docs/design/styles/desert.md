# Style brief: desert

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/desert.json`. Biomes: desert,
badlands (all three), and savanna by fallback (open question from the Phase 5
gate: savanna may want its own style).

## Precedents

North African and Middle Eastern courtyard houses (Fez, Ghadames, Yazd),
Pueblo adobe (Taos), Mediterranean whitewashed towns. Thick walls, few and
small openings, flat roofs used as terraces, courtyards for shade, wind
towers, arcades round market squares.

## Materials

Sandstone family (cut, smooth, plain) for walls, plinths and parapets,
chiseled sandstone trim; acacia beams whose ends show below the roof line
(vigas); acacia doors. Badlands: terracotta in earth tones. Interiors birch
and jungle wood, smooth sandstone, orange, yellow and red cloth, cacti.

## Proportions and massing

1-3 storeys, cubic masses, courtyard and U plans preferred, blank street
walls with the life turned inward. Taller buildings step back in terraces.

## Roof

Flat with a parapet in the wall material; no gables, no overhang.

## Windows and doors

Few, small (1x1), high in the wall, deep in it; blank corners. Doors in a
surround or an arch. Shading over openings (trapdoors, slabs) rather than
glass areas.

## Avoid

Pitched roofs; large glass; wood walls; dark materials that would bake; busy
mixes of the three sandstones on one face (pick one per face, DS-2).
