# Style brief: taiga

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/taiga.json`. Biomes: taiga and both
old-growth taigas (dark forest, pale garden, windswept hills and forests,
stony shore by fallback).

## Precedents

Scandinavian, Russian and Canadian forest timber building: log and plank
houses (izba, Norwegian stue), stave churches, Finnish farmsteads, the
vanilla taiga village. Wood everywhere, a fieldstone footing, steep dark
roofs, carved trim.

## Materials

Spruce plank walls with stripped spruce corner posts, spruce logs, a
cobblestone, mossy cobblestone or andesite plinth, dark oak roofs (one
block per roof, DS-36). Interiors spruce with dark oak accents, cobblestone,
brown, red and gray cloth, ferns.

## Proportions and massing

1-2 storeys, box, L or T plans, log corners; wider than tall.

## Roof

Steep gable (2:1), 1-block overhang, dormers on half; chimneys.

## Windows and doors

Small (1x2, few, wide bays; 1x1 only as accents, DS-33), lintels, symmetric only where the front is short; doors with a
surround. Kitchens often separate.

## Avoid

Light woods (birch) as walls; flat roofs; large glass; stone walls (that is
snowy); everything the same dark brown so the frame vanishes (keep contrast
between posts and walls).
