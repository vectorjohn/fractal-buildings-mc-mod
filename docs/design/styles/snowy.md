# Style brief: snowy

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/snowy.json`. Biomes: snowy plains,
ice spikes, snowy taiga, grove, snowy slopes, snowy beach, frozen and jagged
peaks (stony peaks by fallback).

## Precedents

Alpine and Nordic stone-and-timber buildings: Swiss and Tyrolean houses with
stone ground floors, Norwegian stone-founded farmhouses, Scottish Highland
stone cottages. Compact plans that keep heat, small windows, steep roofs that
shed snow, a big hearth and chimney.

## Materials

Stone-brick walls (a few cracked), spruce and dark oak trim and beams, stone
and andesite plinth, spruce-plank roofs. Interiors spruce with birch accents,
stone bricks, light blue, white and blue cloth, with warm red accents
welcome.

## Proportions and massing

Compact: box or short L, 1-3 storeys, few wings, solid corners.

## Roof

Steep gable (2:1), 1-block overhang, some dormers, a chimney on most
houses (DS-15).

## Windows and doors

Few and small (1x1, sometimes 1x2), wide bays, blank corners, shutters.
Kitchens more often separate (warmth).

## Avoid

Large windows; flat or shallow roofs; long thin plans; open sprawling plans.
