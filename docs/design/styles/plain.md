# Style brief: plain

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/plain.json`. Biomes: the fallback
for any biome no other style claims; the base every other style extends.

## Precedents

No single tradition: a neutral, tidy vernacular, close to a 19th-century
rural or small-town house (Georgian-proportioned box, plain gable). It is the
control group: anything a style adds should be measured against it.

## Materials

White terracotta walls, stripped oak trim, oak beams, cobblestone plinth,
stone-brick roof. Interiors oak and spruce, cobblestone, white and light gray
cloth.

## Proportions and massing

Box, L or T plan, 1-3 storeys, symmetric facades with even bays (2-3 wide),
centred door where the front allows. No jetties.

## Roof

Gable 1:1, no overhang, no dormers.

## Windows and doors

1x2 windows (DS-33), one per bay, aligned; a plain door. Kitchens usually open onto
the living room (DS-10).

## Avoid

Ornament that belongs to another style; asymmetric fronts; high-rises in
these materials (DS-1: a plain high-rise needs stone, brick or concrete
walls, not white terracotta on wood).
