# Style brief: jungle

Status: **proposed** (assistant, 2026-10-25; for John). Style file:
`planner-core/src/main/resources/styles/jungle.json`. Biomes: jungle,
sparse jungle, bamboo jungle (and swamp, mangrove swamp by fallback).

## Precedents

Southeast Asian and tropical vernacular: Malay and Thai stilt houses, Filipino
bahay kubo, Caribbean and colonial tropical houses with verandas. Raised
floors for air and floods, deep eaves for rain, open walls and louvres,
lightweight walls on a heavy frame.

## Materials

Jungle logs as posts and beams, bamboo and jungle planks as walls (small
buildings only), mossy cobblestone footings, jungle-plank roofs with moss.
Town and city buildings in mossy stone bricks, stone and dark terracotta;
high-rises never bamboo (DS-1). Bridge decks in jungle planks alone;
bamboo only on an all-bamboo footbridge (proposed DS-30 amendment; today
the style file's `deck` mixes jungle and bamboo planks cell by cell, a
gap). Interiors jungle wood with bamboo accents,
mossy cobblestone, green, lime and brown cloth, ferns and bamboo in pots.

## Proportions and massing

1-2 storeys, raised on posts where the ground is wet or uneven (not built
yet), verandas on one or more sides, wings with their own hip roofs.

## Roof

Hip mostly, 1:1 or steeper, a deep 2-block overhang; no parapets.

## Windows and doors

Wide openings shaded by the eaves, trapdoor shutters or louvres, fewer
glazed panes. Kitchen open to the living area (DS-10).

## Avoid

Bamboo or plank walls on anything over 3-4 storeys; flat roofs; small deep
windows (that is desert); walls flush with the roof edge (no eaves).
