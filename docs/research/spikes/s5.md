# S5 Terrain adaptation

## Question

Do vanilla `terrain_adaptation` modes (`beard_thin`, `beard_box`, `bury`, `encapsulate`) suffice for a generated city on hills and rivers, or do we grade terrain ourselves (spike S7)? Includes the "adapt buildings first" tactics from the synthesis: reject steep lots, stepped foundations. Large buildings and towers on slopes matter most.

## Result

**Beards suffice for building-scale adaptation. S7 (density-function flattening) is not needed for Phase 3-4; defer it** until a design needs a level area larger than the 12-block beard margin (plazas, a whole terraced city cell, level streets). Use `beard_thin`, one rigid piece per building with the box bottom at floor level, plus planner-side foundations and stepped floors for big buildings.

| Mode / tactic | Verdict | Why (steep site, gradient 0.72, 9 buildings) |
|---|---|---|
| none | not enough | houses overhang or are wedged into the slope (mean void 17% of footprint per house, tower 8%); tower base hangs up to 15 blocks above ground on its downhill side |
| `beard_thin` | **use** | zero void under any floor; each building gets a flat shelf; houses blend in (mean wall exposure 0.94 vs 1.0 ideal, no wall buried); cut faces of the uphill side stay 5-12 blocks high next to the big hall |
| `beard_box` | usable, worse look | same numbers, but carves the whole zone beside and above the box: hollow pits and overhangs around the hall (`steep-box-v0.jpg`) |
| `bury` | **never for surface buildings** | sphere of solid earth covers the houses (mean 9.9 blocks of wall buried, max 13); the town is invisible (`steep-bury-v0.jpg`) |
| `encapsulate` | **never for surface buildings** | same, worse: 15.7 blocks buried on average, max 25 |
| stepped foundation (no beard) | works, looks like stilts | foundation columns remove all voids; the hall's three bays step down the slope; but uphill walls stay buried (up to 6) and nothing grades the ground |
| `beard_thin` + foundation + stepped hall | **best look** | `steep-thin_found-v0/v1.jpg`: shelves, no gaps, hall follows the slope in 3 steps |
| reject steep lots (range > 4) | works for houses, **wrong for large buildings** | only 3 of 9 pieces survive on the steep site, and the hall (the point of the city) and 4 houses are rejected; the 7×7 tower survives (verifier correction, per metrics.csv); use only for optional small fill buildings |

On flat ground nothing is needed: `none` already has no void. At the hill site (gradient 0.44) beards are also clean. At the lake/river-shore site, `none` leaves the tower base in the water (void 4%), `beard_thin` fills it (void 0), but the fill is only a small patch (the ground plane is the floor level, kernel radius 12), so a building standing in open water still stands in water unless the planner adds foundations or picks dry lots.

### When beard is enough, when not

- Enough: any building whose lot slopes up to ~0.7 blocks per block over its footprint (tested: 25x15 hall, 7x7x30 tower, 7x7x6 houses). The shelf is cut (uphill) and filled (downhill) within 12 blocks of each box.
- Not enough:
  - Level area larger than a building: each piece has its own ground plane, so streets and plazas between buildings follow the natural slope and are not graded. Roads could be thin rigid pieces (a piece per segment) but that multiplies the piece count (cost is O(pieces within 12 blocks) per density sample).
  - Cuts deeper than the surface-rule band: faces cut far below `preliminary_surface_level` show bare stone (seen as grey faces next to the uphill walls). Known gotcha (NOTES, 5.x deep-dive); fixing it needs the S7 wrapping.
  - Big water bodies: beard fills only a 12-block margin around each box.
  - Anything that must predict the final ground: `getBaseHeight` ignores the beardifier, so the plan must use its own floor heights (the spike did, from `OCEAN_FLOOR_WG`), not re-probe.

Seed `8` (first of a seed list that yields all four site kinds; sites at chunks (-23,-12), (-35,-12), (-36,48), (42,19)).

It passed (`BUILD SUCCESSFUL`, ~8.5 min test, loadavg ~8-10). An earlier run failed on an over-tight threshold ("bare boxes float on a hill"), so the check can fail; no further mutation run.

## What it means for the design

- Phase 3/4 buildings emit one rigid piece per building (or per wing, for stepped large buildings) with the box bottom at the floor; the structure sets `terrain_adaptation: beard_thin`. This matches ADR 0002's piece-based start.
- The planner chooses floor heights from its own height probe and, for large buildings, steps wings (<= 2 blocks between neighbours worked) and adds foundation columns under the box; foundations are our blocks, written in `postProcess` below the box, not part of the box (so they do not move the beard plane).
- Do not offer `bury`/`encapsulate` for surface styles.
- Large buildings on slopes still produce high cut faces on the uphill side (5-12 blocks next to the hall); the planner should pick lot orientation or accept retaining walls there (a REVIEW question for John).
- S7 stays a fallback. Trigger: Phase 5 needs level streets or plazas across several buildings, or bare-stone cut faces are judged ugly.

## How to re-implement the test properly

Keep the probe-then-generate shape: a probe world picks sites by `getBaseHeight` stats, then per-variant worlds with the same seed. Make `findGenerationPoint` return empty unless the chunk is a registered site (static map set before the world is created) and the variant is active. Needs a normal-world client test (`setUseConsistentSettings(false)`). Pre-generate chunks around each camera server-side, then poll `levelRenderer.hasRenderedAllSections()` (the stock `waitForChunksRender(true, n)` timed out here at render distance 7). Replace the mean-exposure metric with explicit assertions per building class.

## Caveats

- One seed, one biome mix (forest/taiga hillside, snow on the hill site); tree cover hides some terrain in the pictures.
- The "river" site is a lake/ocean shore with dark forest, not a narrow river; no seed in the first 8 gave a clean narrow river with a dry flat lot. Narrow rivers should behave like the shore case (beard fills a 12-block margin), untested.
- Buildings are plain boxes: no doors, no interior, so reachability and entrances on slopes are not covered.
- Timings (heavy lock, loadavg 8-10, lavapipe): probe about 12 s per seed, one variant world with 4 sites and 8 screenshots about 40 s.
