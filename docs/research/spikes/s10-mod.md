# S10 mod half: roads as a per-chunk worldgen feature

Spike, 2026-10-22, branch `claude/spike-s10-mod` (code never merged; commit `S10 mod: ...` on the branch is the reference). Spec: [intercity-roads.md](../../plans/intercity-roads.md), "The spike (S10)" half 2. Planner half: `s10-intercity-roads.md`.

# Question

Can a per-chunk feature draw a 3-wide road from a polyline with cut and fill, byte-identical in any chunk order, at under 5 ms per road chunk on the serial lane?

# Answer

**Determinism: yes. Cost: yes with the sampled surface level (about 0.9 ms cold per road chunk), no with exact `getBaseHeight` probes if every chunk starts cold (about 7 ms), but fine with the memo warm (under 1 ms). Looks: it reads as a road, with two things to fix (sheer cut walls beyond the 4-block shoulder, one-block steps).**

# What was built (throwaway)

- `RoadFeature` (`mod/.../spike10/`): a 26.3 `Feature` record-style type (see below), registered in `FEATURE_TYPE`, `worldgen/feature/s10_road.json` + `placed_feature/s10_road.json` (empty `placement`), added to all overworld biomes with `BiomeModifications.addFeature(foundInOverworld, TOP_LAYER_MODIFICATION, key)`.
- Fixture: 6-point polyline, 5 segments, about 770 blocks, from (-96,-64) to (600,288) on seed 10101. It crosses a lake, a hill (raw heights 63 to 126) and flat land.
- Profile: stations every 8 blocks along the line (global, from arc length); station height = `getBaseHeight(WORLD_SURFACE_WG)` (or coarse, below), floored at sea level; deck = mean of 17 stations (window of +-8, ends clamped by global index) interpolated linearly along the arc. No grade limit, no Catmull-Rom (a straight-chord polyline only).
- Per column: nearest point over the segments whose reach box meets the chunk; distance <= 1.5 is road (gravel at deck), up to 4 more blocks is shoulder (grass, surface = terrain clamped to deck +- edge distance, 1:1). Fill: dirt from the ground up. Cut: top block replaced, everything above cleared up to max(ground, deck+4).
- "Ground" is found by scanning down from the `WORLD_SURFACE_WG` heightmap past air, replaceables, fluids, leaves and logs. Each chunk writes only its own columns.
- Test: `S10ClientGameTest` (client game test; the server game-test world is flat, seed 0, so a noise world needs a client test, see NOTES S3). Four fresh worlds with seed 10101, generating the 212 corridor chunks (108 are road chunks) in order asc, desc, shuffled, shuffled with the station memo cleared before every chunk. Run with `S10=1 [S10_PROBE=coarse|exact] [S10_MUTANT=ground] scripts/client-test.sh`; results in `mod/build/run/clientGameTest/s10-results.txt`.

# Evidence

## Determinism (D7 style)

The digest is SHA-256 over the sorted ledger of what the feature owns: every (pos, block) it places, plus the air volume it claims (recorded whether or not something was there to remove).

| run | asc | desc | shuf1 | shuf2 (memo cold per chunk) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact probes | d47a93123b690ede | same | same | same (155,030 entries) |
| coarse probes | cd8e875ad64ed2ae | same | same | same (155,623 entries) |

Repeated across runs (three runs gave the same exact digest). Read-back after generation: all 2,615 gravel blocks are still gravel in every world.

**Finding 1 (a real bug the test caught in its first form):** the first ledger recorded only effective writes, and one air-clearing write in 3 of 4 orders differed by 1 block. Cause: a neighbour chunk's tree leaves overhang into our chunk either before or after our feature runs, so "was there something to clear" depends on order. The fix is the D7 rule: claim the air volume (a pure function of ground and deck) and digest the claim, not the effective write. Foreign leaves in the road's air volume stay "not ours". The ground height must ignore leaves and logs (done by scanning) and must not come from a heightmap that features update (`MOTION_BLOCKING` does).

**Mutant (control):** ground read from the `MOTION_BLOCKING` heightmap with leaves counted: the four digests all differ (108,685 / 108,786 / 108,925 / 108,946 entries). `S10_MUTANT=ground`.

`WORLD_SURFACE_WG` was stable under neighbour trees in all runs here (a mutant that only changed the ground scan did not fail), so it is the heightmap to use, but that is a fact about this version, worth a test in the real implementation.

## Cost per road chunk

Feature body only (`System.nanoTime` around plan and write), 108 road chunks, worlds generated by vanilla's worker threads in parallel with the feature, under the heavy lock but with **other agents running: loadavg 10 to 20** (`results-*.txt` carry the loadavg at the start). So numbers are noisy and probably inflated. Not a quiet-machine measurement.

| mode | plan (stations + column classification), mean / p90 / max ms | write, mean / p90 / max ms | total mean / max ms | height probes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact, memo warm (shared, asc/desc/shuf1) | 0.4 to 0.5 / 1.0 / 8.8 | 0.27 to 0.38 / 1.0 / 3.5 | 0.7 to 0.9 / 9.2 | 108 in total |
| exact, memo cold per chunk | **6.9 / 8.3 / 11.1** | 0.29 / 0.8 / 3.0 | **7.2 / 11.9** | 2,015 (18.7 per chunk, about 0.37 ms each) |
| coarse (chunk_surface_level + 16), memo warm | 0.06 to 0.14 / 0.13 to 0.25 / 1.1 to 4.0 | 0.28 to 0.49 / 1.1 / 7.6 | 0.33 to 0.63 / 11.6 | 108 |
| coarse, memo cold per chunk | **0.59 / 0.85 / 1.2** | 0.29 / 0.65 / 3.0 | **0.88 / 1.4 (p90) / 3.5 max** | 2,015 (about 0.03 ms each) |

- The cost is the probes: 17 stations per chunk because of the +-8 window, so a cold chunk reads 17 to 19 columns. Writes are 0.3 ms mean for about 1,400 blocks written per road chunk.
- In practice the memo is shared by neighbouring chunks of one road (108 probes for the whole road instead of 2,015), and one road has about 100 stations per 800 blocks, so the amortised cost is the warm row. The cold row is the worst case after an eviction or on a lane that must not share state.
- Pass criterion (under 5 ms per road chunk with the sampled surface level): met, 0.9 ms cold, p90 1.4, max 3.5 (one write max of 7.6 ms in the warm run is likely GC or contention).
- The coarse probe reads about 2 to 4 blocks off the exact one at stations, hidden by the 17-station mean; digests differ between probes, as they should.

## Looks

`docs/research/spikes/s10/s10-road-{0,1,2}.png` (client screenshots, spectator camera 28 blocks behind the point, 16 above the ground, exact probes).

- **road-0 (x 97, z 2):** the road runs as a thin causeway between two hills across a narrow lake; the grey gravel with a one-block grass shoulder is clearly a road line, and the heights step along the profile.
- **road-1 (x 280, z 8):** a straight embankment across a wide lake to the far shore: the fill reads like a causeway or dam, narrow (3 gravel plus about 4 shoulder each side, shoulder slope 1:1, the waterline cuts it).
- **road-2 (x 455, z 196):** the road climbs a hill and is **cut into it as a trench**: because the shoulder is only 4 blocks wide at 1:1, a hillside steeper than 1:1 leaves **sheer 5 to 15 block walls** of dirt and stone beside the gravel. Real, visible, and the main thing to fix.
- The deck steps by whole blocks (the staircase pattern along the gravel). It is a believable cobbled path on gentle slopes but wants stairs or slabs for steady grades.

# Design implications

1. **A per-chunk feature is viable for roads.** It needs no reach limit, no structure start, and the per-chunk read is the global profile plus the chunk's own terrain. Keep the two-phase shape: a pure plan query (columns, deck) and a writer; the planner half supplies the polyline and, ideally, the smoothed deck at stations so the mod does not probe at all (then cost is the 0.3 ms write).
2. **Own the air volume by claim.** The ledger and the D7 digest must record the cleared volume as claimed, and everything above it is "not ours". Leaves from neighbours' later decoration can still overhang or intrude (accepted, as D5).
3. **Ground = scan past leaves/logs/replaceables/fluids from `WORLD_SURFACE_WG`.** Never `MOTION_BLOCKING`. Add the mutant to the permanent test (T2).
4. **Cut needs a real slope.** The shoulder must widen with the terrain (slope 1:1 up to the deck, then blend to the natural height over more blocks, or terrace), or the road should be routed along contours with a grade limit (planner side). At least widen to the cut depth, capped.
5. **Station probes are the budget.** Use the coarse probe or the planner's heights; use a window of +-8 only if the probes are cached. A memo keyed by station index is pure and may be evicted without changing a result (checked with the cold-per-chunk world).
6. **26.3 API:** `Feature` is an interface (`codec()` and `place(WorldGenLevel, ChunkGenerator, RandomSource, BlockPos)`), registered as a `MapCodec` in `BuiltInRegistries.FEATURE_TYPE`; there are no configured features. A `Feature` JSON is `worldgen/feature/<id>.json` with `{"type": "ns:id"}`, and `PlacedFeature` refers to it by id. `ChunkPos.containing(BlockPos)`. With empty `placement`, the origin is the chunk's min corner, once per chunk per biome-holding generation. `BiomeModifications.addFeature` works with `foundInOverworld()` at `TOP_LAYER_MODIFICATION`.
7. **Not tested here:** vanilla villages on the route, bridges over deep water (the fixture's lake crossing is a dirt causeway), a Lithostitched or Terralith matrix, an evicted-chunk regeneration (a chunk regenerated after unload), saving and reloading. Water columns get a causeway.

# Re-implementing it properly

A permanent test (T2) should: build the road from planner output, generate the road chunks in at least four orders in fresh worlds, compare the ledger digest (claim semantics), read back the deck from the world, and keep the leaf-ground mutant. Put timings in a log line, not a hard assert, because the lane numbers depend on load.
