# 7.17 SHOPLOW S3: lot planner (front rise, embankment lots, SHOP_GRADE loosened)

Branch `claude/p7-shoplow-s3`. Files: `settlement/Briefs.java` (`fillLot`, `BANK_LIMIT`, `SHOP_FRONT_RISE`, `frontType`, `shopRiseTooHigh`), `gen/v1/types/shop/ShopType.java` (`SHOP_GRADE`), new test `BankLotTest`.

## What changed
- `empty:bank`: a house, workshop or shop lot whose street surface (`frontY`) is more than `BANK_LIMIT` (4) from the raw ground of the column in front of the lot (the column `frontY` looks out from) stays empty. Checked before and after a re-pick. Reading "front-edge middle column" as the column outside the lot, not the lot's own front row: the street column is where an embankment or cutting shows.
- Front-rise limit: a shop or workshop (architect A2: workshops are held to the shop limit) whose lot's highest ground is more than `SHOP_FRONT_RISE` (6) above `frontY` (or above the front ground when no street is known) is re-picked with its own type excluded (`ClassTable.pick` exclusion overload); no other type fits: `empty:no-fit` (`empty:steep` above relief 8).
- John's J1 (2026-10-26): `SHOP_GRADE` 12 -> 4 (relief allowed `r * 4 <= w + d + 4`: 6 on a 9x14 lot, 8 on 12x24; 12 allowed about 2-4). 4 keeps `railRoomOk` off for shops as before (it only applies at grade <= 3). It is what S2's cut of at most 4 plus a rise of 2 can serve (M - G <= 6).
- Not S3: the A7 forecourt causeway is a site-pass thing (S2).
