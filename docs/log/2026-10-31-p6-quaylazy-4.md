## Did
- `QuayTrace.near()`: one seed per group of segments joined inside the scanned box (`Quays.distinctSeeds`, union-find in scan order), then one fanout piece per group walks the chain and finds its cluster; the cluster ids are collected into a sorted set; then the distinct clusters are graded on the fanout, **each cluster wholly on one thread** (so the per-thread `gradingBy` re-entry guard stays valid: a grade never calls the trace, and a thread that needs a cluster another thread is grading just waits on its FutureTask). Results land in per-index slots and are merged by id order, then sorted by order key: nothing depends on which worker finishes first.
- `QuayTrace.all()`: contour and chains stay serial (row prefetch), clusters grade on the fanout.
- `Bank` gets `fork()` and `fanout()` (defaults: itself, serial). A private `Forks` gives the caller its own bank and each other worker thread one fork per request. `Districts` bank: `fork()` = `new Build(src, ix, iz, exact, false).bank(area, false)` (own memos), `fanout()` = `src.reads().fanout()` (the PARREADS pool, caller-runs; no new pool).
- Walk prefetch: the node memo prefetches a box of `WALK_AHEAD = 32` blocks round a node that misses the memo outside the box prefetched so far (initially the tile's seed-scan box). A hint only.
