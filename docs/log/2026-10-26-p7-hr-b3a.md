# P7 HR-B3a: interior wiring, uses, shaft, lights

Branch `claude/p7-hr-b3a`, plan [plans/highrises.md](../plans/highrises.md) step B3a (7.13).

## What was done

- `interior/FloorPlanner` + `BuildingType.floors()` (default null): `InteriorPass.draft` asks the type's planner first and uses its `Planned(plan, uses)`; `newBand` splits bands on a floor-kind change. `InteriorPass.windowCells` gives the facade's window cells (full widths) and entrances.
- `types/highrise/TowerFloors`: per storey kind from `TowerProgram.read` (LOBBY and OPEN grand, BANQUET, TYPICAL corridor, SUITES corridor of flats, STAIRHOUSE), block and hall converted to plate cells of the mass, window cells as `reserved`, up to 6 draws per plate (draw key (mass, template, attempt), never the storey, so floors before and after a special floor are the same plan), then the guillotine planner. Unit use per building 60 % flat / 40 % office.
- `HighriseRooms`: uses for the generic fallback; hooks per use (light first, no workstation, flat hearth is the furnace, `ceiling_light` for big rooms and every room but the stairwell).
- `InteriorPass.hookSites`: `ceiling_light` rule: grid pitch 10 half a pitch from the walls (a line every 8 down a room with a short side of 3 or less); `HookPlacer.ceilingLight` hangs a lantern on `iron_chain` (length clear - 3, so it hangs at y + 2).
- `TowerShaft.emit` after the interior pass: air column over the shaft from the pit floor through every slab to under the machine room, a 1x2 `window`-role doorway per storey at the shaft's middle in the hall-side wall, a `light` on the pit floor, a stairwell `ceiling_light` at the landing door cell on every storey (and the `sd == 4` guard rail, unused with `SD = 3`).
- `TowerPiers` (after `FacadePass.run`): window bays that would meet the end of a block wall on the facade become blank; the stair house gets no windows. This is what makes the plates fit (see numbers).
- `TowerPlate` (B2's file) changes the integration needed: stair house gets one door between stairwell and machine room (the room must be reachable until B3b's roof doors); back rooms behind the block with no unit beside the block hang off a store room (`closetBeside`) and are storage; office towers: back rooms are storage and every office room of a unit has its own hall door (`hall-access`); `FloorPlanText` draws `ceiling_light` as a light.

## Decisions and surprises

- Piers rather than steering the block: the plates are drawn before the windows exist, so the facade is edited after the fact (`TowerPiers.apply`); B3b's own facade run must call it too.
- `Info.reserved` holds the core ring too, so `TowerFloors` computes its own window-only `reserved`.
- The B0 checks and B2's design disagreed for offices (a back room "office" reached through another office); made the back room storage and gave every office its own hall door.
- Not done: suites rule (">= 60 cells, leftovers merge"): `SUITES` floors are flats with target 14-18, no merge pass.
- Assumptions: five rows in ASSUMPTIONS (unit use 60/40, piers, office back rooms, interim door, ceiling light).

## Round 2 (design review of B3a, architect's deep-flats decision)

- **Wrap-around units (DS-32):** a side strip beside the core block too narrow for a unit is no longer a store room. With no unit on the other side, the strip and the back room are one unit: flats: the strip is the private `entry` (a passage), the back room the `flat` (door through the block's end column); offices: the strip is one office or two stacked (each with a window, cut row clear of side windows, second entered from the first), the back room an office of the same tenancy. If the facade gives the strip no window pair, the old closets remain. `HighriseChecks.hall-access` accepts a unit room reached through a room of its own use that has a hall door.
- **Closets:** after demotions `TowerPlate.refineStores` makes every store a closet (<= 36 cells, <= 2:1): thin or big leftovers are cut along the long side into pieces chained by doors (doors on a new cut slide one cell); a windowed non-thin leftover with a door to a room of the floor's kind takes its use (office/office, bedroom/flat, open/hall; 24+ cells on lobby, open and banquet floors); a banquet kitchen under 35 cells is swapped with the biggest leftover.
- **Studios:** `TowerPlate.Result.studio` marks a flat with no bedroom door; `FloorPlanner.Planned.needsBed` makes `hookSites` add a `bed` right after the light.
- **Deep flats (architect):** `stripDepth` 0 up to 10 deep, 3 from 11; the entry is only in front of the flat (6 wide at most, a closet past it), a bedroom's front part is its wardrobe (door from the bedroom), bedrooms open off the flat; offices no strip.
- Verifier items: `HookPlacer` javadoc and `CeilingLight` (pure geometry, `mod` `CeilingLightTest`: chain `clear - 3`, lantern at y + 2 from 3 clear, y + 1 with 2 clear, in the column); `DEBUG` println and `GEOM` removed; `HighriseProgrammeTest.noWindowMeetsTheEndOfAPartition`.
- Measures (`HighriseStatsTest`, 8 seeds per lot, same counting before and after): see the report.

## Round 3

- Wrap entry (flats): only the 3-deep part by the hall door, 6 wide at most, is the `entry`; the rest of the strip beside the back room is one or two `flat` rooms (windowed, 2:1) and the back room joins them. Spare cells of a strip wider than 6 go to the block side. A strip with no window pair for those rooms stays closets.
- Closets: `split` plans door access first: every piece opens from a non-store room (an existing door, or a new door on a shared wall), else from a neighbouring piece that does; a store already behind a piece that needs a neighbour would be 2 deep: then the split is rolled back and the leftover stays one bigger store.
