## Done
- **Solo sites**: a settled cell that decides alone now has a one-cell `SettlementSite` (follow-up from the SK verifier); `city --cell 0,0` on the 987654321 world dump plans a hamlet, `0,1` a village.
- **Windowed probes**: `FieldRegionView.decidable/covers`; `city --cell` on a fine dump (gate-joined-city) exits 2 with "window ... use --site"; `city-check --sites cells` skips cells the window cannot decide.
- **Bounded caches**: `BoundedCache` (LRU, thread-safe, compute outside the lock). `FieldRegionView` memos and `SkeletonSource.Direct` skeletons use it; `Arterials` memo too (was an unsynchronised TreeMap shared by threads). A touch in `Arterials.java` (one field, one method line) in HUB/ROADS' area: trivial to merge.
- **No double planning**: tiles belong to the settlement owning their centre cell; test over 3 seeds, 11 x 11 cells, all sites incl. solo: every tile has at most one planner.
