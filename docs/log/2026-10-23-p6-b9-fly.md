# P6 B9: fly-through screenshots (6.8)

`mod/src/gametest/.../flythrough/FlyThroughClientGameTest.java`, `scripts/flythrough.sh`. Opt-in client test: `FB_FLY=only`
(the script sets it) runs it alone; without `FB_FLY` it returns at once, so `test-all.sh` is unchanged. Settings by environment
(`--seeds`, `--out`, `--rd`, `--cells`): defaults seeds 987654321,12345,1, render distance 10, search 6 region cells, output
`mod/build/flythrough/` (960-wide JPEGs plus `manifest.tsv`; full PNGs in `mod/build/screenshots/fly-*.png`).

## What it does
Per seed one world. The world's planner scans the region cells around the origin; per tier the nearest settlement of that exact tier.
Six frames each: approach (back toward the origin, at most render distance minus a chunk), overview (south of the centre, from above),
street (end of the longest road/paving box near the centre, eye height, camera y from the box itself), interior (1.5 blocks inside the
door nearest the tile centre whose far side has a ceiling), slope (steepest planned tile, seen from its low side), edge (outside the
radius looking in). Camera heights: exact terrain probe along the line plus a margin, capped at y 175 (the cloud layer at about 192 turns
a frame white). HUD hidden (`client.gui.hud.toggle()`). Chunks around the camera, mid-point and target are generated server-side by the
player's own tickets, the test waits until the client has those three chunks and `hasRenderedAllSections()`.

  caught real defects of my cameras: far overviews above the cloud layer (sky 1.0), cameras beyond render distance (target not loaded),
  and the renderer's "all sections rendered" being true before a far teleport's chunks had arrived (fixed by waiting for the chunks).
  A snow field legitimately fills 60 % with one colour, so the threshold is 80 %.

  show stairs, windows, ceilings, a crafting table.

  Waiting for the heavy lock took up to 90 minutes some days: a run is 25 minutes.
