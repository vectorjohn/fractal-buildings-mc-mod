## Done

- `mod/.../worldgen/FoliageClear` (new): after a clear, reads the 3 x 3 chunks round the chunk (13 blocks, chunk sections, not heightmaps) and removes foliage vanilla would call detached, up to 6 blocks outside the chunk. Rules: non-persistent leaf needs a supported log within 6 leaf steps; a plain `*_log` (not stripped, not wood) of a cluster that touches natural leaves needs a chain of logs to a block that is not foliage; vines and cocoa need a support; snow layer goes with its leaf. Called from `RoadFeature.draw` (after the clear) and `TilePiece.postProcess` (ground pieces, after the raster). Switch `FoliageClear.ENABLED` (tests).
