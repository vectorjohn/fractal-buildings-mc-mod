# ROADS: whole-city road defects (roads-connected, road-grade, tile-border-agree, landmarks)

Branch `claude/p5-roads` (lead-coder), base `claude/run-phase5` (HUB, D2, TR, SC merged in). Goal: empty `CityCheckTest.KNOWN`.

## Causes found (scratch diagnosis tool: per-piece street lists, skeleton-road coverage gaps, ASCII maps)

- **Hub cut (A)**: hall and temple lots were placed before the streets, flush against the square and often on a main road's axis, so `addPieces` clipped the road there. A square the skeleton clamped into its tile away from the hub column touched no road at all; a road leaving a square at 45 degrees ended on a footprint that touched the square only at a corner (not 4-connected).
- **Arterials (B)** and **main roads of cells whose own routes failed** (metropolis-temperate cell 3,-6): `markLotEligible` trusted any skeleton-kind street as a root, but those pieces never reach the hub.
- **Per-tile eligibility (C)**: same root cause as B; the tile cannot know.
- **Gap tiles (D)**: a tile whose centre lies in a cell without a settlement was planned by nobody, so the main road through it had a hole.
- **Village green (E)**: green moved off the street, hall on a detached piece.
- **road-grade (2)**: not a tile-border effect: `Grading` fixed bridge decks at surface + 3, so two small water spans a few blocks apart at different heights forced a 2-3 block step (temperate d8_m60, border a049_m7680). Separately, each tile graded its own clipped piece of a road, so the two tiles at a border disagreed on road height on 4-6 % of shared columns, up to 14 blocks (measured over the 9 gate windows).
- **tile-border-agree (3)**: a hall lot reaching the tile edge clipped a quay in one tile only (s7_5 town); also the 4-cell metropolis case.
- **Landmarks (4)**: hall/temple only placed flush against the square; plan view drew SK's spots. A latent bug: skeleton landmark ids are all `l?` before assembly, so `landmarks.removeIf(id)` would have removed the square too.
- **lot-frontage (5)**: no longer reproduced once KNOWN was empty (D2's wall lots are exempt by D2's own rule); dropped.

## What changed

- `RoadNetwork` (new, memoised in `SkeletonSource.Direct.network()`): every member cell's main roads/paths and every arterial segment, kept to the columns some planned tile draws, flooded 8-connected from where the centre cell's roads start (`roadHub`). `Districts.markLotEligible` now unions streets and plazas and lets a piece carry lots only if it holds a live column.
- Tile claims: `Districts.owns(view, site, grid, hubTile, ix, iz)`; a tile whose centre cell has no settlement goes to the settlement owning most of its 32-lattice columns. `Skeletons.districts` lists such tiles and, per main road, every owned tile from the hub to the last urban tile along it (road-only tiles: outskirts, density 0, streets but no lots).
- Hall/temple: placed after streets, plaza lanes (`hallLots` always after `plazaLanes`), off the tile border band and off kept landmarks; a hall lot whose square reaches no live road is taken back; `landmarkLots` gives an unplaced hall (first) or temple its own lot on the nearest reachable street of the tile; `landmarkFallback` converts the nearest fitting lot as a last resort. Squares that touch no skeleton road are joined like moved ones; `stubLane` joins a square sitting just beside a road (border arterials the lattice lanes cannot reach); moved squares prefer spots beside a road (`roadSideFirst`); a cut road ends with an axis step so it shares an edge with the square (`joinSquare`).
- `CityPlanner`: hub = square centre only if the square's piece reaches a live road, else the road start; the settlement's landmarks are moved to the plaza and hall/temple lots (`placedLandmarks`, `SettlementPlan.withLandmarks`), so the plan view outlines the real square.
- Road heights: `Grading` decks are lower bounds (lower-bound envelope, then clamp); `Grading.road(points, ys, width, exact)` builds from given heights. `Districts.pieces` grades each whole street (skeleton road, arterial/border segment, grid run followed 256 blocks beyond the tile, quay run) once and slices it; streets store vertices plus both columns of every height step, so scenes, `TerrainStats` and `Briefs.frontY` reproduce the exact profile.
