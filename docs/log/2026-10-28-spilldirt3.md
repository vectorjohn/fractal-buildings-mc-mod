# SPILLDIRT3: the shuffle2 concrete->dirt flake, found in the sim

Order test sightings after SPILLDIRT (6c78fd77): `light_gray_concrete->dirt [unknown]` at s1_m10 1287/1288,65,-9504 (shuffle2) in groundwalk 429676cb, bridgeends r1 0e09c461, PERFSRV r9 c6dcad54.

## Diagnosis
- Not a write of ours; vanilla dirt the classifier missed. The failing cell has our street surface concrete directly above it (`above light_gray_concrete air` in the log), so it is the LOWER block of a two-block-thick road (sloped streets: 62 of 3662 road cells in a 9 x 9 chunk window have road above them). 6c78fd77's `trunkBaseBeside` needs a log in the 8 neighbouring columns at y+1..2, and none is left: the tree's logs at the base level are blocked by the surface concrete, and the ones higher up are removed by our later chunk's steps.
- Reproduced with the ORDERSIM harness (fixture gate-temperate-city, scripted `dark_oak` at 1205,72,-9601, 14 orders): `light_gray_concrete->dirt [unknown]` at 1206,71,-9600 in reverse, shuffle-3, -5, -8, -11; last writer `feature:dark_oak@1205,72,-9601`; forward and 6 other orders clean (our write covers the dirt). It did not show at 1287,-9504 itself: the fixture is a step-8 dump, so the sim's street there is one block thick. A search of 83 tree spots over the doubled-road columns gave 5 failing runs, all this spot.
- Our contract checks (C5) passed in the repro: no write of ours is involved.

## Fix
`SpillKinds.solidKind`: a road cell (`roadCell`, the raster's road role) holding a dirt-family block with a solid, non-ground, non-leaf, non-log, non-fluid block above it is `spill` (`surfaceOverBase`). Narrow: needs a road cell and a surface of ours over it; an open-air dirt cell, a non-road cell or a non-dirt block stays unknown.

## Tests

Mutation: with the rule off both tests fail (`expected: <spill> but was: <unknown>`, and the 5 unknown cells).
