# P5 T1: terrain dump tool and first dumps

Branch `claude/p5-t1-dumps`. Re-implemented S4's sampling (not merged from the spike).

- `mod/src/test/.../TerrainDumpTool.java`: plain-JUnit bootstrap of vanilla worldgen
  (`Bootstrap.bootStrap`, `VanillaRegistries.createWorldLookup`, `NoiseBasedChunkGenerator`
  with the overworld preset), surface and floor from `getBaseColumn`, biome at the surface quart
  after a priming lookup; writes `fb-region-dump/1` with empty `layers`, gzipped (byte-stable).
  Driven by `TerrainDumpToolTest.dump` (`-Dfb.dump=seed:x0:z0:step:nx:nz:name,...`).
- `RecordedProbe.read` detects gzip by magic (so `recorded:` specs take `.json.gz` too).
- `mod/build.gradle` (build change): JUnit test deps, `useJUnitPlatform`, 2g heap, `fb.*` forwarding.
- Dumps (0.6 MB total, README lists them): `world-12345-s64`, `world-987654321-s64`
  (-12288 origin, 384 x 384 at 64), `w12345-river-s8` (256 x 256 at 8 from (-1408, -4160)),
  `w1-mixed-s8` (from (7936, -4672), around S4's seed-1 city at (8960, -3648); savanna,
  temperate, jungle). Fine windows are 2048 blocks square; the plan allowed up to 3072, and
  `-Dfb.dump` makes larger ones cheap if R2 or T2 need them.

Verification: `:mod:test` 5 tests (4 plus the skipped-without-property dump): 16x16 window
identical JSON and gzip bytes on 1 and 4 threads; the 32x32 crop of seed 12345 reproduces
`s4-12345-crop32.json` exactly (surface, water, biome; river 63/20 at (576, -4160)); seeds
differ. `RecordedProbeTest.gzipEqualsPlainTwin` (gzip under a non-`.gz` name equals the plain
file). Looked at: height/water renderings of all four dumps (throwaway node script): coarse maps
show ocean and land blobs with peak patches; the river window shows rivers, a bay and the sea
to the south; the mixed window shows branching rivers, a lake and hills. Mutation: not run
(sampling equality against an independent recording is the control).

The 64-step crop matches S4's dump, which confirms the planner-side lattice origin of -12288.
