# 2026-10-31 p6-mainstep (6.33 MAINSTEP)

**Change.** `InstancePassV1.widenMainStep` (called at the end of `steps`, before rails and headroom): a front entrance (not `secondary`, faces north in the shell frame, on the entrance floor) whose front cell(s) already hold a tread gets the step widened by one cell each side along the facade. Both side cells or none (centred, never one-sided). A side cell needs: inside the lot, outside the footprint, not claimed by another flight/landing/door front (`blocked`, `flightTop`), 4 blocks of air above the tread (DS-54 / DOORAIR: wall, eave or porch post stops it), ground at most one block under the tread and no drop over the lot line that would need a rail. The new cells are foundation + `STAIR` site boxes and `treads` entries, so `rails` and `headroom` treat them like any step. Otherwise the door keeps its door-wide step. Switch `InstancePassV1.WIDE_MAIN_STEP` / `-Dfb.nomainstep=true` gives the old output (gallery "before"; a test control).

**Tests.** `MainStepTest` (new): flat:70 and two slopes x house/shop/workshop x 4 styles x 4 fronts: never one-sided, side step never in the footprint, 4 blocks of air above it, at least 40% of front steps widened on flat land, side doors never get 3 wide. Mutation: requiring 3 side cells makes the 40% check fail. Updated for the new meaning: `ShopStoopTest` (a front step at rise 1 is up to 3 cells, a stoop is for rise 2), `StairGroundTest` (cut-short control builds with the switch off: a side cell beside the flight can be a way off the terrain; its no-steps control picks a flight spanning 2+ levels). Goldens regenerated after merging STAIRJOG (45a96b8c).

**Look.** `FB_PRESETS=house-medieval` gallery before/after in `docs/review/mainstep/`: 8 of 16 houses show a wider step; the others keep one (porch, lot edge, flight beside the door).

**Round 2 (architect and verifier).** Corrected r1 claim: seeds 8 and 12 were not widened; r1 widened 6 of 16 medieval houses (1, 2, 4, 9, 10, 13), held back by the house's own eave (a side cell needed 4 clear, the centre step has 3). Now a side cell needs the clear height of the centre step (at most 4, at least 2), a cell under an upper floor (a footprint column) is judged by that air test, and a side cell must stand on the ground itself with no neighbour a block lower, so no rail ever fences an outer cell (r1: 48 of 130 widened cases got rails on slopes; the narrower step there is the fallback, a ground-level step needs no fence).
