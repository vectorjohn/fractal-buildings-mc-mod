# ROADS2: no detached roads, a street lattice that tapers the city (John, Phase 5 gate)

Branch `claude/p5-roads2` (coder), base `claude/run-phase5`. John (gate review of "Whole-city roads"): arterials detached from the
network cannot be allowed; add smaller roads off the arterials so the built-up area tapers (lower density at the edge, suburbs) and does not
stop dead past the arterials or the highrises.

## Cause

Streets were decided pointwise and per tile: an arterial is picked by a hash of its segment, a plain border street by the layout at its
midpoint, grid lines and ports by hashes, and a piece "touching the tile border" was kept because its neighbour might continue it.
Nothing knew whether a chain of them reached a main road, so on the 9 gate windows 12 to 88 street pieces per city (2 to 45 after the lattice)
were detached: arterial chains with no main-road link (snow city), grid-line meshes, 57-column port stubs, quay runs, lone squares and the
main roads of cells whose own routes failed. ROADS only kept lots off them; they were still drawn.

## What changed

- **Street lattice** (`RoadNetwork`, memoised per settlement like before, built from skeletons and coarse terrain only). In a town, city or
  metropolis (`RoadNetwork.latticeTier`: TOWN and up) every tile-border segment is *eligible* when it is an arterial candidate (`Arterials.is`) or
  has 2 of its 3 sample points (ends and middle) urban, crosses no water run over 40 blocks, is no steeper than 1 in 4 (`STREET_STEEP_PER_32` = 8,
  arterials keep their 3 in 4) and does not hit the wall ring's reserved rectangles. It is *present* when a chain of eligible segments meeting at
  tile corners reaches a live road: a main road or path that reaches the hub, or a present segment (closure, iterated with the hub flood until
  nothing new attaches). A present segment is an arterial where the rule picked one, else a 5-wide `b...` street drawn whole (no exact edge
  test, so both tiles and the network see the same street). So an arterial chain with no main-road link is linked by plain streets along the
  lattice, or, where the lattice offers no way, not drawn. `StreetRules.borderKind` reads the network; `Districts.borderStreets` draws it.
- **Everything else attaches to it.** Grid lines stop 8 blocks short of a tile border that has no street (`nearAbsentBorder`), so neither
  tile draws a piece that crosses it; a grid run, quay or lane survives only if its piece holds a root (a main road or `b` street with a live
  column): `finishBase` (grid runs) and `dropUnrooted` (after plaza lanes, after quays, after avenue lanes). Ports are off in lattice tiers (a port stub
  with no lane was 421 of 446 detached pieces in towns). Squares and greens that touch no rooted street are dropped (`dropFloatingPlazas`; the hub
  square is exempt, the skeleton joins it) and so are their landmarks in the plan. Skeleton main roads and paths with no live column are not drawn
  (`RoadNetwork.reaches`, also filtered from `plan.roads`; a gate naming one loses its road id) and arterials the network does not draw are
  filtered from `plan.roads` (`SettlementPlan.withRoads`).
- **Quays** join a street through up to 6 tries (nearest columns and their nearest targets) and may end beside a tile's low-side border street
  (`nearestAttach`: a lane there shares an edge with the border street 4 blocks in; on a high side it would cover the column inside the band
  both tiles compare). A quay no street can be reached from is dropped (31 to 10 quays in the temperate gate city, 19 to 11 quay lots).
- `stubLane` also stops when its tip shares an edge with a street (the hub square 8 blocks from a road on the tile border had no way to join it).
- **Rule `roads-attached`** (`SettlementChecks.cityRoads`): every 4-connected piece of street and plaza columns must be the hub's piece, lots or not
  (before only pieces with a lot front were violations). `CityRoads.strays()` counts them. `CityPlanner` moves landmarks to the plaza/lot picked
  by nearest match and leaves out a square whose plaza was dropped.
- Docs: IR.md (district detail, city pipeline).

## Decisions and known issues

- Towns get the lattice too (all urban eligible segments, not only grid-layout ones): a town's ports were the main source of detached stubs and the
  alternative (minimal links only) needs a path search per component. Hamlets and villages lay one street and keep their rules.
- Waterfront quays now exist only where a street can be reached (fewer, none in tiles without a street).
- Earthwork and bridges rose with the extra streets (gate windows, metropolis-temperate: bridges 356 to 515, fill 322k to 406k blocks); steep road stretches 449 to 601 over the nine windows.
- The lattice puts a 5-wide street on every urban tile border (about 130 to 190 blocks apart), so organic quarters get a rectangular frame; frontage
  lanes branch off it. Judgment for John (REVIEW).
