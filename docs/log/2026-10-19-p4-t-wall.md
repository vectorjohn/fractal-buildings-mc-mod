# 4.6 T-wall: wall and gate

Branch `claude/p4-t-wall`. Type `wall` in `gen/v1/types/wall/WallType.java`.

**What it builds** (lot 24x7 .. 64x11, wall along the front): gatehouse of two towers (10-12 wide, 2-3 storeys, stair core through every storey) and a passage between them (open one-storey room, 5 wide, 5 high ground storey), a bridge room over the passage on the upper storeys, curtain galleries (open one-storey rooms, 1-3 wide inside) running out to the lot ends, flat roofs with a 2-high parapet whose upper layer has every second block removed (merlons). Field gate on the north wall (main entrance), town gate on the south wall (second entry in `FacadePlan.others`), both 3 wide x 4 high DOOR boxes. A door from each tower's first upper storey leads onto the curtain's roof (the wall walk).

**Plan fit.** Invariants unchanged; the type-specific rules are the property tests: `WallTypeTest` (gates 3x4 and the passage connects field gate to town gate by BFS on the voxels; every curtain joins a tower on the ground and has a wall-walk door; flat roofs with gaps in the parapet; height cap 7..20; 256 seeds give >= 100 distinct massings). Mutants: no town gate (-> walk-through fails), no wall-walk door and no battlements (-> two tests fail). `V1PropertyTest` for `wall` (120 per lot size x 4 lots) and `fractal-cli check --types wall --seeds 0..99`: 12800 cases, 0 violations.

**Shared edits** (additive): `RoofPass.run(c, RoofStyle)`, `OpeningPass.windows(c)`; `TypeLimits.oriented`, `V1Case` use, `Matrix` and `MainTest` changes copied verbatim from the shop step; `ReplayTest` and `InstancePassV1Test` turn long lots to the front with `oriented` (temple has a different fix for ReplayTest; whichever merges second keeps either). Golden `golden/v1/wall.txt` generated.

**Look.** Galleries `wall-medieval`, `wall-plain`, `wall-flat` (12 seeds, 5 cameras) opened: towers and gate arch read from the front, crenellations show, cutaway shows the passage through the gatehouse and the long gallery. Curtains are plain and low (5 high) next to the 2-3 storey towers; tiny arrow-slit windows. In REVIEW.

*Known issues:* wall is flat on slopes along its length (one slab at the highest ground, foundations fill); `maxGrade` 3. Curtains have no extra wall towers or stairs to the walk apart from the gatehouse doors.

**After merging 4.4 (facades):** `OpeningPass.windows` was dropped in favour of `OpeningPass.run(c, UnaryOperator<Entrance>)` (additive; the wall passes its 3x4 gate cutter). Golden regenerated. Gallery re-read: the medieval facade rules put half-timbering on the stone wall (looks like a timber-framed wall; a wall-specific facade style is for the style step 4.7). `check --types wall --seeds 0..99`: 12800 cases, 0 failing.
