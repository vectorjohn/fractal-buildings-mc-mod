# 2026-10-21 P5 R2: water bias (5.10)

`RegionPlanner.decide` scores town (B 0.3), city and metropolis (B 0.6) candidates as
score x (1 + B x w), w = clamp(1 - dw/768). Hamlets and villages take the old path unchanged.
Constants and `Tiers.waterBias` live at the end of `Tiers`. `dw` = nearest of (a) a water
sample in the candidate's own footprint disk and (b) a global 192-block lattice out to 768 (a
`WaterCache` array shared by the 8 candidates, about 144 probes), so cost stays flat: probe calls
on the world dumps go 334 k -> 336 k and 351 k -> 353 k (x1.006; limit 1.5). Biased tiers may hold
40 % water in the footprint (water + peak <= 0.5, dry centre still required). `CellDecision` gains
`waterDist` (blocks, capped 768; -1 = not evaluated), written to the region dump as `waterDist`
(V0 draws the heat map from it, and now ignores -1). `RegionPlanner.waterBias=false` /
CLI `region --no-water-bias` reproduce the pre-R2 decisions (the "before" maps).

Deviations (see ASSUMPTIONS): the plan's constants alone lift city + metropolis count by +50 %
(17 -> 26 on seed 12345; the 15 % limit) because the 0.40 cap admits most coast cells that were
rejected for water. So the roll chance and `minSuitability` use the unboosted score, and a coastal
footprint (> 0.15 water) rolls at x0.5 (`COASTAL_CHANCE`). And "+50 % of big sites within 256 blocks
of water" cannot be shown on these dumps: 31 of 32 are within 256 before. Over both dumps, city +
metropolis: count 32 -> 35 (+9 %), within 64 blocks 10 -> 16 (31 % -> 46 %), within 128 22 -> 27
(69 % -> 77 %), within 256 31 -> 34. The test asserts count within 15 %, within-64 share x1.4,
128 share not falling. Towns 48 -> 83 over both dumps (no constraint set).

Verification: `RegionWaterBiasTest` (on `world-{12345,987654321}-s64.json.gz`): hamlet/village
decisions equal with and without the bias (records equal, waterDist -1); no centre in water; caps
hold; count, coast share and probe-call ratio as above; `waterDist` agrees with an independent
`WaterField`; bias is order-independent on the synthetic world. Mutation: B = 0 fails three of
these. Existing goldens regenerated (hamlet/village rows unchanged, town/city/metropolis rows
changed), `recordedCropReplays...` now runs with the bias off (crop too small for the wider reach).
