# ROOMWALK2: shop counter walled in by a light

`city-check --seeds 987654321 --terrain recorded:fixtures/terrain/world-987654321-s64.json.gz --sites cells --scenes auto:2` failed one site
(s2_m6 metropolis, district d15_m36, `furnished-rooms-walkable`, shop r0). Note: without `--scenes auto:N` city-check builds no scenes and
reports 0, and the default terrain is not the world map; the roadjoin report's "world 987654321" means the recorded world fixture.

## Cause
Not HookLayout: the shop counter is placed by `Shopfront.interior` (after `InteriorPass`) through `Shopfront.hook`. Its second ("loose") pass
drops the rule that no hook lies within one cell of the cell in front. In building b12 (8x13 plains shop) the counter went into a corner
cell whose use cell had walls on two sides, the counter itself on the third and a floor `light` on the fourth, so the use cell was an
enclosed pocket. `boxesIn` only protects the use cells of earlier pieces; nothing checked the new piece's own use cell against lights.

## Fix
`Shopfront.hook` now requires (for every kind but bed) that the cell in front of the piece has at least one free neighbour that holds no hook
(`hasFreeNeighbour`, same test as `boxesIn` uses for earlier pieces). The invariant is unchanged.

## Verification
- Regression: `RoomWalkTest.aShopCounterIsNotWalledInByALight` rebuilds the exact lot (seed 987654321, `d15_m36.l12` 15x10 west, brief 4/4/1/1/68,
  recorded world fixture); fails without the fix, passes with it.
- city-check `--sites cells --scenes auto:2`: world 987654321 1 failing / 1 violation before, 0 / 0 after (150 sites); world 12345 0 after (152 sites).
- Full planner-core and cli tests: see report.
