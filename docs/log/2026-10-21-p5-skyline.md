# p5-skyline: a smaller tall core and a longer tail (D3 gate feedback)

John (D3 review): the metropolis reads as "oops all highrise"; wants a smaller area of very tall buildings in a dense core, heights varying inside it, and a long tail of mid and low buildings.

## What changed (planner-core)

- `DensityField.cityProfile` (city, metropolis): density no longer `cap (1 - t^2)`. A seeded core fraction `coreFraction` (0.18 + 0.30 u^2 of the radius: median 0.26, up to 0.48 for a packed centre) holds density 100 falling to 80 at its edge; outside it a long fall `80 (1 - s^1.6)` through block and mid-rise density (70 to 80) to the low-rise rim. Towns and smaller keep the old profile.
- `ClassTable`: the 85-and-up row is highrise 62 / block 28 / shop 10; the 70 to 85 row is block 45 / mid-rise highrise 35 / shop 20; the 50 to 70 row has block 40. `lotStoreys` skews each tower's height per lot (hash, u^2.5 between 20 and the density's tallest, range [h - 12 %, h]), in `pick` and in `Briefs.fillLot`, so the core is peaked with few of the tallest instead of a flat 60 plateau. `HIGHRISE_MAX_RELIEF` 12: no high-rise on steeper lots (see below).
- `CityProgram.landmarkMargin` 14: the hub square keeps 14 from the tile edge. Without it one `DistrictsTest` site (case 10) failed `tile-border-agree`: the square 8 from the border clipped a diagonal avenue in its own tile only (a latent ROADS2-era bug that the new density layout hit).
- Tests: `CityProgramTest.theTallCoreIsASmallShareWithAPeakedHeightDistributionAndALongTail` (tall share at most 8 %, at most 20 % of towers reach 45, mid-rise at least 3 %, 4 storeys or fewer at least 80 %); `ClassTableTest.towerHeightsArePeakedAndHighrisesAvoidSteepLots`; thresholds of the park-tile and skyline tests lowered (fewer downtown tiles). Golden `synthetic-city` and the sample-city fixture regenerated.
- `viewer/scripts/city-stats.mjs` prints a storey histogram.

## Findings and known issues

- City blocks are still almost absent (12 and 0 blocks in the two presets). Class-table weights do not matter: `Districts.blockLots` needs a whole 52+ street block with street on all four sides (and density 70+); most candidate intervals sit in tiles with no grid ring. Lowering `BLOCK_DENSITY` to 60 gave about 20 more blocks and was reverted. Needs the grid layout (Districts/StreetRules) to make rings; carried to the polish backlog.
- The 3-to-4-storey shop is the one type that fits the small frontage lots, so the 0.2 to 0.4 R band is mostly shops with high-rises on the few big lots; a "downtown of shops" look is a lot-size effect.
- High-rises on steep ground: before the relief rule, gate-coast-city dm47_m24 had a 44x42 high-rise on a 28-block cliff whose entrance had no way down.

## Round 2

- `:cli:test` fixtures (`CityCommandTest.viewerFixturesAreCurrent`, `districtFixturesAreCurrent`) regenerated with the README commands (skeleton river-town and joined-metropolis; districts river-town, mixed-city, synth-city). Town districts changed through the class-table rows; the joined metropolis through density and the hub margin.
- Synthetic-city golden lost landmarks and roads (12 -> 8 landmarks, 84 -> 72 roads at first). Cause: not the hub margin but the narrower density. Squares need density 50 and up at their centre and arterials are drawn with a probability that depends on density, and a city (cap 85) fell below 50 sooner outside the core. Fixed in part: a city now uses a shoulder of 0.90 (metropolis 0.80), giving 10 landmarks and 79 roads; the rest of the difference is the intended smaller dense area. Raising the tail exponent instead put high-rises beyond 0.75 R (RoadLatticeTest) in packed-core metropolises, so it was not used. Metropolis presets are unaffected by the city shoulder, so the pictures and tables above stand.
- `ClassTableTest` now pins `reliefAllows("highrise", 12)` true and `13` false (a mutation of the cap to 999 fails it). NOTES carries a correction: high-rise `maxGrade` is 3, not 1.
