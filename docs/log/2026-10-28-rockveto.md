# ROCKVETO: no vanilla boulder over our pieces

2026-10-28, branch `claude/p6-rockveto`. Found by ORDERSIM S3 (SC1 6 x 6: `light_gray_concrete->mossy_cobblestone`, cell 1439,64,-9405, writer `forest_rock@1440,65,-9405`).

## Cause

`BlockBlobFeature.place` (26.3 bytecode, `javap -c`): lowers the origin while `can_place_on` fails below it, then three rounds of "set every cell of the box +-(i,j,k), i,j,k in {0,1}, within radius 0.5..1.5 of the origin, with `setBlockAndUpdate`", moving the origin by (-1..0, -1..0, -1..0) between rounds. No replace check. Cells lie in x, z [ox-3, ox+1], y [oy-3-descent, oy+1]. It runs at LOCAL_MODIFICATIONS in the origin's chunk; our pieces at TOP_LAYER of theirs. A boulder in the neighbour chunk, decorated after ours, overwrites a street cell; before ours, it is overwritten. Real in `old_growth_pine_taiga` / `old_growth_spruce_taiga` (2 per chunk), where the taiga style builds. The gate cities have no such biome, so the real order test cannot see it.

## Fix

- `RockVeto` (mod/worldgen) + `BlockBlobFeatureMixin` (HEAD of `place`): `planner.claimsNear(box)` with `box` = the reach above grown by one block, and `DESCENT` = 8 blocks below for the origin search. Overworld only; pure function of origin and plan; failures place the boulder and count in `WorldPlanners.FAILURES`. `RockVeto.VETOED` counts, like `LakeVeto.VETOED`. Mutant switch `Mutations.ROCKS` (tests only; not an order-test mutant since no gate city has the biome).
- Coverage: `BlockBlobFeature` only. Checked all 61 `levelgen/feature/*Feature` classes for `setBlockAndUpdate`: the others are `FallenTreeFeature` (checks `validTreePos`), `MultifaceGrowthFeature` (checks), `HugeFungusFeature` (nether), `EndPlatformFeature` (end). Disks, ores, ice spike, iceberg do their own replace tests and were not widened.
- `SimFeatures.place` asks `RockVeto` for forest_rock calls, like lakes.

## Tests

- `RockVetoTest` (pure decision, same fixture as `LakeVetoTest`): building vetoed and counted; far below, far away not; `ROCKS` off.
- `RockVetoSimTest`: finds (bare terrain, 64 rng seeds) a real forest_rock in a chunk next to a street cell of SC1 that writes it; with `Mutations.ROCKS` the sim's fast orders fail C3/C4 with mossy cobblestone, with the veto they pass and the rock is vetoed in all 6 orders. Another test pins `RockVeto.box` to cover every non-air write of the real feature over 300 seeds.
- `RockVetoGameTest`: the real `forest_rock` feature through the registry in a server world is vetoed by the mixin over a building, placed with the mutant on, and placed when far.
