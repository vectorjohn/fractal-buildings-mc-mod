# 7.12 Generous windows (p7-windows)

DS-33: windows 1x2 by default, 2x2 common, 1x1 accents only.

## What changed
- Styles: `windowHeight` 2 in all seven; `windowWidth` 2 in plain, plains, medieval, jungle; `bayMax` 4 in plain and plains (2-wide windows need an even bay of 4+). Desert, snowy and taiga keep 1-wide windows (small by width and count).
- `FacadePass`: `glassRich(c)` types (shop, block, hall, highrise) in styles with 2-wide windows pick only bay-2/width-1 and bay-4/width-2 variants, and cut storeys 1+ floor to ceiling (3 tall, sill 0). Decor (lintel/sill/shutters) follows the layout's `Win`. High-rises use this, so they pick the new defaults; their mostly-glass exteriors are 7.13/7.7.
- `check/Glazing` (planner-core): per building, glass cells over exterior wall-plane cells (wall, trim, beam, window, door with open air outside, from the ground slab to the top ceiling; window holes of unglazed styles count as glass), split ground / upper, plus window sizes (connected glass groups per face). `Glazing.Report` renders the per-type and per-style tables.
- CLI: `check` appends the Glazing tables; `city-check` does too for the scenes it builds (`--scenes auto:N`).
- Invariant `window-size` (genVersion 2, 4+ windows, 1x1 over 25 % fails). `glazing-share` stays a report: see ROADMAP known issues.
- Tests: `GlazingTest` (every type x style x lots: no `window-size` violation and 3/4 of windows 2+ tall; type glass shares above 12 %, houses under 40 %; hand-built wall with 6 windows of 1x1 and 1x2 for exact counts). Fixed tests that assumed 1-tall windows: `TowerTypeTest.anUndeclaredHole...` (hole now below the window: the head is the ceiling slab), `FacadeLayoutTest.tallFacadesChangeRhythmBetweenBands` (now at most 1/8 of sides show one layout: with bays 2..4 several drawn variants tile a short face alike). Goldens `golden/v1/*.txt` regenerated (all types: geometry changed on purpose).
- Mutation: styles back to `windowHeight` 1 fails `GlazingTest` (window-size and 2-tall share).

## Numbers (check, all types x styles x lots x terrains x fronts)
Glass share before (seeds 0..2) -> after (seeds 0..29): house 10 -> 20 %, shop 12 -> 25, hall 16 -> 24, block 9 -> 22, highrise 11 -> 29, temple 7 -> 14, tower 8 -> 18, workshop 7 -> 14, wall 7 -> 15, bastion 10 -> 19, curtain 10 -> 18. 1x1 share 93-99 % -> 0-1 %. By style: desert 7 -> 13, snowy 7 -> 12, medieval 10 -> 28, plain 12 -> 32, plains 13 -> 34, jungle 11 -> 34, taiga 13 -> 24. ## Round 2 (architect and verifier findings)
- Taiga: `bayMin 3, bayMax 5` like snowy (few, wide bays). Taiga houses 26 % -> about 14 % glass.
- Floor-to-ceiling (sill 0, 3 tall) is now a style flag `facade.tallGlass` (default off; on in plain, plains and tall). Medieval, jungle, taiga, snowy, desert keep 1x2 and 2x2 at sill +1; the shopfront and the hall great room (`HallFacade`) are unchanged. Glass-rich bay rhythms (bays 2 and 4) still apply to shop, block, hall, high-rise in styles with 2-wide windows.
- 2x2 common: in styles with 2-wide windows (`windowWidth` 2, `bayMax` 4: plain, plains, medieval, jungle, tall) every band draws bays of 2 (1x2 windows) or bays of 4 (2x2 windows). Houses 2-wide windows 2 % -> 9-11 % of windows (a 2x2 is one window where 1x2 bays give two), halls and shops 17-20 %.
- Tests: `GlazingTest.windowSizeInvariantFiresOnOneByOneWindowsOnly` (Invariants.check fires `window-size` on the hand-built wall of 1x1 windows, not on 1x2), misleading test renamed; `FacadeLayoutTest` tolerance 1/24. `FloorplanCommandTest` stair room ids follow the merged room numbering (r3 to r6).
- Merged run-phase6 (A1 tall style, B0 high-rise design warnings, RAILFIX): both report sections kept in `check`/`city-check`; v1 and floorplan goldens regenerated. The `tall` style now shows in the per-style table and its high-rises raise the per-style numbers: use the per-type table, or `--styles`.
