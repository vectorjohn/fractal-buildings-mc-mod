# ROADS3: roads graded to gates, straight gate crossings, block rings, metropolis fabric (Phase 5 gate follow-ups)

Branch `claude/p5-roads3` (lead-coder), base `claude/run-phase5` (1eb3700). Follow-ups from the WALLSLOPE r2 report, the SKYLINE report
and the run ledger's polish backlog.

## 1. Roads graded to wall gates

Cause: the gate lot stands at its ring walk level (its highest ground, raised by `WallRing.walkLevels`), while the road through its passage
was graded alone from the ground (median of samples, 1 in 3), so on steep ground the passage floor sat up to 19 blocks above the street.
Fix: `Grading.profile(points, exact, pins)` takes pinned column heights that the grade clamp never moves; `Districts.Build.gatePins` pins
every centre column of a whole street inside a gate's passage band and one column beyond each face to the gate's walk level + 1 (the
first free y over the slab). The ring levels are shared with `wallLots` (`RingInfo`), a pure function of the ring and exact ground, so
every tile grades the road the same. Case seed 2 `noise:9:90:40:150` `--cell -2,1`: dm8_6.w6 front gap 4 → 0, dm7_7.w16 3 → 0.
`WallWalkTest.gatePassagesStayAtTheirOwnGroundAndMeetTheStreet` now asserts |passage floor − street| <= 1 on both faces on the standard
terrains (200 faces) and on steep noise towns and cities (122 faces): all 0.

## 2. roads-connected on a steep synthetic town (seed 30)

Cause: main road rm1_m2_2 ran out through gate g6 to its outer point (64,-1664), the outer route stepped back west onto the gate column
(32,-1664) and left it on a diagonal through the gate lot's tower. `WallRing.crossings` only checked that the straight run's columns were
in the road's column set (the out-and-back spur supplied them), so the ring was accepted; district detail then clipped the road at the tower
and the part beyond (a piece of 1664 street columns with lot d0_m11.l18) lost its link to the hub, while `RoadNetwork` (centre lines,
no wall clipping) still called it live. Fix: `crossings` checks the run through each crossing in the road's own order (`straightRun`), and
`TownProgram.through` routes the inner part strictly inside the ring and the outer part off it (`DefaultProgram.route(..., avoid)`, falling
back to the plain route; `CityProgram` overrides that form so avenues stay octilinear). The town keeps its wall; its road now zigzags
outside. Test: `TownProgramTest.aRoadThatComesBackOntoItsGateColumnIsNoGateCrossing`.

## 3. City blocks

Measured (temporary counters, removed): candidate block intervals failed on the street ring because grid lines were cut wherever a single
node's own column was not grid land (density below 50, unbuildable sample, quarter change), even when the block itself was grid land. Fix:
`StreetRules.gridSideEdgeOk` judges grid land once per block side (the centre of either block the side bounds), only water and slope stay
per node; `BLOCK_DENSITY` 70 → 50 so the 50-70 row's block weight applies. Blocks: d3-metropolis-flat 12 → 64, d3-city-walled 2 → 22,
synth-city 8 → 15; gate windows 1 → 4 in total (metropolis-temperate 0 → 3, mixed 1). On the gate windows the limit is the ground: of 846
grid candidates, 247 are dry, 60 dry and gentle enough for a block lot, 25 of those ringed. Tests: `CityProgramTest.gridQuartersFormStreetRings...`
(every block ringed, >= 60 blocks at 50-84 and >= 20 at 50-69; 923 and 549 now), `gridLandIsJudgedPerBlockSideSoEachSideIsWholeOrAbsent`.

## 4. Metropolis landmarks and roads

`DensityField.fabric`/`fabricAt`: the plain `cap (1 - t^2)` profile (what every tier had before SKYLINE) for the street fabric; `Arterials`
probability and `CityProgram.squareAt` read it, heights and types still read `at`. gate-metropolis-temperate landmarks 23 → 29, roads
137 → 164 (pre-SKYLINE 29 / 165); metropolis-mixed 8 → 7 and 77 → 71 (different arterial draw). The tall core is unchanged (highrise share
from `at`). Test: `CityProgramTest.metropolisArterialsFollowTheStreetFabricNotTheSkyline` (on flat metros every urban segment with fabric 80+
