# 6.20 DOORBANK: doors below an embankment street

Item: ROADMAP Phase 6 "Playtest 2026-10-28 bug list". John's shot (20.10.04, seed 1573336981619055086, 3858,93,-1557): a front door below a raised street, facing the bank wall.

## Reproduction
- No terrain dump of that world exists, so the case is reproduced on the gate windows plus river-town and hill-town.
- `scripts/door-rise.sh` measures on raw terrain, so most "door below the street, no way up" rows there are an artefact: the generator reads the street level in the row outside the lot's front (`Briefs.withStreetFront`), the raw ground there is higher. With the same street-front probe the count of front doors at rise <= -3 is 80 over the 11 sites, and every one of them has no walkable way up to the street (the walker, from the door, never comes out at street level).

## What changed
- `DoorRise.Door.toStreet` (new last component; the old 11-argument constructor stays): a second walker run (same model as `reached`, but it ends only on a cell outside the building's lots at the street's standing level) for doors at `rise <= DoorRise.BANK_RISE` (-3). Other doors copy `reached`.
- `DoorChecks.bankDoor(lot, building, groundProbe)`: a front entrance door at rise <= -2 (round 2; -3 in round 1) with no way up to the street.
- `DistrictScenes.generateLots` (also the mod's path, `world/Pieces`): a lot whose generated building has a bank door is left empty, status `empty:bank` (the status `Briefs` already uses for streets on a bank), recorded in `fallbacks`. Moving the door or building steps up was not done: the margin and the flights belong to PORCHSTEP and 7.19 FALLSTREETS.

## Verification
- `DoorBankTest` (settlement): over the 11 sites (`FB_BANK_SITES=all`; default two sites, 13 s) the control generates every lot without the rule and finds 80 bank doors (river-town 2, hill-town 1, temperate 4, desert-badlands 5, snow 1, jungle-savanna 6, border 4, coast 1, metropolis-temperate 32, metropolis-mixed 19, joined 5); with the rule 80 lots are emptied and 0 built lots have one. The control count is the "fails on old code" evidence.
- CLI `city` on gate-snow-city: `d29_2.l4` (house, door -4) is now `empty:bank`.

## Known issues
- Only front entrance doors are checked: a side or back entrance facing a bank is not (known issue). Doors at -2 and below are covered since round 2 (a 2-block climb is out of a jump's reach); -1 doors go to the backlog under DS-54.
- Lots lost: 80 of several thousand built lots over the 11 sites (about 1 %); more on hilly maps. 7.19 should bring the streets down and win them back.
