# p5-openplan: open plan between public rooms (coder)

Branch `claude/p5-openplan` (base `claude/run-phase5`). John's gate note (REVIEW, 2026-10-21): partition rooms without
doors, kitchen/living open in modern houses, separated in older ones.

## What changed

- New style key `interior.openPercent` (`InteriorStyle`, default 40): chance that a connection between a kitchen and another
  public room (living, entry, hall) is a doorless archway. Values: medieval 20, taiga 35, snowy 40, desert 60, plains 65,
  jungle 70, plain 85. Other public pairs (living/entry/hall/landing/porch) use `max(40, openPercent)`; bedrooms never open
  (`RoomProgram.openPlan`, unchanged). From 60 up the archway is 2 to 4 cells wide (else 1 to 3), with a pier of wall left at
  each end and a TRIM lintel over spans of 2+ where the storey is tall enough (as before).
- `FloorPlan.withArches` takes a per-pair percent and `wideFrom`; `InteriorPass.pct` supplies it.
- Wings joined across a strip wall (`stripDoor`) can now also be archways (they were always doors, which is why only ~25 %
  of kitchen links were open before): same percent, extension along the strip while both rooms, the cells and the strip allow,
  not into a stair core, not over furniture (`hookAt`).
- Tests: new `OpenPlanTest` (1500-house table with `-Dfb.openplan=N`; asserts plain >= 60 % of kitchen links open, medieval
  <= 35 %, most plain ones 2+ wide, bedrooms keep every door). `FacadeLayoutTest.plainHasNoDecorAndMedievalDoes` now ignores
  INTERIOR-layer boxes (the lintel is not facade decor). Goldens regenerated (hall, house; GEN_VERSION stays 2).
  `CirculationTest`, `HouseProgramTest`, `RoomShapeTest`, `V1PropertyTest` (rooms reachable, roof closed, etc.) pass unchanged.

## Verification

`:planner-core:test` full run passes (534 tests). Mutation: not run separately; `OpenPlanTest` thresholds are well clear of
the pre-change shares (plain 22 % vs bound 60 %). Gallery presets `house-medieval`, `house-plain`, `house-plains` (0
violations) and the new `house-plain-open`; PNG cutaways and ASCII plans for seed 7 and others in
`docs/review/phase5/circulation/` ("After open plan"): plain houses show 2-4 wide gaps between kitchen and living;
medieval seed 7 keeps a 1-wide arch plus a door. CLI/viewer not otherwise changed. Client game tests not run (no mod change).

## Decisions

One percent per style rather than a per-building "open house" flag; cheap to move in the style JSON. No "dining" room use exists in
the house program, so living<->dining is covered as living<->hall/entry.
