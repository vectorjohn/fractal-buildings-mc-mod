# FLY2: fly-through redesign (6.8)

John 2026-10-26: the metropolis frames showed 2-5 buildings, often an empty lot; wanted fewer, better, wide shallow views.

## Done
- `FlyThroughClientGameTest`: frames now come from plan data. `masses()` lists every planned building of the settlement (tiles the settlement owns, `planner.buildings`), weight = footprint x (1 + height/30). `densest()` slides a square window (side up to (view - 16) / 1.3, so the far edge stays inside the render distance) over the footprint in 16-block steps and takes the heaviest, so parks and empty lots are never targets. `sweep()` stands 0.8 x side back in the best of eight directions (lowest terrain on the sight line), at 30-34 degrees elevation (and at least over half the tallest building), aimed a bit up the tallest building.
- Frames: hamlet/village/town one `view`; city/metropolis `sweep-a` (densest window) and `sweep-b` (another window if the footprint is wider than the window, else the same from the other side); town also one `interior`. 3 seeds: 24 frames, was 90.
- Settings: `FB_FLY_RD` (default 10), `FB_FLY_SIZE` (default 1920x1080; `context.getInput().resizeWindow` works, screenshots came out 1920x1080), clouds off (so the camera ceiling is 300, was 175), `--rd/--size` in `scripts/flythrough.sh`. JPEGs up to 1920 wide. Manifest has new columns `buildings` (in the window) and `window`; a sweep window with 0 buildings fails.
- FLYCAP's `stableShot` and noon pinning kept.

## Round 2 (big render distance, Intel iGPU)
- Merged run-phase6 (CITYQ also edited this test: kept the redesigned frames, kept CITYQ's "camera is inside <block>" warning, dropped slope/edge).
- Camera: stands 0.7 x side back (was 0.8), elevation 24 degrees for sweeps and 28 for views, a tall window raises the camera toward 0.55 x tallest building but never past 30 degrees down, and the aim point is lowered so the view is at least 24 degrees down (aimed up a tower the frames were 40-50 % sky and haze). Whole-footprint fitting: the window side is `(rd*16-16)/1.3`, 283 at rd 24 and 381 at rd 32; the footprint of a city or metropolis is 1000-2300 wide, so the sweeps show the densest window plus a second one.
- Per-tier render distance: `FB_FLY_RD` for hamlet/village/town, `FB_FLY_RD_BIG` (default = `FB_FLY_RD`) for city and metropolis; `scripts/flythrough.sh --rd 24 --rd-big 32`. The client option is set before each settlement's frames.
- Fill wait: waits for 98 % of the chunks inside radius rd-1 around the camera plus `hasRenderedAllSections`, cap in wall-clock seconds 60 (rd <= 12) / 90 (<= 24) / 180, `FB_FLY_FILL_S` overrides. First version counted ticks: a loaded machine runs ticks slowly (server 1000 ticks behind) and the cap took twice as long.
- Manifest: new columns `rd`, `fill`, `fill_s`, `tp` (camera `x y z yaw pitch` for `/tp @s`), `commit` (`FB_FLY_COMMIT`, set by the script). The README of docs/review/phase6/flythrough says how to visit a frame.
- `scripts/flythrough.sh`: header says when to run it (opt-in; review packs, phase gates, changes to how cities look; not in test-all); `FB_VK=intel` passes through; `FB_CLIENT_TIMEOUT` defaults to 4 h (the 3600 s watchdog killed a rd 32 run at the very end, exit 124).
