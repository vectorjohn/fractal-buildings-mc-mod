# 2026-10-28 DS35DOORS: more entrances (DS-35 and the workshop amendment)

Branch `claude/p6-ds35doors`, base `claude/run-phase6`. Rules: DS-35 "More entrances" and "DS-35 amendment: workshop doors" (approved
2026-10-28), the WORKSHOP lean-to polish line.

## What changed

- `facade/ExtraDoors` (new): after the main entrances, picks the secondary doors and turns the window bay each takes into a door bay.
  Per type: house (2+ storeys or 80+ ground cells): back/side door, prefers the south face, then east/west, never a street side first; shop
  (upper floors or 80+ cells, not a market hall): house door at the back; hall, temple: a side door nearest the face centre; high-rise: a
  door on a second face (not the main one's); block: one if it has fewer than two entrances; workshop: one door each for its `wing` and
  `lean-to` masses. Towers, stalls, market halls, walls: none. A mass whose stair core fills the middle of its faces takes a cell off the
  centre, or the wall cell where another mass joins it.
- `FacadePlan.extras()` (new fourth component, three-argument constructor kept): `entrances()` is main + others + extras, `primary()` main +
  others. Secondary doors get no surround (`OpeningPass`), 1 wide. High-rise main entrance is 2 wide (`FacadePass.entrance`).
- Interior: an extra door's inside cell is reserved and clear but not an entry hall (`InteriorPass.Info.back`, `RoomProgram.Slot.back`,
  `RoomProgram.backUse`). `HouseRooms` prefers the back-door room as the kitchen and swaps uses so it is never a bedroom; other programmes
  turn a bedroom or plain room into a hall. Workshop programme: the living wing uses `HouseRooms` (kitchen with job blocks, bedroom, no
  workstation), the lean-to is a `store` (chests, counter, light), the hall stays `workshop`.
- `InstancePassV1`: secondary doors (non-front doors of house, shop, hall, temple, highrise, workshop, and a workshop's wing and lean-to
  doors) never lift the slab; where the outside ground is above the floor (by more than 1) or the `steps-reach-ground` model
  (`StairGround.failingDoors`, now public) finds no way down, the door is walled up and the building placed again. The front doors, and so
  the DS-45 door rise, are as before.
- `check/EntranceCount` (new invariant `entrance-count`, and an "Entrances" table in the `check` report): see the ROADMAP line. Not enforced
  on a lot that is not level (any ground relief) or no wall cell off the front has usable ground outside (`EntranceCount.blocked`); the report column says how
  many buildings that was.
- Goldens regenerated: `golden/v1` hall, highrise, house, shop, temple, workshop (block, tower, wall unchanged).

## Decisions and surprises

- The shell is terrain-blind, so the extra door is always generated; the placement walls it up when the ground forbids it. The check is
  therefore enforced only on level lots (relief 0): a lot rising 1 toward the back already seals the shop's and house's back door in a third of the cases, because the dig-in leaves the bank behind them. Alternatives rejected: several candidate doors per building (too many doors on flat ground), a flag in the IR
  (format change for the viewer).
- `steps()` in the instance pass can find a flight the `steps-reach-ground` model rejects (it accepts ground a block above the last
  tread). Not fixed; the secondary door is walled up by asking the model.
- Corner buildings (a door on each street) and a door every 12-20 blocks of a long frontage are not built (DS-35); blocks keep one door
  per unit.
- Rails come before doors (DS-8 before DS-35): `GeneratorV1` first tries the placement without its secondary doors before setting the
  building back, and `InstancePassV1.placeChecked` drops them when their rails leave a diagonal gap.
- High-rise doors: the second door must open into the lobby (the hall rows of `TowerBlock`, the one room the plates always cover there);
  a 2-wide main door only from an envelope 3 wider and 2 deeper than the type minimum (`ExtraDoors.wideMain`): the smallest lots on
  steep ground have no room for the landing, so those keep one wide door and `entrance-count` does not ask for more.
- Flake: none seen. A throwaway `FB_PRESETS` env var is ignored by `gen-gallery.mjs` (it takes preset names as arguments).

## Round 2 (reviews: architect, interior designer, verifier)

- Beds: `HouseRooms.sleepSafeBack` (back-door room never the only bedroom: other bedroom upstairs or elsewhere, else swap with a ground kitchen/living room, else a one-room wing is a kitchen-hall with a bed, `Slot.needsBed` to `Draft.needsBed`); the workshop wing also gets `someoneSleeps` (a bed in its biggest kitchen/living room when it has no bedroom). Small masses (under 20 cells, or under 12 with two storeys) take no door (`ExtraDoors.roomy`); a workshop without such a wing or lean-to gets a person door on its hall. Beds per `check` report ("with a bed" column): house 12273 -> 12268 of 16000, workshop 8392 -> 8623, shop 16000 both, high-rise 2715 -> 2723.
- DS-24: store light is a `ceiling_light` first in its piece list: 1616 of 1616 lean-to stores lit (was 0 of 17).
- DS-26: `OpeningPass` gives every main entrance (not the wall types) a head and jambs in every style; plain buildings now have trim at the door; secondary doors still none. `FacadeLayoutTest.plainHasNoDecorAndMedievalDoes` allows it.
- DS-45: `InstancePassV1.badSecondary` (the `DoorRise` measure) walls up a secondary door whose way down is not walkable, whose run is over 3 risers or whose ground outside is more than a block above the sill; the window comes back (`sealed()`, opening id `sealed-<door id>`, which `EntranceCount.walledUp` reads); `GeneratorV1` first asks for the door on another non-front face (`ExtraDoors.BANNED`, a thread-local face mask, restored in `finally`), then walls it up. Doors never go on the front face now.
- High-rise: the 2-wide main door only when the envelope is even wide (on the axis), else 1 wide.
- Lane (DS-48): a secondary door that leaves a furnished room without a clear lane gives way (`GeneratorV1` tries the walled-up placement).
- `entrance-count` also exempts any building with a `sealed-` window; posts and legs no longer count as floor area; the high-rise rule only applies to big buildings.
- Goldens regenerated on the tree with run-phase6 merged last (`golden/v1` all nine types, floorplan goldens).
- Shop door counts: the earlier `check` runs use the default shop terrains (gentle slopes, relief at most 1); on `plane:64:1:0:4` (a 1:4 slope) the instance pass walls the back door up in a share of shops, as for houses.
- Not done: the empty-room case for rooms that are not tiny masses (kitchen hearth or chapel bench lost to a door: 2-3 houses of 30), needs a door-position retry after the hook layout; a lean-to tub; corner and frontage doors.

## Round 3 (lead coder; John 2026-10-28 "Lets do now": DS-35 on sloping lots; interior r2 violation; verifier r2; architect r2)

- Slopes: `GeneratorV1.withDoors` places the building, and when the placement walls up a secondary door, its flight cannot be railed
  inside the lot (DS-8), or it leaves a furnished room without a lane (DS-48), generates the shell again with an `ExtraDoors.Survey` of the
  building (thread-local `ExtraDoors.SURVEY`, removed in `finally`; only with the direct shell generator): every non-front face and every cell of every window bay is
  scored by the ground outside (in the lot, at most a block above the slab, the ground past the lot at most 2 above the sill, fewer risers
  first), failed cells are banned; three phases of up to 8 tries: cells with a railed plain flight (`EntranceCount.plainFlight`), any
  cell, then the ground as the cut left it, all surveyed on the building placed without its secondary doors. The flight itself is
  the existing DS-45 search (runs of at most 3, landings); `badSecondary` still walls up a door with a run over 3, no walkable way down,
  or a bank (rise below -2 now, the DS-45 limit; was -1). The r2 face mask `ExtraDoors.BANNED` is gone.
