## What

- `core/settlement/Districts.plan(SkeletonSource, ix, iz, exact)` -> `Detail(plan, landmarks, notes)`. `SkeletonSource` (+ `Direct`, memoising) serves one settlement's skeletons, fields and tier program. Pointwise street rules in `StreetRules` (shared grid lines keyed by (seed, axis, tile interval), plain border streets, ports); `StreetCols` (centre-line columns, clipping, polylines).
- Per tile: plazas from the landmarks (`Grading.plaza`, shifted on the 16-lattice or dropped with a note), hall and temple lots flush against the hub square, skeleton roads, pointwise arterials, plain border streets, grid lines, port lanes (a stub both ways over the border, then A* to the nearest base street), frontage lanes (every 40..70 blocks, 24..56 long, A* on the 8-lattice with slope cost), then lots: whole-block `block` lots in grid blocks (density >= 70, the table picks block), frontage packing along axis-aligned runs (type from `ClassTable.pick`, size from the type's test lots, depth <= 44), briefs (`streets` mask and `frontY` through `Briefs`' helpers, `airRights` by decision 7).
- Streets are clipped to the tile plus 8 blocks so neighbours list the same streets; `Street.y` comes from `Grading.profile` (new, no boxes) on the clipped columns, with a point every 8th column, so the scene's `Grading.road` gives the same heights.
- `SettlementChecks`: `lot-frontage`, `lot-dry`, `lot-grade`, `roads-connected` (tile part), `tile-border-agree` (two plans), `detail(...)`. `SettlementPlan.withDistricts`. `Arterials.waterOk` (factored out, same behaviour).
- CLI `city --districts [--scene ID,ID]`; fixtures `viewer/fixtures/settlement/districts/{river-town,mixed-city,synth-city}`; plan view colours for `block`/`highrise` and legend; `viewer/e2e/districts.spec.ts`.
- Cheap-to-change decisions (grid lines per interval, border streets, ports, block only in grid blocks, diagonal frontage by axis-aligned runs, hall lots, airRights): ASSUMPTIONS rows "DL"; the plan's Deviations has the contract changes. Not done by design: SUBDIVIDE layout, diagonal avenues (John, D3). `ClassTable` untouched.

## Surprises and known issues

- Real vanilla terrain gives few lots (the lake-rich window 254 in 18 tiles): SK's patchy mask, not DL.
- Skeleton roads over steep ground keep their cut and fill (`ok()` false is noted per tile, not rerouted: the skeleton owns them); inner grid streets and lanes that fail are dropped, border streets and ports are kept for agreement.
- Road y can differ by a block or two across a tile border (each tile grades its piece); briefs take storeys from the district quarter, not the lot's; hall and temple landmark footprints from SK are smaller than the types' minimum lots (DL grows the lot, SK's rectangles are unchanged).
- Lots use a 4-lattice for relief and a first water screen; every accepted lot is checked column by column.
