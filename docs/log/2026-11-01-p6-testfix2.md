# TESTFIX2: portal-hold flake and LinkShortCutTest time

Two test-only changes.

## PortalHoldGameTest "must start cold"
Cause: `coldExitHoldsThenGoes` and `leavingThePortalDropsTheRequest` built their portal at their own test structure (`helper.absolutePos`). The structures of a batch are close together, so the nether exit area (portal coordinates / 8) was the same for both. The first test generates it fully, so a second test that ran after it failed the cold check. Batch order varies, hence one failure in several runs.
Fix: `inOwnArea` builds each test's portal in a chunk 8192 * slot blocks from the test structure (slot 1 and 2), holds that chunk with a DRAGON ticket, waits until it is entity-ticking, places the frame at the surface height and the pig, and releases ticket, portal and pig at the end (also on failure). The cold check is kept as it was. Nothing else in the checks changed.

## LinkShortCutTest
The three full sweeps (all gates, all links, all orders on 3 worlds) are `@Tag("heavy")`. The default suite runs, on pt1573 only (the other two worlds cost about 5 s per fresh planner and stay in the heavy sweeps): `sampledGateAnswersEqualTheFullRule` (every 40th gate, 10 gates), `sampledLinkStatesEqualTheFullRule` (every 4th link, 16 links), `theAskingOrderDoesNotMatterOnASample` (every 6th link, one shuffle, no gates) and the unchanged `theShortCutDrawsFewerApproaches`. Samples are fixed by index, so deterministic. The coverage asserts (served and not served; LIVE and DROPPED) still hold on the sample (links LIVE/DROPPED/DEAD_END 12/2/2; gates 10: 4 served, 6 not).
