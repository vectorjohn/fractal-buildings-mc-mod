# DS36ROOFS: smooth pitched roofs (DS-36)

2026-10-28, branch `claude/p6-ds36roofs`. John approved DS-36 the same day: pitched roofs in stairs (1:1) or slabs (1:2), never stepped full blocks; hips and valleys with corner stairs; ridges capped; eaves a stair course; the roof material has stairs and a slab.

## What changed

- **Roles** (`ir/Role`, appended): `roof_stair_n/e/s/w` (facing = the high side, local to the building), `roof_slab`, `roof_slab_top`. All solid and closed; `Role.isRoof()` is the family test; the palette key stays `roof` (`Variant`). `PieceRaster.Grid` turns the stair facing with the building's rotation (`Role.turned`).
- **Raster** (`Shapes.roofStair`, `Shape.corner`): stair `shape` (inner and outer corners) from Minecraft's own rule over the neighbouring roof stairs, so the game would pick the same state; slabs get their half. Mod `BlockMapper` maps the roles to the material's stair and slab (`shape` property added), full block with one warning if a material has none.
- **RoofPass**: the exposed top cell of every pitched column is chosen by `Group.topRole` from the heightfield. Stairs face up the slope (1:1 and steeper, incl. 2:1 and the mansard's steep part: stair over the full-block risers that were already there); a summit cell faces across the ridge (back to back on an even span); a lone ridge row, its ends and plateau middles take a bottom slab; hip ring corners alternate their facing along x and z by ring so the eave stairs stay straight and the diagonal gets outer corners; gable ridges face across only. Slab pitches (1:2, 1:3): the first column of a step a bottom slab, the rest top slabs. Overhang: the ring beside an eave is a stair (stair pitches) or a top slab, further rings bottom or top slabs. Dormer caps are bottom slabs in the main roof's material (the stair gable dormer of the DORMER polish line is **not** done). Flat roofs, parapets, risers and gable-end walls unchanged.
- **Check** `roof-smooth` (`Invariants.roofSmooth`, v1 scenes only): a full `roof` block with nothing solid above it, inside the footprint of a pitched roof's volume, unless it is the deck of a flat roof over that column.
- **Palettes**: plains roof `terracotta`, `red_terracotta` to `bricks` x2 + `granite`; taiga `brown_terracotta` to `spruce_planks`; jungle `moss_block` to `mossy_cobblestone` (ASSUMPTIONS row).
- **Viewer**: the roof family is drawn as `roof` cubes (`voxels.ts`; the viewer has no half blocks), so its gallery looks the same as before apart from the palette colours.

## Round 2 (lead-coder, 2026-10-28)

The verifier rated round 1 INCOMPLETE: the order test's `granite_stairs->granite_stairs [shape]` (spiral, shuffle1; 1435,72,-9361, chunk-local z 15, birch leaves beside it) is our own roof stair's shape depending on chunk order, and round 1 had hidden it in `SpillKinds` (e2e88a6b, reverted).

- **Cause** (26.3 decompiled): `TreeFeature.place` ends with `StructureTemplate.updateShapeAtEdge(level, 3, ...)` (TreeFeature.java:188; StructureTemplate.java:396-414), which runs `updateShape` on every block beside the tree's voxels. `StairBlock.updateShape` (lines 102-119) recomputes `shape` from the four horizontal neighbours (`getStairsShape`, `canTakeShape`, lines 121-153). If the stair's chunk is written before the chunk holding its across-border stair neighbour, a later tree reshapes it against a missing block, and the neighbour's write (UPDATE_KNOWN_SHAPE) never corrects it. The stair's own building is enough; no other building is involved.
- **Fix, order** (`mod/.../worldgen/StairSeam`, `WorldPlanner.seam`/`stairsAt`): a building piece also writes, one block across the chunk border, every stair beside one of its own border stairs, as that cell's owner rasters it (the bed pattern of `HookPlacer`). A stair's neighbours are then in the world as soon as it is, and every chunk that writes a stair or one of its stair neighbours rewrites it, so the last of them leaves the raster's shape, which a later update recomputes unchanged. Not recorded in the ledger (the owner records it); its own write contract in the sim (`SimCompare`: only a stair the plan gives there, exactly one block outside its chunk).
- **Fix, rule** (`Shapes.RoleView.stairAt`, `PieceRaster.Grid.stairAt`): a roof stair's corner counts every stair the world will hold beside it, as Minecraft does: interior and porch stairs (with the facing their own rules give), the tile's other buildings, and a cell an earlier building owns counts as that building's block. The grids round a clip grow by 2 (a neighbour's interior-stair facing reads its own neighbours), and other buildings are skipped by bounds (`PieceRaster.near`). In 3 gate windows no roof stair's shape changes from this (count 0), so it is correctness for cases not seen yet.
- **Architect r1 violation**: a hip's eave-overhang corner cell is now an outer-corner stair facing along z (the eave corner above faces along x), so the hip line runs down to the overhang (`RoofPass.eaveCorner`); before it was a bottom slab and the two stairs beside it turned outer (the notch). **Should-fix**: taiga roofs are `dark_oak_planks` alone, jungle roofs `jungle_planks` alone (ASSUMPTIONS row). Plains/taiga brief lines no longer name terracotta. HallTypeTest uses `isRoof()`; the STANDARDS DS-36 enforcement line is back to the approved text.
- **Tests**: `StairSeamTest` (core, 3 gate windows: every roof stair has Minecraft's shape over the whole tile, written out independently of `Shapes`; the seam of every building piece over every chunk clip equals the stairs across the clip beside its own stairs, as the tile holds them), `RoofStairShapeTest` (another building's stair, an interior stair, a cell an earlier building took; reverting `stairAt` fails 3), `RoofSmoothTest.hipOverhangRingTurnsItsCornersWithStairs` (map plus exactly four outer corners on the overhang ring; the old code fails it), `RoofStairSeamSimTest` (ORDERSIM: a roof stair whose inner corner comes from across a chunk border, the real `updateShapeAtEdge` run on it right after its chunk's pieces, 6 fast orders, audit contracts; the `no-stair-seam` mutant fails 3 orders, heavy tag), `WorldgenGameTest` readback (every stair of the fixture town and the flat plains town: `Block.updateFromNeighbourShapes` changes nothing; 1529 and 5827 stairs; forcing roof stairs straight fails it).
