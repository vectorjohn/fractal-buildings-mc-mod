# 4.7 St-jungle

Added `styles/jungle.json` (extends `plain`), line in `styles/index.txt`, gallery presets
`house-jungle` (20x20) and `tower-jungle` (13x13; tower is the merged large type).

- Biomes: `jungle`, `sparse_jungle`, `bamboo_jungle` (no other style claims them).
- Roof: hips 4:1 over gables, rise/run 1/1, overhang 2, no dormers. Facade: post corners,
  sills, lintels, door surround, 1-wide windows, no plinth/timber. No jetties; setback 20 %.
- Palette: 3-4 block gradients per main role (bamboo, jungle planks/logs, mossy cobblestone
  foundation, moss/bamboo-mosaic roof); viewer colours parallel.
- `JungleStyleTest`: ids valid against `vanilla-26.3-*.txt` (St-plains's files), biomes
  owned, and over 150 houses (flat terrain) invariants clean, >= 80 % of pitched buildings
  show an actual roof-over-air overhang of 2 (measured in the IR boxes, not read from the
  style), >= 50 % hip roofs. Mutation: style overhang 2 -> 1 fails the test.
- Gallery (Read): house-jungle front-left: broad green/bamboo hip roofs overhanging the walls,
  bamboo-yellow walls with jungle-log posts, 0 violations on 16 seeds; tower-jungle: tall
  bamboo shafts, hip caps with overhang on shorter towers, flat tops on the tall ones.
- Known issues: no verandas (no pass offers a porch; not added to avoid blocking doors or
  front flights); raised ground floors skipped (optional).
