# Spike S2: terrain probe

Spike on `claude/spike-s2` (not merged). Test `TerrainProbeGameTest` generates real chunks (fill, surface rules, carvers) on ProtoChunks for 10 seeds x 7 windows x 4 mod sets (vanilla, Terralith, Tectonic, both) and compares with `getBaseHeight` predicted first. Findings in `docs/research/spikes/s2.md`, raw tables in `docs/research/spikes/s2-results/`.

Headline: exact probe 99.0-99.9 % equal to real on land (100 % vs the filled chunk), misses are powder snow; icebergs add up to +99 over ocean floor; coarse bias median -12..-16, not stable (64-72 % within +-4 after fix; much worse on Tectonic mountains/Terralith skylands); per column 0.32 / 0.75 / 0.76 / 0.93 ms.

Build-file change: appended block in `mod/build.gradle` (test-only jars via `-Ps2ModSet`), `scripts/fetch-test-mods.sh`, `scripts/s2-run.sh`. No flake change.

Not covered: features, carvers under Terralith/Tectonic (NPE with null WorldGenRegion), quiet-machine timings. Mutation check: predicting from seed+1 drops equal-to-B from 98 % to 5.4 %.
