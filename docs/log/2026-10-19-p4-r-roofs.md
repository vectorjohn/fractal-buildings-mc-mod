## What it does

- Roof **groups**: same-top masses that overlap and are not party walls share one heightfield;
  columns under a strictly higher mass are skipped; an earlier equal-top group owns shared strips.
- Forms (style `roof.forms` weights via `Choice`, keyed per group): `gable` (per mass along its long
  side; a perpendicular wing's ridge runs on into the main mass up to its centre line, so L/T/U/
  courtyards get cross gables and valleys), `hip` / `pyramid` (chessboard distance to open cells,
  so courtyards get hips round the hole), `shed` (high side against a taller wall, else seeded),
  `mansard`, `flat`. Tall groups (`tallStoreys`, default 6) and partly covered rings are flat with
  a parapet (`parapet`, `parapetRole`).
- Voxelising: one ROOF voxel per column at `base + h`; fill beside lower region neighbours (ROOF,
  starting level with the neighbour so steps are face-adjacent) and beside open air (WALL gable
  ends); heights next to a higher mass's wall are capped at its top slab. Closure holds for any
  field, so every form is safe by construction.
- Overhang at y = top slab on free envelope columns beyond the eaves; pitch falls back 1/2, 1/3,
  flat under `maxHeight`; dormers on straight 1/1 street slopes (closed 3-wide box, WINDOW voxel).
- One `Roof` record per roofed mass (so `roof-covers`/`roof-closed` check every volume).
