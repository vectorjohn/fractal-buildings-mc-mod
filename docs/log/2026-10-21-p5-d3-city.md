# D3: city and metropolis programs, density and skyline (5.7; half of 5.13)

Branch `claude/p5-d3-city`, base `claude/run-phase5` (D0 to D2, TR, SC merged). Pictures: `docs/review/phase5/d3/`.

## What changed

- **`ClassTable`** (`settlement/ClassTable.java`): the 70 to 84 row's high-rise is a mid-rise of 10 to 19 storeys rising with density (`highriseStoreys`: 10 at 70, 20 at 85, 60 at 100, 12 % spread); the 85+ row is 70/25/5 high-rise/block/shop; an old town (quarter `old-town`) drops blocks and high-rises (weight to shops) and caps at 5 storeys. The **mid-rise gap**: `HighriseType` limits start at 10 instead of 20 (its property matrix, `check --types highrise --seeds 0..199`: 89,600 cases, 0 failures, `HighriseTypeTest` sweeps 10 to 60), so no new type; highrise digests and `BriefTest` follow.
- **`CityProgram`, `MetropolisProgram`** (`settlement/tiers/`, mapped in `TierPrograms`): 3 to 4 avenues from the hub (metropolis: 8), every second compass point from a hashed start, the four diagonals on 75 % of cities. A route is a straight lattice ray (the exit is the furthest column that stays owned, urban, dry, grade 1 in 3), or an octilinear dogleg (diagonal then axis, either order), else the A* of `DefaultProgram`; `legOk`/`stepOk` check each step. Squares (24 to 36) in the corner of a tile beside about 1 in 4 arterial crossings, touching the arterials, a hall facing half of them (`cellLandmarks`, new `TierProgram` hook; `Districts` reads the landmarks of every cell reaching the tile). Parks: `QuarterField` makes 1 downtown tile in 8 (tile centre density >= 80, not the hub's) `park`, no lots, no grid streets (`streetLayout`).
- **`OldTown`** (`settlement/OldTown.java`, built in `DensityField`): 60 % of cities draw a `WallRing` at 0.25 to 0.35 of the radius, computed from the site and the coarse terrain alone, so the skeleton and every district agree. `Arterials` skips segments that touch the ring (the 5-wide gate passage cannot take a 9-wide arterial), `QuarterField` makes the inside `old-town`, `CityProgram` (subclass of `TownProgram`, `ring` now overridable) builds the wall with gates on the avenues when the centre cell owns every ring column. A walled city's avenues are 5 wide (`TierProgram.mainWidth(SkeletonContext)`), else 7 (metropolis 9).
- **`Arterials`**: a metropolis's outer ring: segments between tiles whose centres lie inside and outside 0.62 of the footprint radius (`outerRing`), a closed staircase.
- `Briefs.storeys` fix (found by the new test): a lot whose centre is in a quarter that drops its type (a high-rise beside an old town or waterfront) fell back to the type's whole 10 to 60 storeys; it now takes the density row's range, within the quarter cap.
- Viewer: `height` layer (lots by top storey), `plan-height.png` in `city.spec.ts`, `scripts/city-stats.mjs`; presets `d3-metropolis-flat`, `d3-city-walled`, `d3-city-avenues`.

## Decisions and deviations

- Highrise limit lowered (the plan's optional "briefMinStoreys 10") instead of a `briefMinStoreys` field. Avenue width 5 in walled cities (the wall type's passage is 5; widening it is not D3's). The ring is found without cell ownership (`OldTown`) so arterials can be kept out of it from any tile; the wall is dropped when the centre cell does not own all of it (joined cities mostly get none, but no arterials inside). Assumption rows added.
- DL's cut-block handling (leftover triangles to plazas) is not done: frontage lots still pack only along straight runs, so a diagonal avenue has no lots along it and the triangles stay empty or hold the straight streets' lots (visible in `city-avenues-plan.png`); the avenues themselves, 45 degrees, no lot overlap and `roads-connected` within KNOWN are tested.

## Surprises, known issues

- Cities planned with a synthetic `--site` that is not a region site's position (e.g. `0,0`) are one small cell whose centre cell may not hold the centre: no hub landmarks or walls. Use `--site` at a region site (`RegionField.siteX/Z`) for synthetic cities (the presets do).
- `gate-metropolis-mixed` now has `roads-connected` (2, in KNOWN; base 0): its hub square cannot grade, moves 150 blocks, and `plazaLanes` finds a lane to a dead end (the arterial 36 blocks away lies in the excluded tile margin); this is the ROADS item's area. The same fix would help every moved square.
- Pre-existing on the integration branch (not D3): `e2e/skeleton.spec.ts` river-town bridge and `e2e/districts.spec.ts` mixed-city violations fail after TR r2.
- Squares in a tile that district detail cannot grade are moved or dropped (about 25 % were moved in the test cities).
