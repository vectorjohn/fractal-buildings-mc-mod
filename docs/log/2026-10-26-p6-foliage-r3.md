# FOLIAGE round 3 (2026-10-26)

Fixes from the verifier and the architect review of round 2.

- **Gametest entrypoints (breaking)**: merged `claude/run-phase6` and took its `mod/src/gametest/resources/fabric.mod.json` (self-registration placeholders); `FoliageClientGameTest` is found by the source scan. `:mod:runGameTest`: all 35 required passed (TeleportHold and TESTISO tests included).
- **Live tree beside a built block**: a log cluster that touches natural leaves and also a built block (fence, wall, planks) now stays a LOG and counts as supported (it seeds the leaf search) instead of turning into OTHER; its leaves stay.
- **Headroom (architect 3a)**: `FoliageClientGameTest` now counts natural leaves in surface +1..+4 over every street and road column after all chunks around have generated, and `FoliageClear.MEASURE/RESIDUAL` counts what each pass itself leaves in its own columns. This found the real cause of foliage still over roads: `RoadFeature` limited its clear to cells up to `ground.scanTop`, the WORLD_SURFACE_WG heightmap, which at feature time knows the terrain but not the trees and plants the features grew, so every canopy above the old ground stayed over the road (RESIDUAL 1734 on seed 1). The clear now reads every cell up to `clearTo`. Result (natural leaves in surface+1..4 at the end): seed 1 road 647 -> 3, street 21 -> 4, seed 31337 road 65 -> 0, street 0; passes' own residual 0. The remainder is crowns of neighbour trees that grew after the chunk's pass: CITYQ's SpillClear territory, not duplicated. Hedges and street trees of ours (persistent leaves) are not counted.
- **Seed 1 middle tree (3b)**: not a crown cut flat. It is a whole mega spruce (2 x 2 trunk, wide canopy) that grew in the "after" world because the pass had removed an orphan stub that blocked it; its crown sits at +7 or higher above the street. A "whole tree if cut" rule was implemented (log clusters labelled, leaves owned by their nearest cluster, a cell cut beside one marks the tree), but switched off (`FoliageClear.WHOLE_TREES = false`): removing a trunk after the fact leaves the dirt its feature put under it, in cells the pieces claim, and the order test (B2) failed 8 solid and 38 claimed-air cells with it. The count of trees still standing that the clear notched is reported instead (seed 1: 83, seed 31337: 35). To switch it on, the pass needs the claimed cells (PieceLedger) or the surface block to put back; see Known issues.
