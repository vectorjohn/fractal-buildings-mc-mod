# FACADE2 (7.12 follow-up): house 2x2 fronts and sill +1 upper floors

Branch `claude/p7-facade2`. Architect decisions 2 and 3 of the deep-flats report.

## What changed

- `FacadePass.pick` weights the 2x2 variant (bay 4, window 2) for houses: street front (mass 0, north,
  face 8+ wide) against the 1x2 variant, sides, backs and narrow faces 1:3 the other way. Medieval fronts
  stay 1:1: the settlement tier is not in `BuildingSpec`/`BuildContext`, so the "town tier and up" rule
  cannot be applied (fallback allowed by the brief).
- Deviation: front weight is 9:1 per variant, not the architect's 3:1. At 3:1 only 48-57 % of fronts had a
  2x2 (target 60 %): the ground door takes a bay, and bands alternate variants. A house front also lets
  stacked bands repeat the wide variant (pick without the p1/p2 exclusion).
- Tiling: with symmetric styles an odd face length can never contain a 4-bay (2+3+2), so for a house's wide
  variant with no 4-bay `layout` gives up one pier cell (at a drawn end) or two (one each end), and as a
  last resort tiles unevenly. `Layout.start` already supported the shift.
- DS39FIX: `tall` (sill 0, 3 tall) now needs `TALL_TYPES` (hall, highrise) as well as `tallGlass`. Shops and
  blocks above the shopfront take the style sill (+1) with the head at the ceiling (`headAtCeiling`).
- `Glazing.Stats` gained `windows2x2` and `north2x2` (1 if a 2x2 window faces local north); the report has a
  `2x2` column.

## Tests

- `GlazingTest.housesCarryTwoByTwoWindowsOnTheirFront`: plain, plains, medieval, jungle, 60 houses each:
  2x2 share of windows 15-30 %, and (not medieval) at least 60 % of houses carry a 2x2 on the front.
- `GlazingTest.floorToCeilingGlassOnlyOnHallsAndHighrisesInTallGlassStyles`: shops and blocks have 0 sill-0
  upper windows in plain, plains, medieval, tall; hall has at least 80 % in tall-glass styles and at most
  30 % in medieval (arcade arches are sill 0). Mutation: dropping `st.tallGlass()` from `tall` fails it.
  The high-rise curtain wall is its own program (every window sill 0 in every style); not asserted (7.13).

`city-check` gates (seed 1, `--scenes auto:2`): 9 sites, 0 failing. `check`: 0 violations, exit 0.
Blocks (17 %) and shop upper floors (21 %) are below DS-33's 25-40 %. The architect's fix (more 2x2, narrower
bays) does not raise glass share: a 2x2 in a bay of 4 and a 1x2 in a bay of 2 both fill half the bay width, and
weighting 2x2 3:1 on shops and blocks changed blocks 17 -> 16 % (tried, reverted). The share is set by
window/bay width (1/2) times 2 of 4 rows. Raising it needs a wider window per bay (2 wide in a bay of 3, or 3 wide
in a bay of 4), a style decision for 7.13/DS-33.

paired windows on fronts; blocks' upper floors now show a sill band under each window row.

## Round 2 (architect review)

- House street front: one variant for every band (2x2 when the inner length is 12+, else 1x2; medieval draws once), bays of exactly 4 with 0-3 spare cells as end piers; the uneven-tiling last resort is gone. Narrow fronts keep their 1x2 pairs.
- Shop and block upper floors in tallGlass styles (plain, plains, tall; also HR-A2's tall-brick/stone/terracotta/concrete variants by inheritance): `P (WW P) x n`, 2-wide windows in bays of 3 (`Win.piered`), same on every storey so they stack. Measured upper-floor glass (test lots, 10 seeds): shop 29 %, block 21-22.5 % (architect's proof of concept: 31 / 24; party and stair walls cost blocks). Check mix (all styles): block 18 % whole, shop 23 % (upper 24 %); city-check shops upper 31 %.
- `BlockFacade` rebuilt layouts without the window shape when moving a door (`new Layout(..., kinds)`); now `withKinds`.
- Goldens regenerated (v1 house, shop, block; floorplan s5). Tests: front 2x2 at 80 % of long fronts (plain/plains/jungle 100 % in the sample), upper glass runs at most 2 wide, shop >= 28 %, block >= 20 % upper.
- Not asserted: "no front row mixes 2x2 and 1x2": IR rows also hold other masses' and blocked windows, so a row test gave false positives; the variant is single per front by construction.
