# 2026-10-21 P5 R1: region field, tiers, `region` command

Ported S4's region code to `planner-core/.../core/region/` (re-implemented, not copied):
`RegionField` (integer site coordinates, exact integer squared distances, `StrictMath.sqrt` only in
`edgeDistance`), `Tiers` (weights, bands, density caps; log-uniform radii, Pareto tail for the
metropolis, `maxRadius` default 4096), `RegionPlanner` (S4's best-of-8 candidates, `Hash64` string
purposes `region/site-x|site-z|roll|tier|radius|cand`, grow policy only), `CellDecision`, `BiomeClass`
(table in resource `region/biome-classes.json`, namespaced ids), `RegionMap` (`fb-region-dump/1`
writer, with `layers`), `RegionProbe` (see below). CLI: `fractal-cli region --seed N --terrain SPEC
[--cell-size 1024] [--window X0,Z0,W,D] [--max-radius N] [--out FILE]`.

Verification (all run):
- `:planner-core:test :cli:test` pass (see report for counts). New tests: `RegionFieldTest` (3x3 search
  equals 7x7; seeds differing above bit 32 differ; `edgeDistance` equals ray-marched distance to the next
  cell), `TiersTest` (frequencies of 20,000 cells within 2 %, radius bands, Pareto median and clip share),
  `BiomeClassTest`, `RegionPlannerTest` (no centre on water or peak over 3 seeds x 625 cells, edge margin,
  all-water world rejects every cell, shuffled order, 1 vs 8 threads, settle rate on flat plains is
  `baseChance`, recorded crop replay, probe reads stay on the lattice and inside `probeReach`, golden
  `golden/region/{12345,987654321}.txt`), CLI `RegionCommandTest`.
- Region maps looked at: ran the CLI on the S4 spike's real 24576-block dumps of seeds 12345 and 987654321
  (384x384 lattice, 501 and 508 cells); rendered with a throwaway canvas script and opened the PNG.
  Settlements sit on land, none on sea; hamlets/villages are dots, towns, cities and metropolises (black)
  are circles clamped to half a cell. Settled share 30 % (149 of 501; rejected: 219 water, 128 roll, 3 peak,
  2 unsuitable). Settled tiers seed 12345: 59 hamlet, 51 village, 22 town, 11 city, 6 metropolis.
- Timing: 130 ms for 501 cells on the recorded lattice (0.26 ms/cell), loadavg 15 (busy machine, cold JIT).
  S6's ~11 ms per cell is the live Minecraft probe; the planner's own cost is negligible, and a decision
  reads at most about 8 x 81 lattice columns.
