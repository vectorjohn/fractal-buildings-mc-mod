# LOTRAIL: railed flights inside the lot, landings as wide as the door

Item LOTRAIL (DS-8 left by RAILFIX). John: "railings never keep you from falling".

## What changed

- `InstancePassV1.steps` now reports whether every landing and flight could be railed inside the lot (`Placed.guarded`,
  `placeChecked`). Pass 0 also tries each stoop landing with its lot-line cells cut off (`connected` keeps it one piece).
- `GeneratorV1.generate`: when the placement is not guarded, retry with the envelope set back: front +1; sides, back and
  front +1; front +2 and sides +1; all +2 (`SETBACKS`). The first guarded placement wins; none fits: the old placement stays
  (lot at the type minimum). Walls, curtains and bastions keep their margins. `SpecPolicyV1.spec(req, lim)` takes the margins.
- Wide doors (opening wider than one cell, ground below the door floor): landing candidates must cover the whole door front,
  or a strip exactly the door's width; the flight starts at the landing's end. Before, only the first cell had a flight.
- Tests: `RailingTest` lost the shop lot-line exemption and gained `presetBuildingsWithAOneCellMarginAreSetBack` (house-slope
  14x12 plane:64:0:1:1 seeds 0..7, shop-slope-deep 11x25, tower-medieval 13x13 noise, zero unguarded edges, invariants 0).
  New `LotRailTest`: 948 buildings (all non-wall types x test lots x 6 terrains x 6 seeds): no unguarded edge unless the lot
  has no room for a setback; every cell in front of a wide door is level or at most 1 below, or a landing tread.
- STANDARDS DS-8 amendment note and ROADMAP 6.9 note.

## Numbers (LotRailTest matrix, 948 buildings)

- 107 buildings (11%) set back (shrunk by 1..2 cells), 0 failed to fit by being rejected; 55 (5.8%: highrise 28, tower 15,
  house 12) stand on lots at the type minimum, have no room for a setback and keep a lot-line flight (known issue; fix: the
  settlement/briefs raise the minimum lot by 2 for sloped lots, or the type's minimum grows).
- 147 wide-door cases, 114 with a landing at door level, 0 with a drop in front of the door.
- Mutation: wide-door code off -> `wideDoorsOpen...` fails (workshop 12x12, drop 3). Before the change the preset test failed
  on shop-slope-deep seed 0 and house-slope seeds 3, 4, 6, 7.
- check --seeds 0..29 all types: 0 violations (about 10 min).
