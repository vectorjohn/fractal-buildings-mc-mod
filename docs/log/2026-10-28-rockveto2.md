# ROCKVETO2: replay the boulder descent

Follow-up to [ROCKVETO](2026-10-28-rockveto.md). Its box assumed the origin sinks at most 8 blocks. Vanilla `BlockBlobFeature.place` lowers it while `canPlaceOn` fails below, with no bound, from the MOTION_BLOCKING height: a mega spruce canopy or deep water starts it high, and it lands beside a street below the box.

## Decision
Replay the descent (`RockVeto.descend(origin, minY, predicate)`), then apply the box to the landed origin (`RockVeto.box`, now x/z -4..+2, y -4..+2, no descent allowance). The mixin passes `((BlockBlobFeature) this).canPlaceOn()` and the level, so the veto reads exactly the cells and predicate vanilla reads and gets vanilla's position. Rejected: a box extended to the probe's surface (a guess at depth; vetoes every boulder near a claim under a tall canopy; the probe is not what vanilla reads). Deterministic: a function of the plan, the origin and the blocks vanilla itself reads (the descent is exactly what the boulder will do in that order). If the descent ends at minY+3 vanilla places nothing and we do not veto.
