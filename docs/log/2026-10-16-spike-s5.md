# Spike S5 terrain adaptation (2026-10-16)

Question and answer in [docs/research/spikes/s5.md](../research/spikes/s5.md). Result: `beard_thin` with one rigid piece per building (box bottom at floor) suffices at building scale; `bury` and `encapsulate` bury surface buildings; flattening (S7) not needed for now.

- Code (throwaway, branch `claude/spike-s5`, not merged): `dev.fractalbuildings.spike.{S5Spike,S5Structure,S5Piece}`, 8 structure + structure_set JSONs, `S5ClientGameTest`.
- Verified: `:mod:runClientGameTest` passes (assertions on void/rejection counts), 32 screenshots read, 13 committed as 960x540 JPEGs with `metrics.csv`.
- Surprises: (1) client tests crashed with "GPU timeout attempting to acquire next frame" because SDL picked the human's Wayland session (`WAYLAND_DISPLAY` set); run with `env -u WAYLAND_DISPLAY SDL_VIDEODRIVER=x11`. (2) The gametest world is flat unless `setUseConsistentSettings(false)`. (3) `waitForChunksRender(true, n)` timed out for far teleports; polling `hasRenderedAllSections` after server-side pre-generation works. (4) `Beardifier` treats any non-pool piece as rigid with ground at `box.minY`, confirmed. (5) A `StructurePieceType` lambda registers fine with `Registry.register(BuiltInRegistries.STRUCTURE_PIECE, ...)`; `StructureType` is a one-method interface.
- Not covered: narrow river, interiors/doors on slopes, S7 comparison, more than one seed.
