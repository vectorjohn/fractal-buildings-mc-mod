# p5-wallwalk: curtain walls and a continuous wall walk

John's D2 note: walls should be walkable between segments. D2's verifier also found every ring lot was a full gatehouse.

## What changed
- `WallRing.pieces` types ring lots `wall` (gate lots, 23 long, only where a road crosses), `curtain` (free stretches) and `bastion` (the 4 corners; was `tower`).
- New types in `gen/v1/types/wall/`: `CurtainType` (one 5-thick gallery across the whole lot, flat roof = walk, crenellated parapet cut across the band at both lot ends, guard door on the town side), `BastionType` (12x11 tower, 3 or 4 storeys, stair core on the west side, doorways on the east and back walls at the first upper storey), `WalkOps` (shared: the band row, the doorway cells, `openEnds`, `endDoor`/`backDoor` with a step block inside).
- `WallType`: in-lot curtains use the same band (was random 3-5 thick, random z, which would not have lined up); a tower end on the lot edge gets a doorway onto the neighbour's walk.
- Convention that joins independently built lots: the walk gallery is centred on the lot's middle row; the doorway uses the two cells next to it, which every neighbour's 5-wide band covers whatever the lot depths (7 to 11) and rotation.
- Viewer plan colours for `curtain` and `bastion`.

## Verification
- `WallWalkTest` (new): voxelises the ring buildings of 5 flat walled towns (180 ring lots) and walks it block by block (level or one up/down, nothing below the first upper storey): from every corner tower every ring lot is reachable on the wall top; from the middle of every gate passage the walk top is reachable by the stairs. Mutation: removing the bastion back doorway fails it (23 lots unreachable).
- `V1PropertyTest` for wall, curtain, bastion: 0 failing at scale 1 and 10 (scale 10: one unrelated `shop` case of 16000 fails, entrance-reachable; not touched here). `InstancePassV1Test` ceiling test needed fallback massings for the tiny height cap.
- Whole `:planner-core:test` (after merging claude/run-phase5), `:cli:test`, viewer `npm test`: pass. `city-check --seeds 1..8 --terrain flat:64,noise:3:70:10:300 --sites random:6 --scenes auto:3`: 96 sites 0 failing; gate sites `--scenes auto:2`: 9 sites 0 failing; no `--ignore`.
- Goldens: `wall.txt` regenerated, `curtain.txt` and `bastion.txt` new; no other type changed.
- Looked at `viewer/out/city/town-walled/` (copied to `docs/review/phase5/wallwalk/`): long crenellated walls between corner towers, walk visible from above and from the street view; gates at the roads only.

## Decisions and limits
- Bastion `maxGrade` 4: with 3 the V1 matrix hits recorded 45 degree slopes where a full-lot tower's front door is 10 blocks above the ground and no flight fits; with 4 the planner drops such a corner lot (`empty:no-fit`) and the matrix avoids recorded terrain.
- Sloping ground: every lot keeps its own base level, so walks of neighbouring lots differ (hilly synthetic towns: 118 of 581 adjacent pairs by more than a block). Not fixed; needs a stepped walk with ramps (follow-up). Flat towns are exact.
- Access to the walk is through the towers (stairs inside, a one-block step up at the doorway); curtain galleries have only a guard door.
