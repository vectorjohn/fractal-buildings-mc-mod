# P6 B3: terrain in a real world (6.3)

Plan: [phase6.md](../plans/phase6.md) step B3 (decision 10 respected: no per-wing ground levels). Needs B1.

## What was done

- **Measured first.** `TerrainClientGameTest` (client, normal worlds, seeds 987654321, 12345, 1): finds the nearest village+ settlement with the world's planner, scans its tiles with 3+ buildings by the live exact probe, generates the steepest and the wettest one (3.5 to 6 s each under load) and reads blocks back: void under each ground-floor column, wall exposure and burial on a ring round each lot, road/paving/deck cells with nothing under them, solid non-leaf blocks within 3 above a road, steps between neighbouring road columns (the tile border included), deepest cut. Two screenshots per seed (deepest cut, road next to water), copied to `docs/review/phase6/terrain/`.
- **First run found** (before any fix): voids under floors 1 to 6 % of columns (up to 97 columns), 1 to 284 road cells over air, headroom failures on every slope tile (58 to 248 columns), all from the same cause: the live ground near a building is the beardifier's, 1 to 4 blocks off the probe the planner graded against.
- **Core fixes (planner-core):**
  - `Grading.surfaceEarthAndAir`: every road and plaza column gets earth from `FILL_BELOW` = 12 under the lower of probed ground and surface up to the surface block, and air from the surface up to the probed ground or `HEADROOM` = 4 above it (was: fill from probed ground only when the road is above it, cut only when below). This is the plan's "extend fill boxes down" and "cut clears up to frontY + 4", applied to every column so tiles stay independent.
  - `DistrictScenes.ground` orders boxes in phases (air, earth, bridge parts, surfaces): the first version (earth, air, ...) let one street's trench cut away the earth under a street crossing it at another level (62 floating road cells on seed 1; 2959 of 390k surfaces in `GroundWalkTest`'s model); air-first fixes it (0).
  - `Pieces.deepen`: the cells rastered into a world get a `FOUNDATION` run under every footprint column down to 12 below the probed ground (and below the planned foundation). Only the raster path does it: `BuildingPiece.building()`, the IR, the viewer, the CLI and the v1 goldens are unchanged. (A first version deepened the piece's building itself; it broke `WorldPlannerParityTest` (pieces equal the CLI scene) and the `no-gap-below` invariant, both intended contracts, so it moved to the raster.) Cheap: underground only.
- **Goldens:** none changed (full `:planner-core:test` green after the change; ground boxes and the world-only foundations are not in the pinned digests). Pinning tests that described the old shape of the boxes were updated: `TerrainToolkitTest` (flat road is 3 boxes, fills and cuts sized by the new rule, plaza columns).

## The known issues

1. **Dirt overhang over a street on a slope: fixed** (headroom 4 over every surface; metric 58 to 248 columns down to 0; the village street shot shows none). *Cause:* the beardifier raises the ground 1 to 2 blocks over the street the probe saw as open, and the old cut only cleared up to the probed ground.
2. **Piece boxes clipped to the tile drop foundations: not real.** A piece's cells are inside its box by construction (`Pieces.box` = lot rectangle plus shell footprint; Y is never clipped by `postProcess`), and the lot is inside the tile. `BuildingCellExtentTest` checks every cell of every building of the fixture town and of sampled tiles of all 9 gate windows: 0 cells outside the piece box, 0 outside the tile.
3. **White patch in `district-overview.png`: real snow.** The village (seed 987654321) is just below the snowline of a `snowy_slopes` hill; the surface histogram around the tile has 249 powder snow, 599 snow and 11 snow block columns, first whitish block `snow` at 332,133,730 in biome `snowy_slopes`. Not an artefact, nothing to fix.

## Findings and deviations

- **Street junctions are not reconciled (plan-level, not terrain).** Where a street meets or ends at another street at a different level the surfaces differ by 2 to 20 blocks (e.g. a street ending at 61 against a street at 81). `GroundWalkTest` on the 9 gate windows: 670 tiles, 370 with a step over 1, 229 over 3; same before and after B3. The plan's "at most one block step between neighbouring road columns" cannot hold until the street network gives each junction one height; recorded in the polish backlog, the in-game test asserts only steps across a tile border (0) and `GroundWalkTest.junctionStepsDoNotGetWorse` ratchets the count. This is why 6.3 is ticked with a known issue and not blocked.
- Bridge decks are beams between piers every 8th column: the "floating" metric accepts a deck within 6 columns of a pier or with water or solid within 8 below. One deck on seed 1 is 14 blocks over its water (deck = highest surface of the whole wet run + 3): looks high, not broken.
- No quay-specific block types were found in the world (quays are `PAVING` ground boxes with the same earth rule); the shore screenshots show a stepped bridge approach and a frozen shore road with no gaps.
- B1's `DistrictClientGameTest` choice of tiles is unchanged; seed 987654321 offers only one candidate tile (2 planned tiles with buildings), so "slope" and "shore" are the same tile there and only one shot is kept.
- Final runs on the merged branch: `scripts/test-all.sh --no-client` and the client test (see the report).
