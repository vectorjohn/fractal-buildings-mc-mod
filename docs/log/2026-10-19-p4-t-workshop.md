# 4.6 T-workshop: the workshop type

Branch `claude/p4-t-workshop`, package `gen/v1/types/workshop/` (`WorkshopType`, `WorkshopMassing`). Registered with one line in `Types.java`.

- **Massing** (`MassingOps.wing`, `MassingCheck`): an open hall (`Mass.open`, one room, 5 or 6 high, less under a low ceiling) at the front edge of the envelope, 8-16 wide, 6-10 deep; a living wing of 1 or 2 storeys behind (50 %) or beside it (49 %), with its own core when it has 2 storeys (tried first, backs off to 1 storey, then other plan, then hall + lean-to alone); optional 1-storey `lean-to` mass (kind `lean-to`) on the free side. Front margin 2 (steps), side 1, back 1; lots 12x12..24x20 (square-ish, `TypeLimits.oriented` from the shop step not used).
- **Facade program** wraps `FacadePass.layout/entrance/decorate` (Fa API): the hall's front is rebuilt as window bays, a 4 or 5 wide door bay and window bays, with a 2-wide door (`FacadePlan.Entrance` width 2) in the middle.
- **RoomProgram**: the hall's use is `workshop` with 2-5 `workstation` hooks (by area), a `hearth` (forge), a chest and a light; the wing uses the default program (beds upstairs).
- Everything after massing (structure, roofs, interior, openings) is the shared pass.
