# P7 HR-B2: tower plate templates

Branch `claude/p7-hr-b2`. Plan: [highrises.md](../plans/highrises.md) step B2.

## What

- `interior/FloorPlan` and its `Leaf`, `Cut`, `Door` records (constructor, accessors, `contains`, `alongZ`, `cellX/Z`) are public. Nothing else in `interior/` changed.
- New `types/highrise/TowerPlate`: `plan(Spec, Draws)` returns `Result(FloorPlan, uses[], stairwell, corridor)` or `null` (caller falls back to the guillotine planner). Templates CORRIDOR, GRAND (`lobby`/`open`), BANQUET, STAIRHOUSE. Plate frame: x along the hall, z from the street side; band B (street), hall rows `[hallZ0, hallZ1)`, band A (core side) with the core block; stairwell and shaft rects are inputs. Not wired into `InteriorPass` (B3a).
- CORRIDOR: units per band via `segment` (even spacing, each cut moved to the nearest legal x within +-2, unit width 4+, `fits` keeps units splittable into rooms), entry strip table (DS-11, strip row slid +-1 so it never ends on a side window, one depth per band segment), rooms side by side (aspect <= 2, widths from the unit, cuts slid to window-free facade cells), 1/2/3 rooms (studio, 1-bed, 2-bed; offices), hall door one cell in at a plate end else mid-unit, back rooms behind the block split at its middle when both sides have a unit, door into the flat/office on their side. `entryStrip=false` uses the same path with strip depth 0.
- GRAND: band B + hall = one room; offices/storage beside the block (lobby: office on the side nearer `entranceX`), storage behind, cut in depth when deeper than 1.5 x width (front keeps the use and the door, storage behind it), cut in width when wider than 2 x depth.
- BANQUET: hall rows = `corridor` foyer, banquet room = band B joined by a 2-3 wide arch (`Door.arch`); stairwell door onto the foyer; kitchen on the side whose plate end is farther from the stairwell door.
- `TowerPlate.dump(Result)`: ASCII printer (F flat, B bedroom, O office, e entry, s storage, S stairwell, K kitchen, Q banquet, L/o lobby/open, P plant, '.' corridor, '#' partition or shaft, '+' door, ':' arch).

## Verification

`TowerPlateTest` (synthetic plates: w 13-40, d 12-40, stairwell depth 3/4, block at legal positions incl. flush with a plate edge, windows every 2-4 along the boundary, unit targets 6/10/14, strip on/off, flat/office): every cell is a room, cut or shaft; leaves min side 3 (corridor/entry 2); flat/bedroom/office/kitchen aspect <= 2; window cell in every flat/bedroom/office/lobby/banquet/open; other leaves touch the boundary; the stairwell leaf equals its rect with exactly one door at the rect's middle onto the corridor (or the grand room), never the banquet; everything reachable without going through a bedroom; entry strips open onto the corridor; dead end <= 4 (the shaft doorway counts); banquet joined only by a 2-3 wide arch; same spec and seed give equal plans. Fallback to the guillotine planner: CORRIDOR 14/400, GRAND 0/400, BANQUET 0/400. Mutations: stairwell door at the rect's end -> 386 plans fail; depth cut off -> `openFloorBackOfHouseKeepsItsOffices` fails.

## Decisions and surprises

- Offices/bedrooms/kitchens whose shape (aspect > 2) or window run fails after the cuts are demoted to `storage`; a flat that fails returns null. Band A strips of depth 3-4 (plate d = 12) can't hold a legal flat (3-deep rooms need 4-6 or 9-12 wide): their units become one `storage` room (hall door), they don't force the fallback.
- The planner needs the block's walls to end on piers (no window cell at a wall end on a facade, including the stair/shaft partition at the back wall and the block's back wall where it meets a flush side). It returns null if a partition would end on a window; B1/B3a must place the core block (and the facade windows) so this holds. The test plates clear those cells.
- Side strips of 1-2 cells next to the block, or 1-2 deep rows behind it, return null (like the plan's "too shallow").
- `TowerPlate.WHY` (thread-local, package-private) says where the last `plan` gave up; a test aid only.
- Open question (John): deep flats get the private `entry` strip (default, `Spec.entryStrip`); the alternative (fewer, wider units) is the same code with the flag off.

## Round 2 (interior-designer and verifier findings)

- Only the piece of a long back-of-house segment beside the core (banquet kitchen) or nearest the entrance (lobby office) keeps its use; the others are `storage` (open floors keep offices everywhere). Two kitchens: 74/400 -> 0; two lobby offices: 25/188 -> 0.
- Entry strip capped at 3 deep, none on office floors. Strips deeper than 3: 0/538.
- Grand rooms longer than 3:1 (banquet) or 2.5:1 (lobby, open) get anterooms cut off the ends (`open`; banquet right end `storage` cloakroom), joined by 2-3 wide arches; the main room keeps the stairwell door and, if possible, the entrance. Over 2.5:1: 0/469.
- STAIRHOUSE takes the shaft on either side. `TowerPlate.WHY` only fills under `TowerPlate.DEBUG` (tests set it). `Result` gained `demoted`; `corridorDemotionsAreRare` bounds demotions at 5 %. Back rooms wider than 2 x depth get their far part cut off as a store room; store rooms are cut to at most 2 x depth wide.
