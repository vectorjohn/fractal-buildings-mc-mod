# 6.12 STEPTOP: exterior flights end in a one-block jump

Branch `claude/p6-steptop` (from `claude/p7-shoplow-s2`).

## What
- `planner-core/.../raster/Shapes.java` `stair`: the walk models count every `STAIR` cell as a half step, but the shape rules turned an exterior step with nothing higher beside it into a full block. Two new cases before that fallback: a `DOOR` one up beside the step (the old rule also demanded a `WALL` under the door, which a ground-floor door does not have, it has the slab), and a landing cell with a lower `STAIR` beside it (faces on, away from the step it was entered from). The rest of a stoop platform (cells not next to a lower step) stays a full block; from a half-step top that is a half-block rise.
- New `RasterEntranceWalkTest` (settlement package, uses `RasterOwnershipTest.scenes/rasterScene`, now also remembers each scene's terrain probe in `RasterOwnershipTest.probes`): a breadth-first walk over the rastered cell shapes (stair shape 2y+1, other solid 2y+2, unrastered terrain a full block under `floorY`, two free cells above, doors block) from every terrain column and ground-owned cell to the cell outside each exterior ground-storey door. Rise per move at most 2 half blocks (a jump), drop at most 2. Also counts full-block `STAIR` cells directly over a lower stair-shaped cell of the same building.

## Numbers (gate windows GATES[0] and [2], 3 districts each, exterior and interior scenes)
- Before: 215 doors, 60 unreachable, 258 full-block steps over a lower stair.
- After: 215 doors, 0 unreachable, 0 full-block steps over a stair.
- Mutation: reverting `Shapes.java` makes the new test fail (numbers above).

## Notes
- First version of the walk used the strict half-step rise from the terrain and flagged ~180 doors with no step at all (door a block above the terrain with no stair). Those are allowed by `StairGround` (drop a block) and a player can jump one block, so the test allows a 1 block rise per move; the bug is the 1.5 block rise.
- Did not remove `RasterOwnershipTest`'s allowance for a full block with no higher step: those are the stoop platform cells (hundreds), correct by design; the jump is checked by the new test instead.
- The viewer reads IR, which this change does not touch: galleries are unchanged. In-game look: see the report.
