# 7.13 B3b: facade bands, roof doors, roof guard (2026-10-26)

Branch `claude/p7-hr-b3b`, plan step B3b of [plans/highrises.md](../plans/highrises.md).

## What
- `FacadePass`: `BandFn` plus `run(c, bands, edit)` / `layout(c, bands)`; the default (`band(m, s, bandSize)`) is unchanged, so other types' goldens only moved where noted below.
- `HighriseFacade` (new): bands from `TowerProgram.band`, now one band per tier (the top storey of a tier no longer draws its own bays: it made 1 pair in 23 differ); lobby and special storeys keep the tier's columns, windows 4 tall (lobby from sill 1 to the slab). `TowerPiers.apply` still runs after it; it now also blanks every bay in front of the stairwell (its flights alternate sides, windows showed on every other storey: floor-repeat 31 -> 1 per case).
- SETBACKSILL, general: `OpeningPass.windowRoom` takes a ROOF block outside the first window row (the lower deck stands at the standing level of the storey above), so sill-0 windows are cut above any setback; B3a's `raiseSill` workaround in `TowerPiers` is gone. Halls (the only other tall-glass type) moved in their goldens.
- `TowerRoof` (new): `guard` = 1-high parapet wall on every top-tier edge cell (gaps beside the stair house filled) with a rail on it, unbroken bar the stair house footprint; `doors` = two doors on the stair house's north face (stairwell at the other storeys' door column, machine room at the shaft's middle). The stair house floor is one below the deck's standing level, so each door starts one up over a 1-block FLOOR threshold inside (see NOTES for why not a carved doorstep).
- `TowerPlate.stairhouse`: no door between stairwell and machine room (B3a's interim); `TowerPlateTest` updated. `HighriseType.roofs()/openings()/facade()` wired.
- Facade key `bayPiers` (default off, mass-timber on): beam columns at every bay line, no slab-line rails (`FacadePass.timber(..., slabLine=false)`).

- Rail ring built in code (no `parapet 2` fallback needed).
- Not done: bay-lines for 3-bays with piered 2-wide windows (would reach ~50 % glass) left to B4.

## Round 2 (verifier and architect findings)
- Roof-door threshold is now a `STAIR` block (not a full block) rising toward the door; `Shapes.stair` turns a stair beside a raised DOOR over a WALL toward it, and `RasterOwnershipTest` accepts a door as the "higher" neighbour.
- Mass timber: `stringCourse` false; `bayPiers` now also draws the flush stripped-wood slab edge (beam) on every storey; projecting bands stay only at mass tops (cornice: podium top, setbacks, crown).
- room-window warnings 6 -> 43 of 42240 came from banquet/grand floors: a side anteroom (`open`) could land on a facade bay that is blank (bands are now fixed per tier). `TowerPlate.grand` drops an anteroom with no window cell. Now 0 of 42240.

- Halls above a lower roof deck now get sill-0 windows (OpeningPass change); hall gallery re-shot (hall-plain, hall-big, hall-flat in viewer/out/gallery/): no problem seen over the pitched lower roofs, where the window just sits above the roof surface.
