# DECKDIAG F3 (shore-aware reroute attempt) and the step 4 recount

2026-11-02, branch `claude/p6-deckdiag-f3`, step 3 and step 4 of [the plan](../plans/deckdiag.md).

## What changed

- `WorldPlanner.rerouteApproach(.., shore)`: with `shore` the lattice search uses the link router's water rule (ADR 0004 WR1): a node is wet at coarse depth >= 1 (or a water biome), no diagonal cuts a wet corner, +200 for a node with a wet neighbour. The old signature delegates with `false`.
- `WorldPlanner.routeNodes`: after the plain attempts, when the best attempt still has a run over the tier limit or along water (`longRuns + alongRuns > 0`), one shore reroute from the original first corner to the last, no banned nodes. Taken when its score is strictly lower **and** it passes `shoreDetourOk`: at most 1.5x the length of the route it replaces and no turn over 90 degrees. (The 1.5x/90 rule is the architect's detour-limit proposal, which John has not decided; here it only guards the F3 attempt. The existing plain reroutes are untouched.) Lattice reads only; order independent.
- Tests: `DeckAlongTest.approachNodesKeepCrossingsAndDropShoreDecks` (sm11_m2 `km11_m2_0` is now kept and `deadEndPorts` is empty; before F3 it was dropped), `DeckAlongTest.shoreDetourLimit`, new `ShoreRerouteTest` (synthetic plane, 1-deep lake strip on lattice row z=32 beside the z=0 line: plain reroute keeps the line, shore reroute moves 32 blocks off and passes the detour limit).
- Probes (opt-in, `-Dfb.census=1` / `-Dfb.planpng=OUT.png`): `DeckCensusProbe`, `DeckPlanPng`.
- Determinism lint caught `Math.hypot` in my first version; now `Math.sqrt`.
