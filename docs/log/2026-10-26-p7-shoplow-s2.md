# 7.17 SHOPLOW S2: dig in (slab, cut, corridor, forecourt), run-aware flights, `door-rise`

Branch `claude/p7-shoplow-s2` (from S3, merged with run-phase6 incl. S1 and S0 core). Plan: [plans/shoplow.md](../plans/shoplow.md) S2, plus the DS-45 amendment (no straight run over 3 risers on any lot, so the plan's S6 search is here) and the architect's answers A2, A4, A5, A7.

## What changed
- `gen/v1/SiteLevel` (new): for shop, house, workshop on their ground storey the slab is `min(oy0, max(G, M - 5))` (G the street at the front doors, M the highest ground over footprint, one-cell ring, volume columns, approach; cut at most 4 above the standing level). Site boxes: `AIR` over every shell-free cell of the ring and under overhangs from one below the slab to the ground, `PAVING` as the well floor (first free block = `oy`, door one step above, as `CutFixture` drew it), a corridor cut out to the lot edge when earth stands against the door, a forecourt of `GROUND` (a causeway of `FOUNDATION`, railed, from fill 2) when the street is level with it, exit cells at the lot edge (a stair block on one side, a cell a block lower on the other) because the street can be a block off the brief's level. Water, wall walks, stilts (market halls) keep the old rule. `-Dfb.legacyslab=true` / `SiteLevel.LEGACY` gives the whole old behaviour.
- `InstancePassV1`: site built per round with the door guard reading `SiteGround`; `steps()` reads `SiteGround` (standing test `DigInTest.aBuriedDoor...`: reading raw ground fails it); `searchRuns` replaces the flight search: breadth first over (cell, descent, half blocks since the last level move), level cells as landings and corners, no route beside itself without a rail, falls back to the old shortest flight per rail pass; stoop at rise 4 for houses and workshops (2 for shops, as before). `SiteGround`: a `FOUNDATION` box may carry a paved stack.
- Checks (`check/DoorChecks`, on S0 core's `DoorRise`): `door-rise` invariant (shop, workshop 2, house 5; fails only on dry lots within the planner's limit, else a design warning), `flight-landings` design warning (a run over 3), both listed by `check` and `city-check` (`DistrictScenes.Built.warnings`). `DoorRise.measure` reads the ground through `SiteGround`.
- Lot planner: houses at most 9 above the street and 4 in the front rows (`Briefs.HOUSE_FRONT_RISE`, `HOUSE_FRONT_ROW`); `Briefs.withStreetFront` lays the street's own level over the row outside each lot's front, so a door near the end of a wide front on a stepped street sees its street (before that 2 of 2400 buildings had no way down). S3 gap: `BankLotTest` workshop front rise.

## Decisions and surprises
- The plan's "paving at s - 1" was moved one lower; corridors, forecourt and the exit cells exist because the brief's `frontY` is one number per lot and the street steps along a wide front; `Briefs.withStreetFront` removes most of that, the exits cover the rest (street from a block below to a block above the floor).
- Not built: A4 wall torch (no light role in IR, mod or styles); A4 "cap the cut at 1 where the top edge is a street" only for houses (shops and workshops have no rise to spare); the architect's A5 fallback is in the code and counted (`flight-landings` warnings), though DS-45 says it is not the rule.
- Owed: heavy layers (order test, client-full, perf-planner), not run (John's machine window).

- Viewer: `terrainTops` now compares the exclusive top (`y1 + 1 >= floorY`) like `SiteGround`, so S2's air boxes (ending at floorY - 1) cut the terrain; new `mesh.test.ts` case fails on the old test. Review pictures regenerated.
- Eave: a shell block over a cut ring cell at the hill surface closed the well into a covered trench (houses, 9 %). `SiteLevel.level` now stands the slab up to the height where the earth's top block clears the eave (houses up to rise 5, others not); `DigInTest.noEaveClosesTheWell` (0 of 4884 ring columns; mutation fails). 45-degree lots (beyond the planner limit) can still have it. Houses at rise 4+ 41 -> 48 of 3525 (the cost).
- Exit cells: the lowered 1x1 pocket is gone (both lateral cells are stair blocks); where the approach is 2+ cells deep the door's outside cell is a stair block (the step below the door), `searchRuns` treats it as the standing place. Workshop corridors are as wide as the door (the approach is door-wide).
- Checks: water exemption of `door-rise` narrowed (wet column above the street); the rise stays measured at the door's own street column (DS-45 wording, S0 core), the planner uses `frontY`: documented; `DigInTest.doorRiseCanFailWithinTheContractAndOnlyThere` (legacy shop on 1:9 must fail, a 45-degree house only warns); a warning for houses at rise 3+ with no built approach (architect's count).
