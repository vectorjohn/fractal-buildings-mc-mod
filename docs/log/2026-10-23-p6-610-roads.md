# 6.10 Roads between settlements, planner half (p6-610-roads)

Date: 2026-10-23. Branch `claude/p6-610-roads`, base `claude/run-phase6`. Spec: [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-roads-between-settlements.md) (accepted), [plan](../plans/intercity-roads.md), spike notes [S10 planner](../research/spikes/s10-intercity-roads.md) and [S10 mod](../research/spikes/s10-mod.md). The mod half (per-chunk feature, terraced cuts) is C1, not here. 6.10 is not ticked.

# What was built

- **`core/road/`**
  - `RoadNet`: nodes by claims (RC), edge-gap graph with routable witnesses (N1) and touching pairs sharing a meeting point (N2), ports on rim + 64 with up to 4 hashed slides (N3), lattice A* with wet nodes, other footprints + 32 and own discs forbidden and a penalty beside wet nodes, string-pull, Catmull-Rom, reroute at most 3 times (N4), per-chunk query (RD1 to RD4).
  - `Curve` (stations every 8 blocks of arc length), `Profile` (one exact read per station, WR1 dry check, WR2 crossings measured exactly, merged when closer than 32 blocks, longest 20, flat deck at the higher bank, deck = mean over +-8 stations with at most 2 blocks of change per station), `RoadJson` (the region layer's `roads` object), `RoadPort`.
- **Port tag (N6)**: `CellDecision.port`, set by `RegionPlanner.decide` for settled towns and larger with large water (a water sample with a water neighbour on the 192-lattice) within 384 of the centre. No village bias, no tier nudge, nothing else in a decision changes.
- **SK (N5)**: `RegionView.ports(site)` (empty by default; `FieldSiteView` with a `RoadNet` answers), `LinkExits` (the member cell nearest the rim point serves each port: a required exit on its urban edge toward the port, then a dry approach), `CellSkeleton.approaches` and `SettlementPlan.approaches` (JSON `approaches`, written only when non-empty). Skeleton changes are additive: exits first, hashed exits within 3 lattice steps of a required one dropped, the window widened to hold the ports; with no ports the skeleton is byte-identical (tested).
- **CLI**: `fractal-cli roads` (region dump with `roads` layer plus the report below), `city --cell ... --roads`.
- **Viewer**: road layer in `region.html` (links, crossings, meeting points, dropped pairs, port rings, `&roads=0`), `links` layer in `plan.html`; fixtures `viewer/fixtures/region/roads-*.json.gz`, `viewer/fixtures/settlement/roads/`.

- **Linked share.** 68 to 70 %: below the probe's 76 to 80 % estimate. The dumps turn every wet 64-lattice sample into a pond, and the 36-block shore margin plus the 20-block crossing limit cut what a dry road may do; no bar (WR4). Unlinked settlements with a routable neighbour: 0 (the N1 guarantee holds, tested). What moved the share, in order (12345): first strict version 46 %, wet nodes at depth >= 1 +3 (stations read depth >= 1, so a node dry on the coarse rule started wet), the diagonal rule and the 36-block shore margin +3 together, dropping a grade ban from the search +6 (the station deck rule limits grade), chords not entering a banned lattice cell +10 (the string-pull had cut straight back through it).
- **Port tag**: 34 of 47 (12345) and 31 of 53 (987654321) settled towns, cities and metropolises on these wet dumps; none of the 150 hamlets and villages.
- **Read cap**: a cold chunk next to the r1340 and r1769 cities: own scan 6.6 to 7.1 km from the chunk (bound 7.3 km, `RoadNet.chunkReadBound`), 160 to 220 cells touched in all. S10's log read 14.5 to 18 km and grew with the largest radius.
- Wet road columns off a crossing: 0.65 to 0.73 % (shore columns under the road width, WR3).
