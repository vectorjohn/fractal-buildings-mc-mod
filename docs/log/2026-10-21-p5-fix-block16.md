# p5-fix-block16: block entrance-reachable at the grade limit

Failure: `V1PropertyTest` at `-Dfb.propertyScale=6`, block case 358 (seed 89, lot 100x80 at 31,-51, front south, `plane:78:1:1:16`): `entrance-reachable` for door o209.

Cause: not the policy. `SpecPolicyV1.reliefOk` measures the real relief over the lot (max minus min floor), so the diagonal fall was already counted (relief 10 <= 12 allowed). The generator failed: the block floor sits at the footprint's highest ground, the west-side door was 8 blocks above the terrain, and `InstancePassV1.steps` routes each door's flight by BFS through the 5-wide margin while earlier doors' flights are blocked cells. Neighbouring flights ran straight to the lot edge and walled the strip off, so the later door had no route.

Fix (InstancePassV1.steps, generic, not block-specific): if the BFS finds no flight of its own, a second pass lets the flight end by stepping onto an earlier flight's cell whose top is one block down or level (checker allows +-1 steps). Pass 1 is unchanged, so every existing output and golden is byte-identical; only previously failing doors change. The accepted-lot rule is unchanged (no ASSUMPTIONS row).
