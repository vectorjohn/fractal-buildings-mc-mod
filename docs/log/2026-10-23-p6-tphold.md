# 6.7 TPHOLD: hold far teleports until the destination is generated

Spec: [teleport-loading-screen.md](../research/worldgen/teleport-loading-screen.md), option 1 (server-only).

- `mixin/ServerPlayerTeleportMixin` (registered in `fractalbuildings.mixins.json`, B7's entries kept) injects at the head of `ServerPlayer.teleport(TeleportTransition)` and calls `teleport/TeleportHold.intercept`.
- `TeleportHold`: held only if same level, jump at least max(64, (view distance + 1) * 16) blocks, and the destination chunk with its 8 neighbours are not all loaded. Then per player: `RegionWarmer.warmPoint` (new public API: warm the region and road networks at a point on a worker, then prefetch the tile; a future that always completes) -> `addTicketAndLoadWithRadius` with our own `TicketType` (`fractalbuildings:teleport_hold`, loading flag, 60 s safety expiry, removed by hand) -> when the future is done, replay the teleport (a thread-local bypasses the mixin) -> remove the ticket. Polled once per server tick, nothing blocks. Action bar "Generating terrain..." every second; timeout 30 s wall clock then teleport anyway. A newer teleport replaces a waiting one; a player who leaves is dropped and the ticket released.
- Off: `-Dfractalbuildings.teleportHold=false`. Not in `fractalbuildings.json`, since that file holds per-world settings.
- Deviation from the brief: the timeout is wall clock, not ticks (see below). The ticket and chunk radius is 1, not the player's view distance, as vanilla does for the player's own chunk.

## Verification
- `TeleportHoldGameTest` (server, 4 tests, registered): long jump to a cold chunk (41000, 41000): player stays put, replay happens with all 9 chunks FULL, not timed out, arrives; timeout path (never-completing load, 300 ms) teleports anyway exactly once; a 20 block hop is not held; disabled flag means no hold. Full `:mod:runGameTest`: 31 of 31 passed.
- Mutation: making `intercept` always return false failed the long-jump and timeout tests.
- First attempt used a tick timeout: the game-test server ran 1623 ticks behind and 600 ticks elapsed in a blink while chunks were still generating. Hence wall clock.
- Client run: see the end of this file.
- Client game test `TeleportHoldClientGameTest` (village of seed 987654321, `/tp` far, asserts the player stays put until the hold ends, then screenshot `teleport-hold-arrived`) is written and registered, but **not run**: the heavy lock queue (4 other worktrees) did not free up within two 1-hour waits. First attempt also showed that a merge that leaves `<<<<` in `fabric.mod.json` breaks every client test (fixed). Run `scripts/client-test.sh` and look at `mod/build/screenshots/teleport-hold-arrived.png` and the `[TPHOLD]` log line.

## Round 2 (verifier findings)
- The destination is now `PositionMoveRotation.calculateAbsolute(of(player), of(transition), relatives).position()`, used for distance, chunk and warm point. Tests: `relativeShortHopIsNotHeld` (player at 30000, `~ ~5 ~`), `relativeLongJumpIsHeldOnTheRightChunks` (`~10000 ~ ~`, held destination equals the resolved one). Mutation (back to `t.position()`) failed both.
- Any other teleport of a held player (portal included) drops the waiting hold first; a hold is also dropped when the player is dead or dying, has left, or changed level. The ticket is removed on the level it was taken on. Tests: `newerTeleportReplacesTheWaitingOne`, `deadPlayerDropsTheHold`.
- Server tests: 35/35. Real-chunk waits in parallel with the heavy worldgen tests starve (chunks of 3 holds never finished in 2 wall minutes), so only `longJumpWaitsForFullChunks` uses real loading; the others gate on a fake load future.
- *Known issues:* a held teleport returns the player itself from `ServerPlayer.teleport`, so callers see the old position; a relative teleport replays relative to where the player is at release (a player who walks while waiting lands offset from the warmed chunks). One run of `worldgen_game_test_fixture_town_places_identically_in_any_chunk_order` failed once with "world blocks differ in another chunk order" on the merged base and passed on three reruns: a possible flake, not from this item (held chunks are far from that fixture).
- Client run (`scripts/client-test.sh`, exit 0): `/tp` from the spawn to the cold village at -576,704 (seed 987654321); the player stayed put for 14.5 s wall (loaded machine, lavapipe), then arrived; `teleport-hold-arrived.png` shows terrain, roofs and trees, no void. Chunk rendering hit the test's own timeout (logged, screenshot still taken).
