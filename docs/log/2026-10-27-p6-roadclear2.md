# 6.17 ROADCLEAR follow-ups: STREETSNOW and TRUNKDIRT

Branch `claude/p6-roadclear2`.

## What
- New `mod/.../worldgen/StreetAirFix` (called from `RoadFeature.place` right after `BuildingAirClear`, so in every overworld chunk after its own features; reach 12 blocks like `BuildingAirClear`). For each planned `Role.AIR` box of the ground pieces round the chunk:
  - STREETSNOW: a `snow` layer on the cell just above the box (`maxY`) with air under it is removed (Grading CUT ends at the probed ground's first free block, where freeze_top_layer laid the snow). Cells a building may own (`SpillClear.lotMayOwn`) or a later box overrides are skipped.
  - TRUNKDIRT: a log in a cell the plan gives as air is removed; dirt/podzol/coarse dirt under it is replaced by the planned block (one-cell `planner.raster` of the piece, `BlockMapper.state`); then `FoliageClear.run` with the cuts takes the rest of the tree. `SpillClear` kept logs alone only because it could not restore that dirt.
- Order independence: decisions use the plan, the raster of the piece and the block in the cell only; the region around each chunk is covered by a pass that runs after the chunk's own features.
- Test: `FoliageClientGameTest` (street part) counts hanging snow, logs in street air and dirt under them; problem when >0 with the fix on; the control pass (BEFORE) turns `StreetAirFix.ENABLED` off.
