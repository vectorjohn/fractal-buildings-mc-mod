# 6.9 Railings (2026-10-22)

**What.** New IR role `rail` (`Role.RAIL`, appended after `DECK`, solid like `fence`). `InstancePassV1.steps` records every
step and landing cell it places (tread level, minimum drop) and, after all doors are done, `rails(...)` adds for each
free cell beside a tread where the ground is at least 2 blocks below a flight step (1 below a landing) a kerb of
foundation up to the tread level and a rail block on it. A rail is therefore one block above the tread, on its own cell.
Cells are skipped when they are part of any flight, landing, door approach or the building footprint (`blocked`), outside
the lot, or occupied in the voxel grid. So rails stand on open sides only, never across the line of travel, and never
where a walker could step off sideways (that needs ground within 1 block of the tread). Where several treads touch a cell the highest decides.
Flights, landings, lookouts (their way up is the same site pass), shop stoops and hall steps are covered; nothing else to rail
exists yet: there are no balconies, flat parapets already use `fence` (left alone) and wall walks are not railed (not trivial).

**Palette.** `rail` entry in palette and colours of every style: medieval dark_oak_fence, plain and plains oak_fence,
desert sandstone_wall (added to `DesertStyleTest.VANILLA`; id is in the 26.3 block list), jungle jungle_fence, snowy and taiga spruce_fence.
Viewer: `ROLES` and `ROLE_COLOURS` have `rail` (drawn as a full cube in the style colour).

**Older readers.** Both the core `IrReader` and the viewer `ROLES` check are strict: an old reader rejects a scene containing
`rail` (as it would any new role since 4.1). The viewer's magenta fallback is only for palette lookups. The mod has no
role-to-block mapping yet (no `Role` use under `mod/src/main`), so nothing to change there; 6.1 must map `rail` (fence and wall
blocks connect to their neighbours by block state, so rails along a flight need the post connection update).

**Tests.** `RailingTest` (5 types x 7 slopes x 8 seeds): every rail has a step beside it at its level, a kerb under it,
no step in its column, ground at least a block below the tread; the full invariant set holds with and without the rails
(so `steps-reach-ground` and `entrance-reachable` are untouched); determinism; control: a rail put on the top step's tread
fails the invariants. Mutation: dropping the `blocked` check in `rails` makes `railsStandBesides...` and `railsChangeNoWalk` fail (run).
`GoldenDigestV1Test`: digests regenerated for all 11 types. I compared the changed case ids per type with the cases whose
JSON contains a `"rail"` box: identical sets (e.g. house 11 of 32, shop 18, wall 25, workshop 9), so only buildings that gained rails moved.
`city-check` on the 9 gate windows, no `--ignore`: 9 sites, 0 failing, 0 violations (report in `docs/review/phase6/railings/city-check.md`).

**Look.** `docs/review/phase6/railings/{house-slope,shop-slope-deep,tower-medieval}-{before,after}.png` (front-left cameras).
After: a dark rail line follows the open side of the house flights; shops get rail along the stoop and flight; stilted
lookouts (tower seeds 4 and 11) get rail up their long flight. Nothing else moved.
