# TPHOLD15: shorter teleport hold, and what happens after it gives up

John (2026-10-31): the hold barely protects you, lower it to about 15 s; later: "hold for what the tile requires, max 15 s".

- `TeleportHold.timeoutMillis` default is now the named constant `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MILLIS` = 15 s (tests use it instead of a literal 30 s). The timeout log line prints the real elapsed time.
- Void safety, from a test: `TeleportHoldClientGameTest.coldMetropolisSurvival` (same client test class, quick layer): fresh world seed 987654321, survival player teleports to the metropolis centre at the generator's base height + 2, hold released with the 3 x 3 not FULL (asserted). It samples the server every 0.5 s: no damage, y never below the surface, standing with 9/9 chunks at the end. Passed on the local iGPU (3 s limit) and on the perf worker (15 s limit, `FB_TPHOLD_LIMIT_MS`).
- The catch: the teleport into the unloaded chunk blocks the server thread (vanilla loads the chunk you are placed in synchronously). `Can't keep up! 40325ms` (the perf worker, 15 s limit), `177909ms` (local, 3 s limit, hold gave up before the warm). So the freeze after the hold is the real cost of a short cap on a cold metropolis. Not a timing measurement (not a quiet-host perf run).
- Option 3: `releaseOnPlan` (`-Dfractalbuildings.teleportHoldReleaseOnPlan=true`) releases when `RegionWarmer.warmPoint` is done on a planner-backed level (hook `testWarm`, game test `releasesWhenThePlanIsReady`; mutation: disabling the release made it fail). Off by default; stall after it not measured, and in the runs above the warm never finished inside 15 s. John to decide (QUESTIONS REGIONPERF).
