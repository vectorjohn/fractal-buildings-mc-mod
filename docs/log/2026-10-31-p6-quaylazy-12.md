## Step 1: `Quays.seeds` and `Quays.chainThrough`
- `contour` now builds the window's cell list (scan order: j outer, i inner) and calls `contourCells`; per-cell segments moved into `segments(near, i, j)`. Same output (the existing `QuaysTest` stays green).
- `seeds(near, ci0, cj0, ci1, cj1)`: the first end of every segment of a cell range, scan order (cell indices, not blocks).
- `chainThrough(near, i0, j0, i1, j1, seed)`: walks the graph of segment ends from the seed (each edge midpoint has at most two cells, both in the walk), collects the visited cells, sorts them in scan order and runs `contourCells` on them: start point and direction are the contour's by construction. Window edges end the walk.
- Tests (`QuaysTest`): `aWalkFromAnyPointGivesTheContoursChain` (200 noise fields incl. forced edges, 3 points per chain, >1000 walks), `seedsCoverEveryChain`, `aWalkReadsOnlyItsCells` (distinct nodes < 4 x chain points + 8), `aSaddleWalkKeepsItsOwnChain`.
- Mutation: replacing the scan-order sort by a reversal fails `aWalkFromAnyPointGivesTheContoursChain`.

- Test fixtures in the `world` package (they need `WorldPlanner.source`, package-private): `QuaySites` (8 sites, fresh planner and source per `open()`; `-Dfb.quaylazy.reference=true` selects the reference trace) and `QuayFilterTest`.
