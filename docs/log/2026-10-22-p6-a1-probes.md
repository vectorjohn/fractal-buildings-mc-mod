# P6 A1: live probes and their cost

Plan step A1 (`docs/plans/phase6.md`), branch `claude/p6-a1-probes`.

## What landed

`mod/src/main/java/dev/fractalbuildings/mod/probe/`:

- `LiveTerrainProbe`: exact floor and water depth with the dump definition. `column()` is the old
  `TerrainDumpTool.surfaceAndFloor`, moved to main code; the dump tool now calls it, so dumps and game cannot drift.
- `ColumnMemo` (per-world `BoundedCache`, one entry per chunk, `int[512]`), `ChunkFill` (three ways to fill a chunk),
  `SparseColumns` (single-column memo for the region 64 and coarse 32 lattices, where a whole chunk is waste),
  `LiveBiomeProbe` (PRIME lookup, then the climate sample at the surface quart; returns `minecraft:plains`, i.e. what
  `RecordedProbe.biome` returns), `LiveProbes` (the four probes over one memo), `ProbeInit` (one log line).
- `mod/src/main/resources/fractalbuildings.mixins.json` (empty list, package `dev.fractalbuildings.mod.mixin`) and its
  entry in `fabric.mod.json`. No mixin was needed: `NoiseChunk`'s constructor and `Aquifer.FluidPicker` are public, so
  `ChunkFill.Batched.fluidPicker` re-creates the generator's private picker (7 lines; the parity tests catch drift).
- `FractalBuildingsMod.onInitialize`: one added line (`ProbeInit.register()`).

## Timing (heavy lock; seed 987654321, `gate-temperate-city`, 1 thread)

**Load: `loadavg` 20.25 before, 12.8 after, on 20 cpus** (other agents' builds). Not a quiet machine: (a) measured 0.55 ms
per column against S6's 0.15 to 0.38, so absolute numbers are probably 1.5 to 3x high. Ratios are the reliable part.

| fill of one chunk (256 columns) | ms per chunk | ms per column |
|---|---|---|
| (a) 256 x `getBaseColumn` | 141.6 (149.2 on repeat) | 0.553 |
| (b) batched 16 x 384 x 16 volume | 5.0 | 0.020 |
| (c) windowed (surface level +-32) | **1.9** (0 fallbacks in 48) | 0.008 |

What one district plan (`Districts.plan` + `Briefs.fill`, 33 districts of the city) reads, from a recorded-terrain trace:
mean **21,119 calls, 10,124 distinct columns, 431 distinct chunks (23 columns per chunk)**.

Cold district plan on live terrain (fresh memo, five districts, mean of ms): single columns memoised 4,545; chunk memo
filled by (a) 47,703 (the memo is a loss with (a)); by (b) 2,056; by **(c) 813** (513 to 1,084).
Skeleton of the 1-cell city on live coarse reads, cold: 9.4 s with only 1,404 columns read (about 0.7 s of probes, the rest
is planner compute under load; not separated from JIT warm-up).

## What it means

- Decision 1 holds: the exact definition is affordable if (and only if) chunk reads go through (c). **Use
  `ChunkFill.DEFAULT` (= windowed); do not take the per-column path for districts.** A6 (cutting probe counts) is not
  needed for district reads: probe time is now about 0.4 to 0.8 s per cold district on one loaded thread, bounded by 431
  chunk fills, and neighbouring districts share chunks through the memo.
- The plan's assumption of under 300 ms per city tile is not met on this loaded machine (513 to 1,084 ms); it probably is
  on a quiet one (about 2x lower), but B8 must confirm. Workers in parallel (B4) divide it. If B8 still misses, the cheap
  next cut is to fill only the 4 x 4-column sub-blocks a district reads (columns per chunk is only 23 of 256), not A6.
- The region layer (stride 64, 17k to 28k columns cold) still pays 0.55 ms per single column: about 10 to 15 s cold on one
  thread, loaded. This was not measured here. A volume with `stepBlockX/Z` = 64 (DensityVolume supports steps) could make
  region reads batched too; not tried. B4/B8 decide whether the coarse-screen fallback or this is needed.
- Windowed correctness rests on the bracket check (air at the window top, floor inside) plus the margin; no failure in
  72 + 24 random chunks, but nothing proves it for exotic terrain (Terralith/Tectonic are B7's matrix). Fallback to (b) is automatic when the check fails.
