# 2026-10-21 P5 DB: briefs and district scenes (5.4 wiring, 5.7 mapping, 5.3 grading)

Branch `claude/p5-db-briefs`, base `claude/run-phase5` 6fe487b.

## What

- `core/settlement/Briefs.fill(plan, quarterKind, StyleField, exact)`: per lot the style (`StyleField.pick` at the lot centre), storey range (the `ClassTable` entry of the lot's density and quarter within the type's limits), `streets` mask (a side counts with >= 3 street or plaza columns next to it, mapped to the lot's local frame, front forced), `frontY` (graded street y at the column just outside the front centre, interpolated along the street; plaza y on a plaza; else `NO_FRONT_Y`), status. A lot whose type fails the size/relief checks of `SpecPolicyV1.spec` is re-picked with `ClassTable.pick` (real relief), else `empty:steep` (relief > 8) or `empty:no-fit`; house/workshop with relief > 8 become `empty:steep`.
- `core/settlement/DistrictScenes.build(plan, landmarks, exact, biome, exterior)` returns `Built(scene, plan, fallbacks)`: GeneratorV1 per `build` lot with its brief, terrain patch over tile + 8 with `water`, `ground` boxes from `Grading.road` per street and `Grading.plaza` per plaza (Kind to Role: ROAD road, PLAZA paving, CUT air, FILL/DECK/PIER ground), `Invariants.check` on the scene, `SettlementChecks.district` plus the new `road-grade` rule (`SettlementChecks.roadGrade`, on each street's graded centre line) and `plaza-grade` on the plan. A lot the generator still rejects (IllegalArgumentException) falls back to a 1-2 storey house, else `empty:gen-failed`; the returned plan records it. `exterior` strips interiors after the checks.
- `SettlementChecks.footprint` made public (street footprint rectangles).
- `StyleMixReportTest` (the style-mix report ST deferred).
