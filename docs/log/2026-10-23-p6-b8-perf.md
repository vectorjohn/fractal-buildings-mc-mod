# P6 B8: performance budget in CI (6.7)

Branch `claude/p6-b8-perf`. Plan step B8 in [phase6.md](../plans/phase6.md). Numbers table for the gate pack:
[review/phase6/perf/perf.md](../review/phase6/perf/perf.md).

## What was built

- `PerfPlannerTest` (`mod/src/test/.../perf/`, `-Dfb.perf=1`, no server): live vanilla terrain, cold planners. Cold region/road network, cold district plans
  (median/p95 of 7 tiles), warmed (workers) vs lane, exact-probe call count, N players in different cities with the heap-scaled default caches
  (observer counts recomputes), the B7 footprint query (by reflection, so it also compiles without B7). Writes `mod/build/perf/planner.json`.
- `PerfClientGameTest` (client game test, only runs with `FB_PERF` set; `FB_PERF=only` makes the other client tests return): two normal worlds of seed
  987654321, one with settlements and one with frequency 0 (same terrain, nothing of ours). Each: `/locate` (settlement, vanilla village) as the console,
  a 33 b/s elytra flight (teleport per tick, spectator) from 2.5 km out to the gate city with server tick times recorded, then a cold 9x9-chunk block in the
  metropolis (teleport, nothing warm), then the next block after warm+prefetch settled. Region-file bytes before/after (flushed), heap after GC, lane counters
  (`LaneStats`, three new getters). Writes `world.json`.
- `scripts/perf.sh` (heavy lock inside) runs both and `scripts/perf-check.mjs`, which compares with `mod/perf-budget.json` (budget + CI ceiling; default ceiling 2x
  budget; above the ceiling fails, above the budget prints `miss`) and writes `summary.md`. `scripts/test-all.sh --perf` runs it as a step (opt-in: ~10 to 15 min).
  Not run through test-all end to end (only `--help` and `bash -n`).

## Answers (quiet = loadavg under 2 at the start of that half; the machine was shared with other heavy jobs, so see the load per number)

1. **Cold district plan**: median 409 ms (380 to 472), p95 638 ms; first tile of a metropolis 1.29 s. The 300 ms median is **not** met (1.4x); p95 is. The lane pays 0.04 ms
   when prefetched. It is hidden by warming except for teleports/respawns. **Recommendation: against A6 now.** A miss of 1.4x that only the no-lead case sees does not pay for
   cutting the footprint (and JUNCTION will add probes: see 7). Revisit if John finds the teleport hitch bad: the first teleport block's worst chunk was 4.0 s, and that is region, not district.
2. **Region layer + roads cold**: 4.2 to 5.6 s (4.76 s at load 1.7), budget 1 s: **missed 4 to 5x**. Warmed on workers (4.6 s) 34 to 68 s ahead at elytra speed, so travel never pays it; a
   teleport into an unvisited metropolis pays it once as one stalled chunk (4.0 s in game; Minecraft's watchdog is 60 s). **The W2 coarse-screen fallback is needed only if a 4 to 5 s
   stall on teleport into a metropolis is unacceptable**; a cheaper first step is to `warm()` on join/respawn (not done: spawn is rarely inside a metropolis). I recommend leaving W2 off the Phase 6
   list and putting the hitch in front of John (REVIEW).
3. **`/locate`**: settlements 1.6 s fresh, 1.1 s from spawn (budget 10 s) in the game, with B7 merged. A vanilla `#minecraft:village` locate in a fresh area (the veto runs on the server thread for each candidate, cold)
   took **6.7 s** (at load 5 to 22), then 0.4 ms repeated (cached). The microbench behind it: the footprint query scans 169 cells, 7.5 s for the first cold call, 58 ms mean walking a 12 km line (10.9 s total).
   Under the 60 s watchdog and under the 10 s budget, so nothing to fix now; the cost is cold region decisions (~44 ms per cell) that are cached afterwards. If it grows (config with a larger `maxRadius`
   makes the scan wider), narrow the reach or screen with the coarse lattice. Not implemented.
4. **Multi-player**: 3 players in 3 cities (867 chunk requests, 21 tiles) and 8 players (2,312, 55 tiles): **0 recomputes**, +31 and +69 MB heap, with the default caches (heap-scaled, retention). Tile
   counts stay under the caps (32/64); the over-capacity regime is covered by CACHE's `WorldPlannerCachePressureTest` (cap 4). 8 players cost ~2 min of cold planning at load 5.
5. **Per-chunk cost on the lane**: postProcess 1.1 ms mean on the cold block, 2.0 ms warmed (up to 4 ms at high load), budget 5 ms: ok, B5 bed checks included (BEDFIX merged). Start creation on the lane
   9 to 27 ms mean (max 94 ms), 8 concurrent. The whole **chunk** wall (terrain + features + lighting + ours) is 3.2x vanilla in a downtown (67 vs 21 ms; budget 1.5x, S6 saw 2.1x): the mod's own parts
   (start 9 ms, postProcess 2 ms) explain ~11 of the 46 ms extra, so most of the rest is the extra block updates, heightmaps and light of a built-up chunk, which no budget on our code can cut. The 1.5x was
   proportionally unrealistic; propose 3x or measuring on the lane only.
6. **Save size**: +62 % to +102 % region-file bytes for 162 downtown chunks (1.0 to 1.7 MB, 6 to 10 KB a chunk; varies because only changed sectors flush). Budget +10 % was S6's whole-area figure; downtown is
   the worst case. The start NBT itself was not measured separately. A metropolis of ~25k chunks cannot be extrapolated from downtown.
7. **JUNCTION**: not merged when measured (branch `claude/p6-junction` is separate). Current state: a metropolis's first tile plan reads **93,170** exact probe columns (1.29 s cold). Re-run `scripts/perf.sh` after
   JUNCTION merges; the budget file checks the plan time and the warmed lane.

## Other findings

- Worst server tick during the flight: 337 ms at the moment the city is reached (lane joins/computes the first starts); 3,990 ms once at load 22; vanilla 96 to 313 ms at world start. Budget invented here (250 ms), ceiling 10 s.
- Inline share 0.7 %, joined 10, done 2,402: warming works at elytra speed.
- The ordering note: the world test flies first from the far side so the metropolis block is truly cold; an earlier order that flew toward the metropolis side showed a 3.3 s stall.
- Region growth and tick numbers swing with load; the ceilings in `perf-budget.json` are set from the noisy runs, so load does not flake CI.
