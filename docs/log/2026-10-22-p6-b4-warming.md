# P6 B4: prefetch, warming and lane counters

Branch `claude/p6-b4-warming`. Plan step B4 in [phase6.md](../plans/phase6.md).

## What was built

- `Workers` (`mod/.../worldgen`): one pool per server, at most 4 daemon threads `fractalbuildings-worker-N` at min priority, size
  `clamp(cpus/4, 1, 4)`, bounded queue of 512 (a full pool refuses; prefetch and warming treat that as "not now"). Started on
  `SERVER_STARTING` (hands itself to `WorldPlanners.setWorkers`), shut down on `SERVER_STOPPING`.
- `RegionWarmer`: every 20 ticks, per overworld player, one queued task at most (a `busy` flag) that `warm`s radius 2 cells around the
  player and around the point 5 s ahead along the last second's velocity (capped 600 blocks), then `prefetch`es the tile under each
  point when the tile changed. Pure caches only. `warmPlayer(...)` is public for tests.
- Start creation (`DistrictStructure`) calls `planner.prefetch(tile)` once the tier matches.
- `LaneStats` (`-Dfb.lane=1`, or `LaneStats.enable` in tests): start creation and `postProcess` mean/max/peak concurrent, building
  lookups DONE / INFLIGHT / INLINE, prefetch/warm calls and mean warm time, refused tasks. `/fb lane` prints `LaneStats.report()`.
  Off, every hook is one volatile read. Lookups are classified by a new core hook `WorldPlanner.observeBuildings(Consumer<Lookup>)`
  (`FlightCache` knows whether it served a finished result, joined a running one, or ran the task itself).
- `FractalBuildingsMod.onInitialize`: one added line (`RegionWarmer.register()`, which registers `Workers` too).

## Timing note: does warming hide a cold district? (`LiveWarmTiming`, live vanilla terrain seed 987654321, 4 workers)

Load 9.5 before / 7.6 after, 20 cpus (not a quiet machine). Settlement `s2_m2` (metropolis, centre 2304,-1920); tile 144x192 blocks.

| | ms |
|---|---|
| cold: region resolve + road network (skeletons) | 3,309 |
| cold: district plan + buildings + ground of the centre tile (3 buildings) | 1,001 |
| cold: a neighbour tile, skeleton warm (11 buildings) | 483 |
| warmed: worker time, region + skeletons | 3,215 |
| warmed: prefetch of tile + 8 neighbour plans, wall on 4 workers | 979 |
| warmed: the lane then pays for the tile / for a neighbour's buildings | 0.03 / 64 |

Reading it: the first touch of a metropolis is about 4.3 s cold, all of it hidden once the warm has finished (the lane pays 0.03 ms,
then 64 ms for a neighbour). The warm needs about 3.2 s plus 1 s of prefetch, started when the settlement is within 2 cells
(2,048 blocks) of the player or of the point 5 s ahead. Lead time at walking/sprinting (4.3 / 5.6 b/s): 366 to 476 s, hidden with a
huge margin. At elytra speed (30 to 60 b/s) the lead is 34 to 68 s against 4.2 s of work, still hidden. What is **not** hidden:
teleports/respawns into a settlement (full cold cost, accepted), and a settlement entered from a direction change faster than the
1 s tick plus 5 s lead (the tile prefetch needs the player or the lead point to be on the tile, so at 60 b/s a tile of 144 blocks
(2.4 s) is crossed before its 1 s prefetch of the neighbours is guaranteed done: that case relies on the lane joining workers
(INFLIGHT) rather than hiding entirely). The district plan itself (0.5 to 1 s per tile) is not made cheaper by warming, only moved;
A6 stays a B8 decision. Not measured here: in-game walking (no client run; the game test world is flat and has no structures).

## Decisions / surprises

- `Workers.create(n)` is public so tests make pools without touching the server's; the default pool is the one `WorldPlanners` uses.
- `STATE` of the warmer is cleared on `SERVER_STOPPED` (tile keys must not outlive a planner).
- Warmed "ahead" point uses the last second's displacement; a stationary player warms only their own position.
- Known issue: `prefetch` only fires when a settlement tile lies under the player or the lead point; flying in a straight line over
  open terrain toward a city 3 km away warms the region (radius 2 cells) but the first tile prefetch waits until the city is near.
