# VILLROAD: roads bend round vanilla villages and spur to their street

John (2026-10-26): `docs/review/phase6/roads-ingame/road-country-side.png` shows a vanilla garden sitting right over an intercity road. His choice: option 3 (the road bends round the village piece and rejoins) plus a spur path from road to village, so they "really seem integrated". Reports: `.claude/reports/p6-villroad*.md` (outside git).

**Diagnosis.** Vanilla structure pieces are placed in the structure step, before features. `RoadFeature.draw` yields every column whose ground is within `FOREIGN_SLACK` of a piece top (MF2), so the road was cut at the garden and stopped at its edge. The B7 veto covers settlement footprints only, and `RoadNet` cannot see villages.

**What was built (round 8 state).**
- `VillageJoin` (planner-core, pure): from a village's piece boxes (and its street pieces) and the planned road columns of its window, columns within 4 (+2 shoulder) of a box are removed and each run is replaced by the cheapest 8-connected path round the pieces, in the route's own width, material (`road()`/`deck()` copied, DS-30) and linear deck height. One spur per road component (at most 2, within 40, a second one at least 30 from the first): a 3-wide band, 5 wide for the first 2 cells at the road (a T mouth, DS-52), aimed at the nearest of the village's own path cells and stopping on the cell beside the first path cell it meets (a straight 4-connected line, else the cheapest 4-path round the houses), so it abuts the vanilla street and never overwrites it. Street boxes are passable (outside their path they are grass margin); other pieces are not. Without path cells (unit fixtures) the r6 rule (end on a street box's centre line) still applies. The spur is graded (DS-31, r8): every band cell gets a station (4-connected distance over the band from the road column it leaves), and a profile over stations runs from the road's surface to the met path cell's height in steps of at most 1, following the ground where it can; when the rise is more than the line is long, other road columns are tried so the spur leaves the road further along (`steep` counts any that still can't be graded). Order independent: the answer depends only on the boxes and the columns.
- `VillageRoads` (mod): reads the village starts within 8 chunks (cached per planner), applies the join to the chunk's 3 x 3 column lists, reads each street piece's path cells from its template (`dirt_path`, desert `smooth_sandstone`; read at STRUCTURE_STARTS through the template manager and the piece's position and rotation, so the same in any chunk order) and lays the spur in that block, cut or filled to its graded height. The heights come from `VillageGround`: the noise column replayed with a beardifier built from the village start, which is where `terrain_matching` puts the street (GravityProcessor on WORLD_SURFACE_WG), with no chunk read, so in any order. Village and heights are built once per start. `RoadFeature`'s yield to foreign piece boxes (MF2) no longer takes spur cells inside a street box (r6's spur was cut there and stopped in the grass margin, the "stops short" look); other pieces still stop it. The 17 x 17 start scan is skipped when no planned road column lies within 4 chunks.
- `RoadsClientGameTest`: the default mode is unchanged except that the village checks are not required (logged, and still checked when a village is near the corridor); `FB_ROADS_VILLAGE=1` makes the test find its own village (`findNearestMapStructure` along the planned routes) and adds the `nojoin` control order (join switched off, which must change the road and fail the check), the `spuryield` control (spur cells yield to street boxes as in r6: the strict end check must fail), the `spurshort` (bare centre line 2 short) and `ungraded` (r7's ground-following spur) controls, both of which must fail, a plan view PNG of the influence box (`road-village-plan.png`) with the template path cells,  and three shots (`road-village`, `road-village-bend`, `road-village-spur`). `FB_ONLY=<TestClass>` runs one client test.

**History.** r1 diagnosis; r2 the join and the first in-game check; r3 fixed `RoadFeature.draw` (an `any` flag that made every intercity road switch off); r5 the village-finding test and the nojoin control (which had not switched the join off); r6 (verifier: default mode failed; architect: show the bend, wider spur, end on a street block). r7 (verifier: the r6 end check passed on our own blocks; the spur stopped at the street box): spur aimed at the templates' path cells, yield exemption, strict end check (the end drawn, 4-connected to the road through drawn cells, beside a path block we did not draw within 1 of its height), template path cells checked against the world (60% or more must be path blocks), VillageJoinTest cases for path cells, a house in the street margin, shuffled paths and route material. r8 (architect: DS-31 violation, 2-block steps where the spur climbs the terraced margin): graded spur, step check fails above 1, firmer controls, a required run fails with no spur.

*Known issues:* villages at the edge of the 8-chunk scan window are joined only by the chunks that see them; the grade's heights do not model carvers that open the surface, other structures' beards or lakes placed before the village (the in-game check logs how often they equal the placed path block); the spur's cut sides are bare walls as high as the cut (no terrace like the road's edges); the bend hugs each piece box rather than swinging a smooth arc (suggestion: 6-8 clear, grown convex hull); only village starts, no farms-avoidance; a second spur only to a different street 30+ away; the order-test spiral air-to-dirt flake belongs to the SPILLDIRT class, fixed on run-phase6.
