# CIRC2: squarer rooms, bedrooms first (coder)

Branch `claude/p5-circ2` (base `claude/run-phase5`). John's 5.11 review: houses lost bedrooms, seed 9 got a
long skinny living room; lean square, bedrooms before repeated other room types, not always.

## What changed

- `FloorPlan.plan`: the 8 re-plan attempts are now scored, lowest wins: `deep * 200` (rooms 3+ doors from the hub,
  as before) + `shapePenalty` (1 point per 10 % of aspect ratio beyond 2:1, per non-corridor room) + `(scale-100)/25`
  (so bigger rooms are a fallback, not the aim). Used to be "first plan with no deep room, else least deep".
- `FloorPlan.Planner.split`: a room longer than 2.2:1 is cut across its length even when it is under the target
  area (not beside a corridor, where cuts are already forced across). This is what removed most long rooms: a 3x9
  wing is now two 3x4 rooms.
- New `RoomProgram.refine(roles, areas, uses, where, draws)` (default no-op), called by `InteriorPass.band` after the
  per-room uses. `HouseRooms.refine` (ground floor only): spare (`ROOM`) rooms, in a seeded order, get first a bedroom
  (one-storey house, or a lone wing room 80 % of the time), then the missing kitchen, then the missing living room
  (the entry room is already "living" when >= 16 cells), and every further one a bedroom, except 15 %
  (`REPEAT_PERCENT`) that repeat a kitchen or living room. A one-room hut (entry only, < 16 cells) is a bedroom.
- Tests: new `RoomShapeTest` (aspect share < 8 %, >= 1.5 bedrooms per house, over 300 houses of three styles;
  `-Dfb.roomshape=N` prints the table below). `HouseTypeTest` relaxed in two places: a ground-floor bedroom is allowed in
  a multi-storey house (not when it is nearly all of the ground floor), and a one-room hut has a bedroom instead of an entry.

## Decisions

Spare ground rooms in a multi-storey house may be bedrooms (the old test said none); a lone wing keeps a seeded
chance of a kitchen so a house made of wings is not all bedrooms (seed 7 still has none: three wings, 80 % each).
Cheap to change: `LONG_TENTHS`, `SOFT_ASPECT`, `SCALE_COST`, `REPEAT_PERCENT`.
