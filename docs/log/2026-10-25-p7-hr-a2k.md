# 7.7 A2k: keep variants for towers of 7+ storeys

Plan step A2k of [plans/highrises.md](../plans/highrises.md) (DS-1: no oak-log beams on 7-10 storey towers).

- `styles/keep.json` (extends `plain`), `keep-desert.json` (extends `desert`), `keep-jungle.json` (extends `jungle`), each with `"biomes": []`, palette and colour overrides only. Keep: stone-brick wall and trim, polished andesite beam, cobblestone foundation, smooth stone floor and deck, cobblestone-wall fence and rail, stone-brick stairs. Desert: cut sandstone wall, chiseled trim, smooth sandstone beam, sandstone-wall fence. Jungle: mossy stone bricks wall, stone-brick beam/trim/floor/deck, mossy cobblestone-wall fence and rail. Ids appended to `styles/index.txt`.
- `variants.json`: three new rules after `tall-default` (own entries, nothing reformatted): `styles desert` -> keep-desert, `styles jungle` -> keep-jungle, any other base -> keep; all `types tower`, `minStoreys 7`. The tower keeps its open stair (no stairwell, no shaft).
- Test: `types/tower/KeepVariantsTest` (rule mapping per base style, 6 storeys and non-towers unchanged, no `_log`/`_planks`/`_fence`/stripped/bamboo in wall, beam, trim, deck, rail, fence, foundation of the three keeps, and 7 base styles x 150 seeds of real specs).
- Goldens: only `golden/v1/tower.txt` changed (regenerated, not hand-merged).
- BlockMapperTest stair/slab list: unchanged. The keeps' `stair` and `roof` ids (stone_bricks, mossy_stone_bricks, smooth_sandstone, planks of jungle roof) all have stairs and slabs; cut/chiseled sandstone are only wall/trim (full blocks).

Verification: `:planner-core:test` (736 tests; the only failures were the tower golden, regenerated, and PaletteCalmTest, whose viewer colours must equal `block-colours.json`: fixed), `:mod:test` pass, `check --seeds 0..29 --types tower` 21120 cases 0 failing.
