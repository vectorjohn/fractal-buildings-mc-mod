# 7.17 SHOPLOW step S1v: viewer shows cuts and areaways

Viewer only; no Java. Works from a hand-built fixture, so S1/S2 can land independently.

- `viewer/src/mesh.ts` `terrainTops` now also lowers terrain columns by each building: footprint (volumes with local `min[1] == 0`,
  placed with `placeColumn`) to `min(top, origin.y)`, and by building `air` site boxes that span the surface (start below the
  column top, reach it) to the box bottom. Raw heights are used for the "spans" test. `paving`/`ground`/`foundation` site boxes
  stay voxels. Buildings at or above the ground change nothing (all existing output unchanged; 139 viewer tests pass).
- `viewer/src/render.ts`: a scene with any cut uses the heightfield terrain mesh even when it is a single building. Before, the
  instanced path drew one cube per column and ignored cuts (earth inside the room). Found by the screenshot.
- Fixture `viewer/fixtures/shoplow/cut-fixture.json`, from `viewer/scripts/gen-cut-fixture.mjs`: 8x8 shop, slab oy 64, terrain
  64 to the street, rising to 68 at the back; air + paving ring on the sides and back.
- Tests: `mesh.test.ts` (footprint, ring, untouched beyond, rotation, no-op for a raised building, mesh has no top quad above the
  slab, control with the cut data stripped); `renderDistrict.test.ts` (cut scene uses the heightfield, both mesh modes; mutation:
  dropping `|| cut` fails it). `e2e/shoplow-cut.spec.ts` writes `out/shoplow-cut-1..3.png` (top, street, east side), looked at:
  no earth in the room, the trench ring open down to the slab level, door at street level with the cut face behind.
- docs/VIEWER.md: paragraph under colours.

## IR fields consumed (S2 must emit these)
Building `origin[1]` = the slab (floor box local y 0 at world `origin.y`); `shell.volumes[]` (`min[1] == 0` = footprint);
`shell.bounds.size` and `rotation` (placement); `siteBoxes[]` in the building's local frame with `role: "air"` (cut: bottom y is
where the column drops to; must reach the original surface), `role: "paving"` / `"ground"` (voxels on top). Terrain `floorY`
is the raw ground. World-frame `scene.ground` air boxes work as before.
