# 6.27 DISTPERF: district cold plan time

HEAVY6 saw `district.cold.median.ms` rise from 400-480 ms to 655-1263 ms (ceiling 1500).

## Diagnosis
- Probe counts in the pinned evidence: 90933 (620ca7fd, DS36ROOFS r2) then 136645 at DS35DOORS (9935ce0df8, 208b540118) then 154230 at head. The median at low load follows (399, 667, 630-708). DS36ROOFS's 717 was measured at load 9: noise, probes unchanged.
- JFR on `PerfPlannerTest` (head): the district plan is dominated by terrain noise fills (`Aquifer.computeSubstance`, from `Districts`/`SkeletonSource`, not from the generator). Inside the generator, `InstancePassV1.searchRuns` is the hot spot, and within it `touchesRouteOk`/`onRoute` (walks of the BFS route chain for every neighbour of every state).
- DS35DOORS (`placeKeepingDoors` rounds, `badSecondary`, door retries in `GeneratorV1.withDoors`) re-places a building up to dozens of times; each re-reads lot-wide columns from the live probe.

## Fix (output unchanged)
- `DistrictScenes.generateLots` wraps the probe in a `MemoProbe` (one thread, one district): probes 154230 to 67078.
- `searchRuns` marks the cells of the route to the state being expanded once per pop; `onRoute`, `routeLength`, `touchesRouteOk`, `endTouchesOk` become lookups (a route never visits a cell twice, so cell to state is unique).
- `PerfPlannerTest`: new row `district.cold.cpu.ms` (process CPU over the seven plans), the only reading that is meaningful on a busy host.
