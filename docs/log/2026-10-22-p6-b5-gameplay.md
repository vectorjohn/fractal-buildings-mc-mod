# P6 B5: gameplay hooks and commands (6.4)

Branch `claude/p6-b5-gameplay`, base `claude/run-phase6`.

## What was done
- `planner-core/.../world/WorldHooks` (pure): a building piece's hooks in world coordinates (`Placement` applied to position and facing), `bedInOneChunk`, per-hook seeds (`Hash64(worldSeed, purpose, x, y, z)`), `hasVillagers(tier)` and `villagerBeds` (the 10 placeable beds of a tile with the smallest position hash; independent of lot order).
- `mod/.../gameplay/HookPlacer`: called from `TilePiece.postProcess` for building pieces, after the shell cells, for the hooks inside the chunk (clipped to the piece). Bed (colour by hash; foot at the hook, head toward `facing`), chest (loot table `fractalbuildings:chests/<type>`, seed `RandomSource.create(hash).nextLong()`), workstation (workshop: smithing/stonecutter/fletching/blast furnace/grindstone/cartography; shop: barrel or lectern; house: loom/composter/cartography; temple: brewing stand; others: lectern/barrel/loom), table (crafting table), hearth (furnace), altar (enchanting table), counter (barrel), bench (spruce slab), light (lantern, hung from a ceiling within 3 cells for half of them). A hook is skipped when its cell is not air or has no floor; a bed is skipped when its head would cross a chunk edge or is not air. Hook blocks enter the `PieceLedger` through `override` (state replaced, no second owner counted).
- `Villagers`: one vanilla villager per selected bed, UUID derived from seed and position; only if `Gameplay.villagersEnabled()` (the pinned `villagers` setting, read at overworld load) and the tile's tier is hamlet or village.
- `FbCommand`: `/fb info` (settlement, tier, tile, lot, type, style, hook count), `/fb locate [hamlet|village|town|city|metropolis]` (nearest settlement centre within 8 region cells, clickable `/tp` suggestion). `/locate structure #fractalbuildings:settlement` works through B1's structures.
- `api/Settlements.at(...)`: read-only lookup for other mods (README).
- Loot tables: `data/fractalbuildings/loot_table/chests/{house,shop,workshop,temple,hall,tower}.json` (curtain and bastion use tower, block and highrise use house).

## Decisions and surprises
- 26.3 has `Blocks.BED` as a `ColorCollection` (`Blocks.BED.red()`), `EntityTypes.VILLAGER`, villagers in `world.entity.npc.villager`. A bed's POI is its head block.
- Villagers at worldgen: `finalizeSpawn` + `addFreshEntityWithPassengers` on the `WorldGenLevel` worked (entities appear after the tile generates).
- Villager selection is over placeable beds (both cells in one chunk), but the shell may still refuse a head cell, so a district can end up with fewer than 10.
- Beds per building are many in tall buildings; the cap keeps people at 10 per tile, but all beds stay.
- Round 2: each chunk places its own half of a bed (decided from the planner raster at both cells, so chunk order cannot matter); the villager comes from the foot chunk. Flat plains seeds 1-30: buildings with bed hooks but no bed 8 of 642 with the one-chunk rule, 0 with halves (`BedPlacementTest`).
