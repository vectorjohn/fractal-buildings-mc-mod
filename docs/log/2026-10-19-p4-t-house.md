# T-house: the house as a type (4.6)

Branch `claude/p4-t-house`. House massing (M) already gave L/T/U/courtyard plans, wings, jetties and setbacks; this step adds what makes it a house inside.

- `HouseRooms` (via `HouseType.rooms()`): ground floor living / kitchen / entry (the room behind the door is the living room when >= 16 cells), upper floors bedrooms, a stair room with >= 24 cells counts as a room. Hooks: bed + chest (+ table, 2nd bed by area) in bedrooms, table / hearth / chest in living rooms, hearth / table / chest in kitchens.
- Additive change to the interiors API (default methods, no change for other types): `RoomProgram.use/hooks` overloads with `Where(storey, storeys)` and `newBand(storey)` so ground and upper floors never share a plan.
- Massing: cores in houses never stand mid-wall (new `MassingOps.core(..., centre)` overload, `centre=false`); `HouseMassing.roomy` rejects a layout where a core-served mass keeps < 5 free wall-side cells (otherwise a small upper floor was all stair, no bedroom). Small envelopes now give one-storey houses instead.
- Front margin stays 1 (limits unchanged). Presets: existing four `house-*` pages kept.
- Golden `house.txt` regenerated.

Verification: `HouseTypeTest` (4 envelopes x 256 seeds x 2 styles): every 2+-storey house has a bedroom with a bed upstairs and none on its ground floor; living room or entry downstairs; hearth in 89 %; variety floors. Mutants: upstairs use `room` -> two tests fail; bed hook removed -> two fail; `MIN_FREE = 0` -> bedroom test fails. `:planner-core:test :cli:test` pass; `check --types house --seeds 0..999` 0 violations. Gallery `house-medieval` (front-left, cutaway) and `house-plain-flat` opened: wings of other heights, jetties, a courtyard house (seed 15), partitions and hook markers in the cutaway, 0 violations.
