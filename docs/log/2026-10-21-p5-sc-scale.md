# 5.9 SC: scale check (2026-10-21)

**Added.** `fractal-cli city-bench --seed S (--cell CX,CZ | --site X,Z,TIER,R) [--terrain T] [--reps N --warm N] [--scenes N] [--cache N] [--region-cache N]`
(`cli/.../CityBenchCommand.java`) and `scripts/city-bench.sh` (the whole matrix plus the cache and heap sweeps, one JVM per
configuration, run under the heavy lock). Per repetition (fresh caches) it times the cold region decision, each cell skeleton, assembly,
and per district `Districts.plan` and `Briefs.fill` with counted exact (`floorY` calls), coarse and biome probes; then one whole
`CityPlanner.plan` (bytes allocated by the thread, retained heap after a forced GC), and a sample of scenes with their own probe counts;
JSON sizes and peak RSS. Not part of any test task. `CityCommand.fieldView` gained a cache-capacity overload.

**Results** are in [docs/review/phase5/scale.md](../review/phase5/scale.md) (tables, raw output beside it). Run on branch commit
9bf001f (run-phase5 `9fbee73` merged), heavy lock held, loadavg 1-min 4 to 10 at the start of each configuration, 5/10/15-min 9 to 15
(the machine never got quieter than that; the first sweep at loadavg 10 to 30 gave the same shape with 1.5 to 2 x slower numbers).

**Verdict.** Per-district cost is flat on recorded terrain (1.2 to 1.4 x from a city to the 8-cell metropolis) and per lot on every
terrain; the raw synthetic ratio (1.8 x with scenes in the JVM, 1.1 to 1.2 x without) is JIT state and a different district mix (the
small synthetic city is mostly 8-lot outskirts). Not flat, found: the exact-probe count per district exceeds the 1,000 bar in the dense
cases (above) and scenes read 100k+ columns each; this is the lever for 6.5 and 6.7, not the plan's CPU. Memory is a non-issue:
retention under 5 MiB, 64 MiB heap suffices for 484 districts. Bounded caches: skeleton capacity 4 is enough, 1 to 2 cost 1.5 x to 4.8 x.
Skeleton under 50 ms: yes. A cold region decision is 1 to 3 ms (recorded terrain), the one-off live cost being the probes (NOTES, MC1).

**Not fixed (not small).** District probes: `Districts.plan` memoises `floorY` and `waterDepth` per column of the whole built area; reading a
coarser height grid for layout would change plans (determinism baselines), so it is a design decision for 6.5/6.7.
**Caveats.** Single thread; loaded machine (+-30 %); 5 to 25 timed reps; "retained heap" is below GC noise, allocation bytes are the reliable memory figure.
Quick control: `-Xmx64m` completes the 484-district plan, proving retention is small.
