# P5 SK: cell skeleton

Branch `claude/p5-sk-skeleton`. Plan step SK (5.2 skeleton half, 5.6, 5.8 gates); no ROADMAP checkbox of its own (DL, DT and MC2 tick 5.2, 5.6, 5.8; notes added under each).

## What

- `core/settlement/`: `Skeletons.build(seed, RegionView, Cell, CoarseProbe, DistrictGrid)` builds one member cell's `CellSkeleton`; `Skeletons.plan` builds every member cell and `assemble` merges them (any order) into `fb-settlement/1`. Parts: `CellGeometry` (Voronoi polygon, exact ownership, canonical shared edge), `CoarseTerrain` (pointwise water, peak, slope on the 32 lattice), `DensityField` (deformed disk of 8 angular harmonics, `cap (1 - t^2)` plus hashed noise and an arterial bonus), `QuarterField`, `Arterials` (tile-border segments for city and up), `TierProgram` + `DefaultProgram` (hub, exits, A* main roads on `LatticePath`, hub square with hall or temple, `streetLayout` for DL), `SkeletonContext`, `RegionView` + `FixedSiteView` + `FieldSiteView` (adapts MC1's `FieldRegionView`), `TerrainCrop`.
- `SettlementChecks.skeletonInCell` and `cellGateAgree`; `fractal-cli city` (`--cell` through the join rule or `--site X,Z,TIER,RADIUS`; writes `settlement.json` and the `terrain.json.gz` crop).
- Follow-ups from earlier verifiers: one tier enum (`settlement.Tier` now carries weights, bands and caps; `Tiers.Tier` removed, region code and tests updated); `WaterSamples.of(CoarseProbe)`; `LatticePath.defaultCost(exact, water, step)` (the old overload kept); `GroundBox.Kind.role()`; `DistrictGrid.Tile.rect()`; TA's `Rect` replaced by `settlement.Rect` (DB's `DistrictScenes` adjusted); `Grading.RoadGrade.ok()` (the steep-column boxes stay: callers reroute or reject, DB already reports `road-grade`). TA's lattice-window cost is bounded because the skeleton searches only a cell's own window at step 32 (at most 2 water-run states).
- Viewer: fixtures `viewer/fixtures/settlement/skeleton/{river-town,joined-metropolis}` (README with the commands), `e2e/skeleton.spec.ts` (plan view over them), review PNGs in `docs/review/phase5/skeleton/`.

## Decisions and surprises

- Gate columns are the lattice column nearest the hashed border point and ignore slope (a ±20 error would reject most columns): `skeleton-in-cell` is "owned or one of the cell's gates". Exits and the mask do change under the ±20 error, so that test counts cell gates (plan deviation recorded).
- Arterials and district lists use the pointwise urban test, not the hub-reachability mask, so neighbours agree. Arterial parts of one segment are emitted per cell and joined by id at merge.
- The hub of a cell that does not hold the site centre is the urban column nearest the cell's own site (plan text); on a joined city these are anchors on the cell edge side.
- `quarters` in the plan always list all eight kinds; district `origin` is the corner chunk.
- Known issues: the urban mask is fragmented on hilly vanilla terrain (`CoarseTerrain.STEEP_PER_32`); exit roads can be short on tiny hamlets; the Landmark hall sits in the hub tile even when the hub is near its edge; `TierProgram` has only the default program until D1 to D3; `walls` is a no-op.
