# 4.8 Site: front steps, height cap, replay

Branch `claude/p4-s-site`. Plan step S of `docs/plans/phase4.md`.

## What changed

- **`entrance-reachable`** (`check/Invariants.java`, `Invariant.java`; genVersion >= 2 only): from the
  cell outside each ground-storey exterior door, a BFS with rooms-reachable's moves (terrain solid up to
  `floorY`, never back through a door) must reach a standing position on the terrain itself, inside one
  of the building's lots and outside its footprint.
- **Front steps** (`gen/v1/InstancePassV1.java`): per ground-storey exterior door a landing one cell out
  at slab height, then a flight of one STAIR block per cell down to the ground, on FOUNDATION to the
  ground. Breadth-first over lot columns (so the shortest flight; ties prefer straight out, then
  clockwise, then counter-clockwise), so on the house's front margin of 1 the flight turns along the
  facade. Free of the shell (headroom checked), off every door's outside cell, off the footprint. A door
  whose outside ground is level with, or one below, the slab gets none. The slab is lifted to the ground
  in front of a door when that is higher than the footprint's (otherwise the door would be buried). No
  route: nothing is added and the invariant reports it.
- **Height cap**: `SpecPolicyV1` already caps `maxHeight` (F0); added tests: every type at ground
  250..314 stays under y 319 with 0 violations, 315/316/319 fail cleanly (`IllegalArgumentException`,
  CLI exit 2).
- **Replay**: `cli/.../ReplayTest` runs 20 replay-shaped commands through `Main.run` and compares
  SHA-256 of the output with the core's own generation (violations embedded); a check that dropping
  `--at` changes the scene; the no-room exit 2. The commands already carried type/lot/`--at`/front/
  terrain flags since 4.1, so no new flags were needed.
- Gallery preset `house-slope` (14x12, `plane:64:0:1:1`, front north, medieval).

## Verification

- Before the steps, `V1PropertyTest` with the new invariant: 759 of 2000 house cases failed
  `entrance-reachable`. After: 0 (also at `-Dfb.propertyScale=5`).
- `fractal-cli check --seeds 0..40` on four lot sizes x plain/medieval: 0 violations on `plane` slopes
  1:1 in x, z and diagonal, 2 blocks per column, 1 block per 2 columns, and noise. Fails only at 3:1
  diagonal (beyond any maxGrade; the flight needs longer than the facade ring).
- `InstancePassV1Test`: 7 slopes x 4 fronts x 6 seeds all clean; steps on > 1/3 of them, none on flat;
  removing the flight (steps and their foundations) makes the invariant fire in all 19 scenes tried; a
  lifted-slab case; height cap tests.
- Goldens: `house.txt` regenerated (17 of 32 lines changed, the sloped cases).
- Viewer: `viewer/out/gallery/house-slope-front-left.png` (after) shows a one-wide brown stair running
  diagonally along the front facade down to the grass; the before render (run-phase4 CLI) shows doors
  hanging on the stone plinth with no way down.
- `:planner-core:test :cli:test` pass; `npm test` 58 pass.

## Notes

- Houses keep `maxGrade` 1 (no Deviation needed): they get steps even at 2 blocks per column.
- Types with front margin 0 get no steps (the outside cell is not in the lot); type steps pick margins >= 1.
