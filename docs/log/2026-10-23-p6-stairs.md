# STAIRS: interior flights one step short

John (playtest, high-rises): the walkable staircase ends one block before the next floor.

## Cause

`InteriorPass.flight` built steps i = 0..h-2 (h - 1 steps) for a storey of h blocks. A stair block at y is left at its high half, y + 1; the floor
above has its top at y0 + h + 1; the last step was at y0 + h - 1, so the walker stood at y0 + h: a one-block rise. Every interior flight in every type
(house, shop, workshop, hall, tower, block, high-rise, bastion, wall towers, temple) had it. Not the raster: `Shapes.stair` gives a stair
whenever a higher block is ahead; the old last step had the slab ahead, so it was a stair too. The question about a full block only matters for the new design (below).

## Fix

- `InteriorPass.flight`: h steps, the last in the slab layer (its top is flush with the next floor). The walker leaves it sideways onto the
  landing of the next flight (the old arrival cell sat next to that landing anyway), so `Core.length` stays h + 1: no massing, fixture or statistics change.
  The slab cut stays over the other h - 1 columns. The opening box now covers h columns and h rows.
- `Shapes.stair`: the top cell of the last column has nothing higher ahead; with an ascent hint it now stays a stair facing up the flight (it would
  have fallen to Shape.NONE, a full block, and the step before it would be a jump again). Buried cells keep the old rules.
- New invariant `stair-rise` (`check/StairRise.java`, `Invariant.STAIR_RISE`, in `Invariants.check` for genVersion 2, so it is in city-check, `check` and V1PropertyTest):
  along each STAIR opening, from the floor before to the floor after, entry y + 1/2 and exit y + 1 per stair block, at most half a block per step; off the last step
  onto a plain floor block level with it (straight on or sideways).

## Numbers (V1PropertyTest, all types, test lots, before the fix with the new check)

Failing cases: bastion 120/120, block 240/240, shop 1600/1600, highrise 48/48, wall 480/480, hall 161/200, tower 813/1000, house 879/2000, workshop 423/1600, temple 62/480 (first violation in each, mostly "rise of 2 half blocks"). After: 0 in all types.
`StairRiseTest`: 9 types x 2 styles x 12 seeds pass; control (remove the last step) fails every time; raster test (top cell of every step column is a stair facing up the flight, incl. the last) fails when the Shapes rule is removed.

## Goldens

`GoldenDigestV1Test` digests changed for every type with stairs: bastion, block, highrise, shop, wall 32/32; hall 28, tower 29, house 17, workshop 6, temple 1 of 32 (only the cases with an upper floor). The new last step box and the changed opening box are the only diffs; nothing else moved (lots, massing, rooms are the same: core length unchanged). Determinism holds (same digests twice, the golden test). `city-check` on the 9 gate windows (`--scenes auto:2`): 0 failing. `:mod:runGameTest` passes.
