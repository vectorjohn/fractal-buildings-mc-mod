# ORDERSIM R3 and S5: the road seam and SC4 roads

Branch `claude/p6-ordersim-s5`. Details: `docs/plans/ordersim.md`, "S5 and R3 as built".

- **R3**: `RoadFeature.placeIn(level, planner, chunkPos)`, a pure move of everything `place` did after the dimension and planner checks; `draw` made static. `OrderSim.roadStep` now calls it (the copied pre-draw lines are gone, no drift). Production behaviour unchanged.
- **VillageRoads**: needs a `WorldGenRegion`, so it is off in the sim (non-region level). Structure starts remain step S8.
- **SC4** (`OrderSimRoadsTest`, `RoadWorld`): region planner over `w12345-river-s8` layered on `world-12345-s64` (the window alone is too small to decide any settlement), densest 4 x 4 window with road and district columns, hashed k = 3 plus 4 scripted crowns over the road. C1 to C3, C5, C6 pass in 6 orders; the road digest is the same in all (1597 road cells).
- **Checks added**: road cells are set apart from the reference raster, checked against the ledger (C3) and by digest across orders (C2); C5 rule for `RoadFeature`. `SimWorld` primes the WG heightmaps (they were primed lazily, order dependent).
- **Liveness and mutants**: `roadLeaves` removes leaves, the road clear takes leaves, the ground scan passes a planted leaf and log (36 of 46 columns); `RoadFeature.MUTANT_GROUND` is caught (10 failures, C2 road digest); a scan that stops at leaves fails the scan test; `roadLeaves` off fails only the liveness count.
- **Cost**: 1.4 to 1.8 s planner + plan, 1.1 to 2.9 s for six orders, whole class about 12 s. Under 5 s, so no slow tag.
