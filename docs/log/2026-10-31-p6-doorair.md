# 6.32 DOORAIR: door steps claim street headroom

Branch `claude/p6-doorair`, 2026-10-31.

## Finding

STEPBURY (6.13) already claims air over every tread, flight end and door-outside cell as AIR site boxes (`InstancePassV1.headroom`),
but only `HEADROOM = 3` cells, while a street claims 4 (`Grading.HEADROOM`). A step is its column's top solid, so a crown could
stand at step+4 (FOLIAGEFAIL, 6.26). The claim already travels in the building's piece (raster), so `BuildingAirClear`
and the ledger see it in every chunk: no mod change needed, order independence is the same as for any building air.

## Fix

`InstancePassV1.HEADROOM` 3 -> 4 (planner only). The boxes still go only where the shell leaves the cell free.
