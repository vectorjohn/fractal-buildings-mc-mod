## Done
- `TeleportHold` (TPHOLD, the `/tp` hold): releases only when the destination 3 x 3 is FULL. `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT_MILLIS` stays 60 s (John's choice) and is now a stuck-generation limit: at it the hold is dropped, the player (never moved while held) stays where they were, gets the chat message "Destination still generating; teleport cancelled, try again", and the ticket goes. `testReleased` keeps its signature but `timedOut` is always false; new `testAborted` hook.
- `PortalHold` (6.30 PORTALCOLD): `DEFAULT_MAX_HOLD_MILLIS` 60 s (was 120 s); at it `abort` drops the request and its tickets, marks the entity in `CANCELLED` (the gate then answers "do not go" while it stays in the portal; cleared once it has left), and tells a player. Vanilla's `portalProcess` is not touched (vanilla reads it right after the wrapped call; nulling it NPEs). `lastStats` always says `timedOut=false`.
- Both tickets now expire after 3 minutes (were 60 s and 150 s): a ticket as short as the limit would unload the chunks under the hold.
