# LONGDECK with DECKDIAG steps 1-2 (F1, F2)

2026-11-02, branch `claude/p6-longdeck`. John: long approach decks for towns and up only. Coordinator folded in [DECKDIAG](../plans/deckdiag.md) F1 and F2 (same lines of `Profile.approach` and `routeNodes`) plus the architect's angle clause.

## What changed

- `Profile.approach(.., maxRun)`: hamlet and village approaches use `MAX_CROSSING` (20) as the longest deck, town and up `MAX_DECK_RUN` (100) (`WorldPlanner`, both `Profile.approach` calls).
- F1: a station is wet when water lies under the road's own width (every block of floor(hw) each side) within half a station along (was 3 lines, +-8).
- F2: `Profile.alongBank` is now a crossing test: (a) at least half the centre line over the run is water; (b) at deep (>=2) stations the water reaches past 8 blocks on both sides at least half the time, and there is one; (c) the longest unbroken centre-line water stretch is at most 1.5x the water's reach across the road (left plus right, each to 40) at its middle. `routeNodes` scores `(longRuns + alongRuns) * 1000 + wetFailures` and returns null on an along run, so it is dropped (and step a, another exit gate, still runs).
- Fixtures: `pt1709-{lake-sm11_m2,neck-sm4_m6,bay-s8_10}-s4.json.gz` (the probe crops, 250 KB), `JoinWorld.deckdiag()`.
