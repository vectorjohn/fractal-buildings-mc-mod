# RAILFIX: railings that hold (6.9 defect)

**Defect (John playtest 2026-10-25).** Rails were placed at the tread's own y on a kerb to `h - 1`: top 0.5 above the walking surface, and a player steps 0.6, so a rail could be walked onto and off.

**Fix.** `InstancePassV1.rails`: kerb of foundation now reaches the tread's walking surface (`h + 1 - g` high) and the `RAIL` block stands on it at `h + 1` (fence collision 1.5, so its top is 1.5 above the feet). Still one rail per free cell beside a tread where the ground is at least `RAIL_DROP_*` below, still highest adjacent tread decides, so rails step up and down with a flight. New: no rail in front of any leaf of a wide (double) door (`doorFront`); before, the old rail level was below the door so it did not matter, and `RasterOwnershipTest.doorsAndStairsFaceTheRightWay` caught the second leaf walled in.

**Walk model.** `Invariants.walkFloor(Role)` (public): solid but not `FENCE` or `RAIL`; used by `standing` and `walkStanding`, so the top of a rail or parapet fence is not a floor.

**Tests.** `RailingTest`: `railsStandOneBlockAboveTheSurfaceAndLeaveNoGap` (every rail is exactly one block above an adjacent tread's surface; every open edge beside a tread with drop >= 2, in the lot, outside the shell and not in front of a door has a rail; 541 rails and 50+ edges over 5 types x 7 slopes x 8 seeds), `aRailTopIsNotStandable`; the beside/kerb/drop checks moved up by one. Mutations: rail back at `h` fails the two height tests; walkFloor allowing `RAIL` fails `aRailTopIsNotStandable`. Golden v1 digests regenerated (rail boxes moved; expected in every type that has an exterior flight). `city-check --seeds 1 --sites gates:fixtures/terrain/gate-sites.tsv --scenes auto:2`: 9 sites, 0 violations.

**Pictures.** `docs/review/phase6/railfix/*-after2.png` (gallery front-left of shop-slope-deep, house-slope, tower-medieval) against `docs/review/phase6/railings/*-after.png`: rails read one block taller beside flights; no other change.

**Not done.** City bridges still have no rails (road and ground deck code, which CITYQ is working in; needs its own item). Balconies, lookouts' own platforms and wall walks have no rails either (no such pieces exist yet).

## Round 2 (architect and verifier findings)

- **Lot line:** `steps` prefers routes whose open sides lie inside the lot: pass 0 only landings and flights with no unrailable lot-line side, pass 1 flights only (a stoop landing may stay on the lot line), pass 2 any route (before: always). Rails cannot leave the lot (`in-lot` invariant), so a one-row margin cannot be guarded: *Known issue:* a shop whose only margin row is the lot line keeps an unguarded landing on the street side (needs a setback from the lot or the city planner).
- **Corner posts:** a free cell with a rail on both its x and z sides gets a post (no 0.75 gap between diagonal rails).
- **Stepped rails:** a rail takes the level of the highest tread within one cell (8 neighbours), so its top is 1.5 above every tread it can be stepped from.
- **Doors:** no rail in front of any exterior door at the rail's height, any storey.
- **Not done (4):** wide doors or gates over a 1-wide flight keep an unguarded drop beside the flight; a full-width landing is the real fix (own item).
- **Tests:** `RailingTest` checks rail y >= every adjacent tread y + 1, no diagonal gaps (29 corners), every open edge guarded including lot-line edges (non-shop types all; shop lot-line misses counted below a bound). Mutations: no corners, no raise, no strict pass each fail it. Goldens regenerated; planner-core 699 pass; `check --seeds 0..29` 141120 cases 0 failing; city-check 9 sites 0 violations.
