# 2026-10-21 p5-d1-villages (5.1 part: hamlet and village programs)

**Done.** Hamlets and villages now get their own tier programs; towns and up still use `DefaultProgram` (D2, D3).

- `core/settlement/tiers/`: `StraightProgram` (shared), `HamletProgram`, `VillageProgram`; `core/settlement/TierPrograms.of(tier)` picks the program and is used by `Skeletons.build/plan` (5-arg overloads) and `SkeletonSource.Direct` (4-arg constructor). D2 and D3 add their tiers to that switch.
- Main street: one exact straight axis-aligned line through the hub (two-point roads, no A*), on the gentler of the two axes (mean height change per block over the walkable run; hash on a tie). The walk is in 8-block steps while the column is in the cell (`CellGeometry.owns`, exact), urban and not steeper than 10 blocks per 32. District detail only lines straight runs of 7 or more columns with frontage lots, so an A* road (the default) gave the first test hamlet 0 lots.
- Hamlet: PATH, width 3, 22 to 40 blocks long (hashed), lopsided about the hub if one side runs out of ground; no landmarks, `lanes()` and `ports()` off, so the only street is the track. Village: MAIN width 4, up to 2 x (50 + radius/5) blocks, a cross street on 50 % meeting the main street 40 to 90 blocks from the hub (nudged off tile borders), a green at the street start with a temple (70 %) or hall (30 %) on a side off the street axis, frontage lanes kept, ports off.
- New hooks, defaults unchanged for other tiers: `TierProgram.lanes()` and `ports()` (Districts skips `frontageLanes` / `ports`), `DefaultProgram.sideAt`, `villageFace`, `landmarkMargin`.
- `StraightProgram.anchor`: a hub on or near a tile border moves the street start (and the green) to 28 (village) or 12 (hamlet) blocks inside the tile; tries the tile on the other side of a border too. Without it about 20 % of villages had the green clamped off the street and 6/200 had a hall lot hit by a port stub (the cause of every `roads-connected` and `lots-disjoint` hit in the first runs). `flattest` slides a village green along the street (up to 48 blocks) to the flattest coarse ground, since district detail moves a green it cannot grade.

**Verification.**
- `SmallTiersTest` (200 sites per tier, three synthetic terrains incl. a river): hamlet 3 to 8 buildings (observed 3..8, median 5), no landmarks or plazas, one straight PATH line; village 10 to 60 (observed 15..58, median 29), one green, exactly one temple or hall lot flush against it with its front toward it, never on a street, temple 70/30, cross street on 55 to 145 of 200, roads straight and 4 wide; `CityPlanner.check` has 0 violations and 0 stray fragments for all 400; byte-identical on re-plan. Swapping the tiers back to `DefaultProgram` fails 6 of the 7 tests (mutation check).
- `city-check --seeds 1..6 --sites random:60` on two synthetic terrains, no `--ignore`: 720 sites, 390 hamlets or villages, 1 village violation (`roads-connected`, see below); the rest are the known town and city ones.
- Presets `hamlet-river`, `village-river` (T1 river window), `hamlet-mixed`, `village-mixed` (mixed window): `npm run city-gallery` passes (10 tests); plan PNGs and a 3D district opened. Hamlet: a short track with houses and a workshop each side. Village: main street with a cross street, green with a temple or hall, lanes and houses along both. `e2e/city.spec.ts` now needs only more than 0 planned districts for a hamlet (it has 2).

**Known issues.** About 1 in 400 villages on rugged ground: district detail cannot grade the green, moves it sideways (rings of 16 blocks, perpendicular tried first) off the street, so the hall lot facing it is a separate piece (`roads-connected`, in `CityCheckTest.KNOWN`). KNOWN is not widened. A real fix is a Districts change (try shifts along the street first), left to the HUB/ROADS owners.
Hamlet building types come from the ClassTable (an occasional shop or tower at density under 25); D3 tunes that table.
