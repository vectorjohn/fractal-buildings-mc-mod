# P6 CITYQ: sparse and "ocean" cities in the B9 fly-through

Diagnosis of the two generation problems in the B9 frames ([flythrough](../review/phase6/flythrough/README.md)), then three
planner fixes. Pictures: [review/phase6/cityq/](../review/phase6/cityq/README.md).

1. **12345 city is not on the sea.** Its centre is on land (snowy taiga and plains at y 63 to 72, frozen rivers); the disk is 17 %
   water by the coarse raster (22 % on the 64-block world dump), under every cap. The B9 edge camera stands 40 blocks outside the
   radius on the side away from the origin, east, which is open frozen sea, and with render distance 10 it sees only the outer 160
   blocks of the disk: sea. The city was sparse for a different reason (3).
2. **4 of the 6 city/metropolis fly-through targets were dropped join claimants.** A city or metropolis whose claim loses to a
   higher-priority neighbour decides alone with the grow clamp (at most half a cell, then 0.95 of the edge distance) but kept its

   of B9 hit them. On the three world dumps: 2 of 4 metropolises on 12345, 1 of 4 on seed 1, 2 of 14 cities on 987654321.
3. **The street lattice cut whole urban tiles off the hub.** A plain lattice street needed 2 of 3 sample points (ends, middle)
   "urban" (which includes not steep by the 4-neighbour 3-in-4 test) and a rise of at most 1 in 4; tiles reach the hub only through

   drops every street not rooted on the live network, and the lots with them.
4. **Seed 1's site is poor ground.** A quarter of its disk is steeper than 3 in 4 and the hub tile is 17 of 30 samples steep; the
   region screen measures water and peaks but not slope. That is real terrain, not a bug.

Also seen: the live game put seed 1 cell -1,3's centre at -768,3776 (candidate 6), the recorded world dump at -1024,3968
(candidate 2); 9 other sites matched. A live-versus-recorded region probe difference, not followed up here.

## Fixes

- **Tier fits radius** (`CellDecision.sizedAlone`, `Tiers.forRadius`, in `FieldRegionView.compute`): a city or metropolis that
  decides alone and ends below its band takes the largest tier whose band starts at or below its radius. Winners keep tier and drawn
  radius; nothing else changes (centre, radius, port tag, roll). Join goldens (synthetic `RegionTestWorld`): exactly 5 cells changed, all
  tier only (12345: metropolis r150 to village, metropolis r276, city r343 and r228 to town; 987654321: metropolis r226 to town).
  On the real dumps the 4 dropped claimants above become towns. Road widths between settlements follow (roads fixtures regenerated).
- **Lattice reaches rolling ground** (`RoadNetwork.streetPoint`, `STREET_STEEP_PER_32` 8 to 16): a plain lattice street counts
  points that are inside, dry and not a peak (slope is judged once, by the grade test along the street) and may rise 1 in 2
  (arterials keep 3 in 4). Each change alone gained little (517 to 527 and 549 lots); together 790.
- **Corner-only plazas** (`Districts.dropFloatingPlazas`): a plaza a street meets only diagonally reaches no road, as the city's
  4-connected `roads-attached` flood sees it. The denser lattice exposed one on the steep synthetic set (seed 9 town, a market
  square moved off rough ground).

## Not done, proposals

- **Screen big claimants at their drawn radius.** The region screen measures water on the grow-clamped disk (512) even for a
  claimant that will take 800 to 2500 blocks (ASSUMPTIONS MC1 row). On the 64-block world dumps, surviving metropolises reach 47
  to 50 % water on their real disk (seed 1 cells -8,1 and 10,4, 12345 7,-3, 987654321 2,-2 = gate-metropolis-mixed) against 6 to
  20 % at 512. Proposed rule: a claimant's footprint is sampled at min(drawn, `footprintCap`); not done here because it widens every
  claimant's probe reach (`probeReach`, `FieldRegionView.readRadius`, window coverage of dumps and the mod's region reads).
- **Slope in the region screen.** Seed 1's site shows a tier can land on ground a quarter steep; a steep share in the footprint
  (4-neighbour test on the 64 lattice) would be the next lever. It changes every decision and all goldens; for a later item.

## Round 2 (verifier: client test `TerrainClientGameTest.runWithoutRoads` failed)

The B3 slope pick moved to tile 3,-2 of seed 12345 (relief 122), which has streets and lots only since the lattice change. Two
failures there, both reproduced and explained:

1. **"76 footprint columns with air under the floor", shop `d3_m2.l5`.** Reproduced offline with live vanilla probes in a `:mod:test`
   probe (bootstrapped like `TerrainDumpTool`): the shop is a **market hall** (5.12), the whole building on a row of posts, raised
   floor at y 100, the open floor under it bare ground at 94 to 96 with posts founded at y 93 to 95. That is the accepted design
   (REVIEW 5.12 ticked; `ShopOpenGroundTest` asserts nothing but posts below the shop floor and `ShopStoopTest` that the open floor
   is reachable from the street). A paved, founded floor under the hall was tried and broke both tests, so it was reverted. The B3
   metric took the lowest FLOOR of each column as its ground floor, which under stilts is the raised floor. Changed: for a building
   whose shell has `leg` volumes, the metric now checks the block under each post's lowest beam (what holds it up) and skips columns
   whose lowest floor is the raised floor (over the open leg zone); other buildings are checked as before. Not a relaxation for any
   building with a ground floor. Lookout towers on stilts would have hit the same false positive.
2. **"2 road columns with solid terrain within 3 blocks above", x 655.** Mossy cobblestone 1 to 2 blocks over a lattice street at
   x 655, the last column of chunk 40; the street is on the tile border 656, a chunk line. Our pieces write in the last decoration
   step, after their own chunk's features, but chunk 41 decorated later and its forest rock (a vanilla local-modification feature)
   spilled into chunk 40's already cleared street air. Every lattice street runs on a chunk line, so this class grew with CITYQ.
   Fix: `mod/.../worldgen/SpillClear`: after its own cells, each ground piece writes the planned air (CUT boxes not overridden by a later
   box) of neighbouring tiles' ground within 4 blocks outside its chunk, where the world holds something else; cells within a lot or
   its air rights are left alone (a building may own them; no building is computed). Order-independent: those cells are air in the
   plan whichever pass writes last. `WorldPlanner.prefetch` now also computes the eight neighbours' ground on the workers, so the lane
   never grades a neighbour inline (the warming game test caught the first version, which rastered through the neighbours' buildings).
   Leaves are FOLIAGE's (`FoliageClear`); this pass only rewrites planned air.

Also: comments of `CoarseTerrain.ROAD_STEEP_PER_32` / `roadSteep` and `Arterials.gradeOk` said 1 in 2 for the value 24 (3 in 4); the
comments now say what the value is (TR 5.3 apparently meant to keep 16; the value was not changed).

Not catchable offline: city-check sees no vanilla features. The stilt void was not a planner defect, so no new invariant.

## Round 4 (architect: DS-18 violation, cities taper at their built edge too)

- Merged claude/run-phase6 (f1e582f; TESTISO, RAILFIX, LOTRAIL, MINLOT). Conflicts were the district fixtures (regenerated, not merged) and the `GroundWalkTest` ratchet (kept 108 / 21; it passes).
- Finding: the lattice change opened a west quarter of the 12345 coast city (x about 630, r 500 to 550). It ended in ten 15 to 17 storey high-rises with nothing built beyond, because the class comes from the radial density field only.
- `BuiltEdge` (planner-core): the depth of the built-up area outward from a point, along three rays (straight out and 30 degrees either side), sampled every 16 blocks.
  - A sample counts as built-up only if all of these hold:
    - its coarse mask column is URBAN or OPEN (from the skeleton of whichever settlement cell owns it);
    - its tile is planned, with a drawn lattice segment on its border (the street network reaches it);
    - the coarse ground rises at most 8 blocks from the previous sample.
  - A run ends at a gap of more than 32 blocks.
  - `Districts` caps the class density of street lots and block lots with it: under 96 blocks no high-rise (69), under 128 no tower (84). The quarter still reads the field density.
  - It is settlement-wide data, so tiles stay order-independent.
  - Each condition came from a case:
    - mask alone: 13 edge towers became 6 (mid-rises at the edge, mask 150 blocks past where streets go);
    - reached tiles: 3;
    - contiguous run: 1, but 15 on the gate windows (exact relief empties tiles the coarse mask calls buildable);
    - coarse slope: 12 at the strict thresholds.
- Check: `SettlementChecks.edgeLots` / `cityEdgeTaper` finds high-rises with no built lot within 120 blocks outward (45 degrees).
  - It is reported as a city-check design warning, not a violation.
  - `EdgeTaperTest` holds the count: coast city 2 (13 without the cap), gate windows 15 (38).
  - A unit case covers the cone, the reach and the type filter.
  - Mutation: cap off, and both fixture tests fail with 13 and 38.
- Cost and gain:
  - High-rises on the gate windows 823 to 722 (cities lose the most, e.g. snow city 8 to 6; metropolises about 10 %).
  - Coast city high-rises 81 to 47, built lots 794 to about 1016 (smaller types pack more lots).
  - Gate window lots 8845 to 9228. `city-check` on the gate windows: 9 sites, 0 failing, design warnings edge-taper 15.
- Picture: `docs/review/phase6/cityq/c12345-city-r4-height.jpg` (plan with heights). The west quarter's outer streets now hold low lots.
- `BuiltEdge` fetches the road network once per instance. `SettlementDeterminismTest`'s evicting source rebuilds it on every call, and the first version (once per tile it looked at) kept that test running for over an hour.
- Also: the two B9 NOTES lines moved from "Baselines" to "Tests and known flakes".

## Round 7 (diagnosis of the r6 B2 failure)

- The r6 client run (started 07:59 on 998d41d, failed 08:42) predates r6's last commit (7f1958e, 08:46), so its log has neither the `lake` class nor the unknown-solid-cell lines. Its 5 failing orders each had `cobblestone->stone [unknown]=3`; the other 2 orders had none, so the cause depends on chunk order.
- Checked r6's explanation against 26.3: `data/minecraft/worldgen/feature/lake_lava.json` has barrier `stone` and `can_replace_with_barrier` = not `#lava_pool_stone_cannot_replace` (bedrock, spawner, chest, end portal frame, reinforced deepslate, trial spawner, vault, leaves, logs); `LakeFeature.place` (javap) sets the barrier on solid shell blocks that pass it and has no structure check. Cobblestone passes. A lake from a neighbour chunk decorated after ours therefore turns our cobblestone into stone, and only in orders where that neighbour comes later. The only other 26.3 feature configs naming `minecraft:stone` (glow_lichen, spring_lava_overworld, spring_water, sculk_vein) list it as a block to attach to or sit in, not one they place, and SpillClear writes only air, so this is a vanilla spill, not our write.
- No code change in r7: 7f1958e's classifier (stone with lava within 3 blocks = `lake`) is the fix; the r7 client run is its first run. NOTES has the lake fact.
- Then FOLIAGE landed. Its verifier found 2 street and 3 road cells of natural leaves left in the headroom on seed 1: the crowns of standing neighbour trees that grew after the chunk was cleared. Neither pass removed them, because SpillClear left all trees alone. Round 7 changes:
  - `SpillClear.run` now also removes natural (non-persistent) leaves in planned street air, up to `LEAF_MARGIN` = 12 blocks past the chunk (rocks still 4). Logs stay, because of the trunk dirt.
  - New `SpillClear.roadLeaves` (from `RoadFeature`, every chunk with roads on) removes natural leaves in the 4 headroom cells (`col.y()` .. +3) of the 8 neighbour chunks' road columns, skipping district columns. `RoadSurface.plan` sets the road surface from the column alone, so every chunk computes the same cells.
  - Order: SpillClear now runs *before* FoliageClear in `TilePiece` and `RoadFeature` (the brief said after). FoliageClear removes every unsupported cell within 6 blocks, whoever cut it, so running it second also removes anything the leaf cuts left hanging. FoliageClear only ever writes air, so SpillClear has nothing to redo after it.
  - `FoliageClientGameTest` now asserts headroom == 0. Its control pass also turns the leaf clear off (`SpillClear.LEAF_CLEAR`), which gives the check a signal.
- Client run on c6836a8 (run-phase6 79e1d009 merged), whole suite exit 0:
  - B2: 7 orders, one digest f7a62bf749da, 0 unknown solid cells. `cobblestone->stone [lake]=3` in 5 orders. Air bad 110-835 (r6: 914 in forward). 6 of 6 mutants caught.
  - FOLIAGE seed 1: headroom street 43 -> 0 and road 17 -> 0 (control vs on), orphans 0. Seed 31337: headroom 0 in the control too, so that seed gives the check no signal.
  - B3: all six tiles headroom 0 and floating 0.
