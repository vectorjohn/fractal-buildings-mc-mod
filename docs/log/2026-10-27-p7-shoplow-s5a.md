# 7.17 SHOPLOW S5a: every buried cell is claimed

Test only; no generator change, no golden moved.

- `BuriedRasterTest` (planner-core, package `gen.v1` for access to `DigInTest.cases`): for every volume of a dug-in building, each cell from
  local y 1 up to the old ground (`raw floorY - 1`) must be emitted by `PieceRaster.building` (solid, or air from a room part or site box).
- Cases: `CutFixture.scene(true)` (3-deep cut), and `DigInTest.cases` for shop, house and workshop on `plane:64:0:1:{4,6,9}`, four fronts,
  three lots, 3 seeds: 288 cases, 57 bury something, 3674 buried cells, none unclaimed.
- Controls (the check can fail): the fixture with no emitted cells reports >40 unclaimed; a house with its room parts removed from the shell
  leaves cells unclaimed in >=5 cases (the room-part clear is what claims the interior).
- Finding: dropping the cut's `AIR` site boxes leaves nothing unclaimed in these cases (0 of the house cases), because inside the footprint the
  shell's solid boxes and room parts already cover every cell. So S2's cut builder needs no extra footprint `AIR` boxes; the cut's footprint runs
  (`foot` branch in `SiteLevel.cut`) are redundant here but harmless. `PieceRaster` untouched.
- Not covered: the real `Pieces.deepen` / Beardifier shelf in the world (S5b).
