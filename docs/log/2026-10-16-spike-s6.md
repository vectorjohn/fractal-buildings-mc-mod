# S6 scale and cost spike

Branch `claude/spike-s6` (from `claude/spike-s3`, run-phase2 already merged). Findings: `docs/research/spikes/s6.md`. Throwaway code: `mod/src/main/java/dev/fractalbuildings/spike6/`, tests `mod/src/gametest/.../S6ClientGameTest.java`, `S6Run.java`, driver `spike-s6-run.sh`, pure runs `S6Pure`.

- Built a stand-in generator (boxes IR, calibrated CPU spin: 1 / 50 / 200 ms), district profiles village..dense, a two-level plan (skeleton + district) and a monolithic variant, a memoised IR/plan cache with DONE/INFLIGHT/INLINE classification and prefetch workers, a simulated moving player, a suitability decision and a `/locate` path.
- Ran about 60 fresh-world experiments under the heavy lock (loadavg 5-20). Lane stats repeat within 3%; wall times are noisy.
- Main numbers: exact height 0.15-0.38 ms; monolithic plan 0.5 s per Mblock² at city density (34 s at 8192²); two-level skeleton 83 ms at 4096², district 40-100 ms constant; postProcess never concurrent (serial lane); downtown cold 6.5 s lane against 0.5 s warm; lead about 26 blocks of player travel; prefetch with 4 workers left 0 inline lookups at elytra speed; 13 B per reference piece; evict/regenerate byte-exact incl. live heights; suitability 12-17 ms coarse, 140-185 ms exact; `/locate` 0.1-0.3 s coarse against 3-34 s exact.
- Surprises: a thrashing cache rebuilds a whole district plan per piece lookup (needs briefs in pieces); coarse `chunk_surface_level` reads 16.5 blocks low and without correction vetoed most land; the first land search was too strict (relaxed).
- Mutation checks: IR counter and probe counter fail the pure compare (123-2,059 and 181-2,896 mismatches) and the IR counter fails the in-world compare (43 of 885 chunks).
- Build files: none changed. Spike edits outside spike6: gametest `fabric.mod.json` (client entrypoint), `FractalBuildingsMod` (init), `spike3/CityGrid` (S3 placement now off unless `S3_ORDERS` set, so S3 cities do not appear in S6 worlds).
- Not done: see "Not done or not conclusive" in the findings.
