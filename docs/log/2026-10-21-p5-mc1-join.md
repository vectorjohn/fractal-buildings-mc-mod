# 2026-10-21 P5 MC1: join rule (region half of 5.8)

Added `core/region/FieldRegionView` (the join rule, ADR 0003 R2 one round), `CellKey`, `SettlementSite`;
`CellDecision.withRadius`; `RegionMap.layer` now uses the join policy (`growLayer` keeps the old one) and
writes `claimedBy: [cx, cz]` per member cell (absent otherwise); CLI `region --policy join|grow`
(default join). `RegionPlanner` is untouched (it stays the grow decider, used for cells that decide alone).

Rule: claimant = settled decision with drawn radius > 0.4 S (checked from the hash first, so only large
tiers are ever decided); claims every cell whose site is within the drawn radius of its centre; survives
unless an overlapping claimant (dropped ones count) has higher priority (`Hash64 "region/priority"`,
unsigned, ties to lower (cx, cz)). Cell result: member of the unique surviving claimant, else its own
grow decision; a surviving owner's radius is the drawn one. Bounded read: claimants within R of the cell,
rivals within 2R more, so `readRadius()` ~ 3R + a little (13.6k at R = 4096). Caches memoise only (D6).

Verification (all run):
- `:planner-core:test :cli:test` pass. New `FieldRegionViewTest`: each cell alone on a fresh view,
  shuffled order, and 8 threads on a shared view all equal the batch (2 seeds); a brute-force global
  reference implementation of the rule agrees on 25x25 cells x 3 seeds (also: no two survivors overlap,
  dropped claimants' cells are members only of a winner, winner radius = drawn); winners reach past the
  old 512 clamp; a probe that counts reads outside `readRadius()` of the cell's site sees none; small
  `maxRadius` reproduces grow exactly; golden `golden/region/join-*.txt`.
- Mutation: scanning claimants only to half the distance made 3 tests fail. Lowering `readRadius` to
  2R + slack was NOT caught (the test worlds rarely have a rival chain that reaches 3R; the bound is an
  analytic upper bound, tested as such).
- Region maps: ran the CLI on both world dumps (501 and 508 cells) and looked at a Java PNG and the V0
  page screenshot (`viewer/out/region/world-12345.png`): large cities and metropolises are circles of
  their drawn radius spanning cells, with white dashed claim links to member cell sites (20 claims in
  seed 12345). Settled owners wider than their cell's fit: 18 / 16.
- Viewer fixtures `viewer/fixtures/region/world-*.json.gz` regenerated with join; `region.spec.ts` now
  asserts the real dumps show claims (> 5) and still passes (5 tests).
- Cost (flat plains, synthetic probe, loadavg ~16.8 busy): cold single-cell resolve 0.7-1.1 ms and
  17k-28k probe columns; 1681 cells batch 92 ms (0.05 ms/cell, caches shared). Live estimate above.

Known / open: window-edge cells differ from the unbounded world (documented in the class); caches
are unbounded (the mod should bound or key them per world); R2's water bias will change decisions and the
goldens (regenerate). ROADMAP 5.8 is ticked by MC2.
