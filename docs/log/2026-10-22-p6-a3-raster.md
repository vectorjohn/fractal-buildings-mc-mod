# P6 A3: cell raster, ownership and block shapes

Branch `claude/p6-a3-raster` (plan step A3 of [phase6](../plans/phase6.md); there is no ROADMAP checkbox for the A steps).

## What

`planner-core/.../core/raster/`, pure Java:

- `PieceRaster.building(all, index, clip, sink)` and `.ground(boxes, buildings, StyleAt, clip, sink)`: boxes into a grid
  over `clip` grown by 1, emit cells inside `clip` in (y, z, x) order with role, block id and `Shape`.
- `Shape` (record: `facing`, `half`, `hinge`, `axis`, `connect`; `Shape.NONE`), `Shapes` (doors, stairs, panes, fences,
  logs), `Variant` (palette pick by `cellHash`, port of the viewer's), `StyleAt` (ground style: nearest building, as the
  viewer's `styleLocator`), `CellSink`.
- Ownership rule written in `docs/IR.md` ("Cell ownership"): building owns touched cells (solid or air clear), ground
  owns the rest of its cells, earlier building wins between buildings.

## Verified

- `RasterOwnershipTest` (gate-temperate-city and gate-border-desert-jungle, the 3 biggest districts each, 6 scenes, 543k
  cells): no cell emitted twice; building cells equal an independent `VoxelGrid` composition; ground cells equal a
  brute-force composition; no gaps; raster over 16x16 chunk clips in shuffled order is identical to the whole (shapes
  too); two runs equal; door cells: lower/upper by the cell below, walkable front and back; stair shapes face a solid
  higher cell; a full-block step never has a higher step beside it.
  Numbers: 462 door cells (231 upper), 214 stair shapes, 832 full-block steps, 10,597 log axes; 0 ground cells yielded
  to buildings and 87 building air clears on these cities (buildings and ground are disjoint there; the rule is tested on
  a synthetic overlap in `OwnershipTest`).
- `ShapesTest`: stairs (with and without an opening) and doors (single and 2-wide hinges), fences, panes and log axes
  at all 4 rotations.
- `VariantTest` + viewer `cellhash.test.ts`: 50 rows from the TS function.
- Mutations: ground ignoring buildings failed `OwnershipTest`; mirrored left/right failed `ShapesTest`.
- Look: `planner-core/build/raster/district-N.png` (written by `writeOwnershipPictures`): left role colours, right owner
  colours with door (red) and stair (yellow) facing ticks. Looked at district-1: roads and buildings disjoint, buildings
  one colour each, stair/door ticks on facades.

## Decisions and notes

- Full-block steps are legitimate: the top step is level with the floor slab (no higher cell), so it stays
  `Shape.NONE`; A4 should map only stair cells with a `facing` to stair blocks.
- Interiors are not air boxes, so a building clears nothing but its explicit `air` boxes (87 cells here). The mod must
  not rely on the raster to clear terrain inside buildings.
- Connections are computed inside a piece only: a fence in a building does not connect to a ground cell beside it.
- `CellSink` lives in `core.raster` (A2's plan lists it under `core.world`; A2 should import it).
- Slabs not done ("if cheap"). `rail` (6.9) is not merged: `FENCE` carries the connecting shape; add `RAIL` to
  `Shapes.of` beside `FENCE` when the role lands.
