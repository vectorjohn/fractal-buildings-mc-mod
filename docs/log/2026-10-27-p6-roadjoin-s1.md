# 6.15 ROADJOIN steps 1 and 2: heights at the join, approaches follow the water rule

Branch `claude/p6-roadjoin-s1` (from `claude/p6-roadjoin`), plan [roadjoin.md](../plans/roadjoin.md).

## Done
- Step 1: `WorldPlanner.streetLevel(site, x, z, extra)` finds the district street (nearest centre line within `width/2+1`, ties by id; else a plaza) at a column, reading the site's planned tiles around it (`Street.nearest` is public now). `approachRoads` pins station 0 (exit reach 6) and each following station that lies on a street (up to 24) to that level; the port pin stays last. `Profile.approachDeck` takes a pin map; `Profile.steepJoins` counts neighbouring stations `limit()` could not join (`Approach.steepJoins`).
- Step 2: `Profile.approach(st, exact, pins, width)`: a station is wet when water lies within 8 blocks along it over the road's width (+1); crossings merge as in `build`; length is the real wet extent; the deck is flat at the higher bank but at least water+1 (water+2 when the span is over 6, `HIGH_SPAN`); a pinned station inside a crossing keeps its level; ramps are 1 in 4 by `limit()`. `span` flags make crossing columns DECK in the raster. When a crossing is over 20 long, `WorldPlanner` reroutes (up to `RoadNet.MAX_REROUTES`): bans the 4 lattice nodes round the crossing, lattice search on the coarse probe (water forbidden, grade limit), box of exit and port + 128. The attempt with the fewest `wetFailures` is kept; water is never filled.
