# D2: town program, walls and gates

2026-10-21, branch `claude/p5-d2-town` (plan `docs/plans/phase5.md`, step D2).

## What was done

- **`tiers/TownProgram`** (selected by `TierPrograms.of`): `exitCount` 3 to 5 on the centre cell (radial main roads), everything else from `DefaultProgram`
  (market square and a hall facing it, frontage streets). On `WALL_PCT` = 40 % of towns (hash of seed and centre) it asks `WallRing` for a ring.
- **`tiers/WallRing`** (pure functions, D3 reuses them): `ring` (rectangle 0.5 to 0.7 x radius round the hub; each side pulled in on its own, down to 0.3 of
  that, until every centreline column and the lines 6 blocks beside it are `ok`, 70 % urban, at least 100 across; coordinates are multiples of the lattice step and
  30 blocks from every tile line), `gates` (where the hub-to-target line leaves the ring, slid along the side to the nearest place where all lots still fit),
  `pieces` (the lots, clockwise from the NW tower), `crossings` (reads gates back from final roads), `blocked`/`band` helpers and id/shape predicates.
- **Roads through gates**: a road to a target outside the ring is routed in three parts (hub to 16 inside the gate, a straight run through, then on to the target).
  `TierProgram.walls(c, hub, roads)` is a new hook (default: the old `walls(c, hub)`, no gates); `TownProgram` reads the crossings back from the roads, emits the
  closed `Wall` and one `kind: wall` gate per crossing with its road id, or drops the whole ring if any crossing is not a straight perpendicular gate run.
- **Districts** (`wallLots`): each tile takes the ring pieces that lie inside it as `wall` / `tower` lots (ids `<district>.w<n>`), reserved so that streets are clipped at
  the lot except through the gate passage (`WallRing.blocked`); pieces of neighbouring tiles within 24 blocks are reserved too and port stubs avoid ring lots, so both sides
  of a tile border agree. Dropped lots (water, taken ground) are noted.
- **Checks/briefs**: `lots-disjoint` lets a street cross a gate lot (a `wall` lot exactly 23 long) inside its 5-column passage; `lot-frontage` skips ring lots (they face
  the field); `Briefs` and `DistrictScenes` never swap a ring lot for another type (empty instead).
- Viewer: `wall` lot colour; preset `town-walled` (world-12345 dump, seed 2, town at -797,-3872).
- Regenerated: settlement goldens `flat-town`, `recorded-river-town` (3 to 5 main roads), `river-town` skeleton and district fixtures.

## Verification

- `TownProgramTest` (60 synthetic towns over flat, noise and river terrain; 21 got a ring with 88 gates): 2 to 5 main roads and market/hall; an independent crossing check
  (every place a main road meets the ring is a wall gate on that road); ring pieces each inside one tile, lengths 23 to 64 and depths 7 to 11, one gate lot per gate, all
  present as lots; `CityPlanner.check` has no violation other than the suppressed `roads-connected`, `road-grade`, `lot-frontage`; 14 wall district scenes build with
  no violations and no fallback; every gate passage column lies on a main-road street; determinism.
- Mutations: reserving gate lots whole (no passage) -> `everyGatePassageLiesOnTheMainRoad` fails; no road stitching through the gate -> 3 tests fail (rings refused, passages
  off the road); (the crossing rule's own mutant: a plan without its gates fails `theCrossingRuleCatchesAMissingGate`).
- Looked at: `viewer/out/city/town-walled/plan.png` (ring 256 x 288 with corner towers, three orange gate markers where the white main roads cross it straight, hub market
  inside, lots outside the ring too) and `dm6_m24-overview.png` / `dm6_m25-overview.png` (crenellated gatehouses with orange gate leaves in the wall line, the road
  reaching the south gate, corner and wall towers). Scene check: 0 violations.

## Findings

- Rings are rare where the ground is wet or steep: on 341 blind town sites of the world-12345 dump 2 got one (first-failing-sample counts: water 736k, water margin 830k, steep 301k, peak 26k, not owned 3k; with steep, margin, urban and ownership switched off only 8 of ~136 drawn rings are found: the dump is mostly sea); region-planner towns about 1 in 5; none on the nine T2 gate windows (40 seeds each, tier town). Sides are pulled in independently to help. A ring that is found can still lose lots where exact water (any depth) is not on the 32-block coarse lattice (`wall lot ... dropped (wet ground)`); `WATER_MARGIN` 28 keeps rings 28 blocks from coarse water: 14 gave 0 to 13 dropped lots of ~60 on real sites and a river town with 6 gaps (and a `roads-connected` violation in the fixture), 28 gives 0 to 2.
- A 23-long wall lot has no slack, so the generator's gatehouse (towers 10 + passage 5 - 2 shared) puts the passage exactly in the middle: that is what lets the road
  meet it. Longer gate lots would put the passage at a draw.
- Hash-dependent port stubs hit ring lots at tile borders; fixed by sharing the ring reservation across the border, not by dropping the check.
