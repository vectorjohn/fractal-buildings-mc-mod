# 6.15 ROADJOIN step 4: lots off the approaches

Branch `claude/p6-roadjoin-s4` (from `p6-roadjoin-s1`, merged with s1 r3 and run-phase6).

- `Districts.Build.markApproaches`: every approach of the settlement (the curve `WorldPlanner.approachNodes` gives, width + 2, via `RoadNet.raster` on zero decks) is flagged `APPROACH` in the tile raster. `APPROACH` is part of the free masks of lots, plazas, wall lots and landmark/avenue squares (not of streets: they may cross).
- `SkeletonSource.approaches()` (default empty: the CLI without a road graph) and `Direct.withApproaches(supplier)`; `WorldPlanner.source` supplies `approachNodes(site)` lazily, so no tile reads another's plan and every tile of the settlement sees the same corridor (tile borders agree, no ordering dependence).
- `LinkExits.approach`: `URBAN_PENALTY` 30 steps-of-10 per lattice step through the urban footprint (other than the exit), so approaches leave town. Effect on k4_4_0 is small (it is routed 1015 columns vs 935; its exit is on the far side of the village; a mid-street exit is step 3/DEADMAIN territory, not touched: `exitFor`/`pick` unchanged).
