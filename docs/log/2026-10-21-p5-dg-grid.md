# P5 DG: district grid

`core/settlement/DistrictGrid` (pure, seed + position only): world-wide tile lines at chunk
`10k + o(k)`, `o` in -1..1 hashed per (seed, axis, k); tiles 8-12 chunks, chunk aligned.
API: `tileOf`, `tile(ix, iz)` (block rect `[min, max)` + origin chunk at centre), `tileAt`,
`owner` (= containing tile; a road on a line is split along it), `segments(ix, iz)` (four
border `Segment(axis, line, index)` records; `hash(seed, purpose)` is identical from both tiles).
`tileOf` is O(1): walks down from `floorDiv(chunk, 10) + 1`, at most ~3 line hashes.
`PERIOD` and `JITTER` are constants (decision 1 unconfirmed); changing them is one edit.

Tests (`DistrictGridTest`, 8): exact partition over 4096² windows at 4 offsets x 3 seeds, shared
borders, 128-192 width and alignment, origin chunk within 6, seeds differing above bit 32, offset
spread, shuffled queries, segment keys. Mutation: JITTER=2 fails the width test.
No P0 types used; tile/segment records are local to DistrictGrid.
