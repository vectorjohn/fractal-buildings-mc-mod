# 4.6 Hall / civic (step T-hall)

Branch `claude/p4-t-hall`. Owns `gen/v1/types/hall/**` (`HallType`, `HallMassing`, `HallRooms`,
`HallRoofs`, `HallFacade`, `HallArches`), `HallTypeTest`, `golden/v1/hall.txt`, `gallery-presets/hall-*.json`.
Shared files touched (additions only): `Types.java` (one line), `MassingOps.coreAnywhere`,
`RoofPass.run(c, RoofStyle)` overload (merged from the run branch), `V1Case` (lots over 120 get noise terrain, the recorded crop is too small).

## What it does

- **Massing**, four sub-kinds by draw (3:3:2:2), each from open (one-tall-space) masses; stairs only in
  small non-open towers or annexes, because a core can't stand in an open mass (M8).
  - *market*: open arcade storey + open upper hall (1-2 storeys, same footprint) + a stair annex beside it
    that joins both floors; hall >= 16 x 20.
  - *town*: tower at the front (main mass, entrance, core, 3-7 storeys) + tall open hall (2-3 storeys) behind it
    joined along the hall's front wall.
  - *cathedral* (lots >= ~41 x 38): nave 15-21 wide (3 storeys) + two aisles (1 storey) + transept across the end +
    narrower choir + 0-2 bell towers with cores beside the aisles. Strips between nave, aisles, transept and
    choir get 3-wide arches every 6 blocks (`HallArches`).
  - *bath*: four open ranges round a courtyard (front range 1-2 storeys, two side ranges, back range).
  - Retries with new sizes, then the next kind, then a plain open hall. Buildings stay <= 150 x 120 (halls <= 44 wide).
- **Roofs** (`HallRoofs`): the shared pass with the style's pitch lowered so no roof rises over 7 blocks
  (run = ceil(span * rise / 14)), no mansards. Highest roof block above its volume's top slab measured: 7.
- **Facade/openings** (`HallFacade`): shared bays with tall windows (up to 4) on open masses; market arcade
  = 3x3 glazed arches from the floor every 9 blocks on every ground face.
- **Rooms**: open halls are one `hall` room with stalls (`table`), `light`s, `chest`s scaling with area.
- Limits: envelope >= 20 x 20, margins 2/2/2, storeys 1-3, test lots 30x30, 56x44, 90x64, 120x80, 200x200,
  `maxGrade` 3, 40 seeds per lot.

## Verification

- `:planner-core:test :cli:test` pass (211 core tests). `V1PropertyTest` hall: 200 cases in ~1-2 s; also
  `-Dfb.propertyScale=10` (2000 cases incl. slopes 1:3, noise, recorded terrain): 0 violations.
- `HallTypeTest`: all four kinds >= 10 % on 116x76, no plain fallback on roomy lots, <= 5 % on 26x26 (no
  cathedral there); every nave/hall open with min side >= 15; footprint >= 20x20 over 4 envelopes x 256 seeds;
  furnishing; cathedral arches; market arches; roofs <= 10 above their volume (7 measured) also on 200x200;
  diversity floors (30x30: distance 0.7588 / 77 signatures, 90x64: 0.8829 / 89; floors 80 %); same seed same massing.
- `fractal-cli check --types hall --seeds 0..99` (default matrix): 16000 cases, 0 violations.
- Mutants: hall depth min 15 -> 8 fails the span test; arches removed fails the cathedral test; `MAX_RISE` 40
  fails the roof test (roof 23 high); seed ignored fails the diversity floors.
- 200x200 lot (noise terrain, 12 seeds, load ~40 on the host, so noisy): generate (shell + site) 8-72 ms
  (median ~14), invariant check 42-281 ms (median ~80); bounds up to 1.3 M cells.
- Gallery (`FB_PRESETS=hall-medieval,hall-plain,hall-flat,hall-big`, all cameras, 0 violations): halls read as
  civic buildings, not houses. Before roofs were lowered, a 1/1 gable on a 35-wide hall stood 17 high; after, flat-ish
  hips and 4-6-high gables. Cathedrals show the cross plan with a lower aisle roof and tall nave windows, towers
  with a hip or flat roof; baths show hips round the courtyard; market halls show a row of wide glazed arches.

## Decisions and deviations

- **Cathedral has no crossing tower** (plan said "crossing tower"): a core must stand in a non-open mass, the
  crossing is open. Bell towers beside the front aisles instead.
- **Market arcade is glazed arches, not door openings** (plan: "wide door openings"): extra exterior doors each
  get a flight of front steps (4.8) and on slopes the 2-block margin can't hold several flights; with doors,
  `entrance-reachable` failed in 3-9 of 200 property cases. Only the shared entrance is a door.
- `MassingOps.coreAnywhere`: cores at corners and middle, never the front inside row, for masses whose back wall is a
  shared strip (tower before a hall); `MassingOps.core` finds no place there.
- Hall window and arcade heights fixed (3-4); sizes ignore the style's massing keys except ground/typical heights (>= 5).
