# 2026-10-26 MINLOT (DS-8): lots on slopes leave room for a setback

Follow-up to LOTRAIL: 57 of 948 matrix buildings sat on lots at the type minimum with no room to step in from the lot line, so they kept a lot-line flight without a rail.

## What

- Rule (`SpecPolicyV1.railRoomOk`): ground relief over the lot of 2 or more (the least drop that gets a flight rail) needs a lot `RAIL_ROOM_WIDTH` = 2 wider and `RAIL_ROOM_DEPTH` = 1 deeper than the type minimum. Not for types with maxGrade > 3 (block) or wall types.
- Chosen (grow the lot, else let another type take the spot) because the lot planner can grow the lot instead of losing it: `Districts.dims` takes the grown minimum when the relief is known (the retry loop already re-ran with the measured relief), `ClassTable.pick` and `Briefs.buildable` drop a type that does not fit (so a brief swaps to another type or stays empty), landmark lots in `Districts` skip a spot without room, `SettlementChecks` LOT_GRADE reports it,.
- Relief threshold 3 was tried: 11 highrise 20x20 cases at relief 2 kept an unguarded edge, so 2 stays.
