# 6.18 ROOMWALK: rooms blocked by furniture (2026-10-27)

Branch `claude/p6-roomwalk`. Design source: interior answers Q1 and proposed DS-48 (not yet accepted: numbers are constants, ASSUMPTIONS rows).

## What changed
- **Invariant** `furnished-rooms-walkable` (`check/RoomWalk`, flood fill in `check/Walkability`, wired in `Invariants`, genVersion 2+): per room, the floor that is not blocked (floor below, two air cells, a single step up allowed; furniture and a bed's head block) joins the inside cell of every door and arch, the foot and top of every stair, and each piece's use cell (in front; a bed needs a cell beside either half). 2-wide lane (cells of a clear 2x2 square, plus one stub cell) for hall, shop, lobby, banquet, taproom, workshop, open rooms whose short side is at least 5.
- **Placement** (`interior/HookLayout`, called by `InteriorPass.hookSites`): required pieces before optional (table, bench, light), a trial flood fill per candidate, a piece with no lane-keeping cell is dropped, a bedroom without room for its bed becomes `storage` with a chest. Narrow rooms (short side 3): one long wall plus the end wall away from the door first. Targets the empty room cannot join are not asked of the furniture. Standing `light` dropped where a ceiling light hangs (LAMPS).
- **Beds**: head against a wall or partition (also along a wall with the head in a corner), both cells checked; hook = foot, `facing` toward the head, so `WorldHooks.headX` and the mod are unchanged.
- Fixes found by the invariant in code that places things after the interior pass: wide entrances now clear every inside cell (`InteriorPass.info`); `Shopfront` counter (use cell free, no piece walled in: `boxesIn`) and upstairs bed (head to the wall, fallbacks along the wall); `WalkOps` wall-walk doors avoid furniture, dropping it only when no cell is clear (`BuildContext.dropHooksNear`; without that fallback bastion rooms lost their only door, room-graph failures).
