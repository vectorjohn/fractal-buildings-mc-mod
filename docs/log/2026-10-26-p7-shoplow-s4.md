# 7.17 SHOPLOW S4: market hall rework (2026-10-26)

Branch `claude/p7-shoplow-s4`, plan [shoplow.md](../plans/shoplow.md) S4, with the architect's A6 and the interior designer's I1 answers overriding it.

## What changed

- **Spec and brief**: `BuildingSpec.square`, `Brief.square`, `LotBrief.square` (JSON key `square`, written only when true, so every other spec hash and district file is unchanged); `SpecPolicyV1` keeps it for shops on relief <= 1; CLI `--square` (`gen`, `batch`, `floorplan`); gallery preset key `square`; viewer `ir.ts` reads it.
- **Which lot** (`Briefs.markSquareLots`): per market or square plaza (greens are village and hamlet plazas: no halls there), the lot with the lowest hash among shop lots with relief <= 1, a front of 11+ (envelope 9) whose middle looks at the plaza within 6 blocks; on 60 % of plazas; never two lots on one square.
- **The hall** (`ShopType`, `ShopMassing`, `Shopfront`): `groundMode` is MARKET for `square` and envelope >= 9 (the old 20 % draw is gone: non-square shops are arcade 30 % or enclosed); legs 3, 3 or 4 from width 11 (`Stilts.MIN_LIFT` 3; towers keep 4; no beam ring at lift 3); no front landing; no shopfront; the facade entrance is virtual (`OpeningPass.run` accepts a cutter that returns null), so there is no door in any wall.
- **The stair**: `Core.LEG` (from = -1): a core along the back edge whose first flight (`InteriorPass.flight(... fromGround ...)`) starts on the paved open floor (a `foundation` slab over the footprint), is 1 wide, rises `lift` steps along the back row and leaves through a hole cut in the first floor; flights of the storeys above stack on it as in any house. Fence stringers on both sides of its higher steps; rails at the opening's west end and (one-storey halls) its front side. Opening `stair` with `from: "outside"`.
- **Invariants** (`Invariants`, `StairRise`): the stair from outside is the hall's door for `rooms-reachable`, `entrance-reachable` (walk from its foot to the terrain) and `shell-closed` (its shaft is open on purpose); `stair-rise` reads the cell before it at ground level only (before, the hall's own floor over the foot hid a missing floor).
- **Rooms** (I1): one room per floor (`ShopType.MARKET_FLOORS`): `hall` on the first floor (hooks table, hearth, ceiling light grid (two from a 12-long room), chest, light: a table group and a fireplace group, no lectern), `store` above (chests, a table, ceiling lights). No bedrooms, no counter.

## Known issues

- Halls almost never appear in a planned settlement: the district planner puts only landmark lots (hall, temple) and streets round a square, so no shop lot faces it (17 real planned cities: 0 hall lots; 150 synthetic sites: 38 hall lots over 772 market/square plazas). Needs ordinary lots round squares (architect to specify; touches `Districts`).
- No council chamber (`long_table_bench` piece) and no village store (villages have greens only).
- The open floor has no stalls or other furniture (a plain paved floor under posts).
