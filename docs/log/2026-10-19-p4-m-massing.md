# 4.2 Massing (step M)

Branch `claude/p4-m-massing`. House massing for generator v1: plans with wings, jetties and
setbacks, built from reusable ops.

## What was done

- `gen/v1/mass/MassingOps`: `grid`, `main`, `wing` (E/W/S side, align start/centre/end, strip =
  wing width, always at least 2 narrower than its side), `jetty` (1..style max <= 2 per side, parent =
  volume below), `setback`, `stack` (podium/shaft/crown, for wave-2 towers), `core` (common interior of
  a stack, off the front row, not next to a shared strip). Each returns null rather than a bad mass.
- `gen/v1/mass/HouseMassing`: draws a plan from `massing.plans` weights (box, L, T, U, courtyard),
  wings with their own storeys (40 % one lower, 35 % same, 25 % one higher, capped by the type), then
  on the main block a jetty (1 or 2 steps, up to 4 sides) or a set-back top storey. Each differing
  storey is its own mass with the one below as parent. A wing that is taller than its neighbours gets
  its own core from the storey it is no longer linked. Anything that fails `MassingCheck` backs off
  (second jetty step, then sides back/east/west/front, then a plain stack), then a simpler plan, then
  fewer storeys.
- `MassingStyle` gained `plans`, `jettyChance`, `setbackChance`, `groundExtra`, `groundExtraChance`
  (defaults keep other types unchanged). plain: box 4, L 3, T 2, no jetty, setback 15 %. medieval:
  all five plans, jetty 1 at 40 %, setback 15 %, ground storey +1 at 25 %.
- `HouseType.mass` calls `HouseMassing.build`. `StructurePass` and `MassingCheck` unchanged.
- Fixtures appended to `TestMassings.all()`: `jettyStep`, `setbackWing`, `tallWingCore`. `house.txt` golden regenerated.

## Verification

- `:planner-core:test :cli:test` green (152 tests); `MassingOpsTest` (10,000 draws per op: wing strip
  >= 3 and narrower than its side, jetty overhang in [1, max] on >= 1 side and 0 elsewhere, setback
  strictly inside, stack bands, core inside every host), `HouseMassingTest` (2 x 1000 seeds, 18x18
  and 29x15): medieval 56 % multi-volume (>= 40), wings that differ 100 % of those (>= 70), 507 of 1312
  (39 %) 2-3 storey houses jettied (>= 20), setbacks occur; plain: 0 jetties. Every massing passes `MassingCheck`.
- `V1PropertyTest` also at `-Dfb.propertyScale=10`: green. `check --seeds 0..999 --types house`: 128,000 cases, 0 violations.
- Mutants: overhang limit removed -> `MassingOpsTest.jettyOverhangsWithinTheStyleMax` fails; wings made
  the size of the main block with the same storeys -> `HouseMassingTest` fails (3 tests).
- Gallery (`npm run gallery`, medieval flat, front-left): before (F0) every house was one gabled box.
  After: seed 15 courtyard with a ring roof; seeds 1, 3, 4, 17, 24, 42, 46 L/T/U with wings; seed 2 and
  20 a low wing against a taller block; seed 5 and 13 a tall back wing; jetties (upper storeys wider,
  overhanging the front) on seeds 16 and 27; setbacks (narrower top storey) on seeds 2 and 32. All cells 0 violations.
- Diversity (medieval, seeds 0..255): 20x20 mean Jaccard 0.82, 0 near-duplicates, 18 distinct
  (w, d, storeys, rooms) signatures (see NOTES).

## Decisions

- Wings only attach to the main block and wing storeys may exceed the spec's storeys (<= type max);
  the grid count is the tallest mass.
- Front z offset is 1..2 (not 0) when there is room, so a front jetty fits.
