# FLY2 r4 (2026-10-27)

Architect should-fix round (`.claude/reports/p6-fly2-architect.md`):

- Street-level frames per settlement: `street` (longest main street, else arterial, else street, from the flattest 90-block stretch, eye 3 over the surface, rd 16), `square` (nearest plaza to the centre, seen from across it toward the nearest hall or temple landmark within 80; else the plaza's own middle), `bridge` (city and metropolis; longest `DECK` box of 12+, 30+ blocks to the lower side). Missing street, plaza or bridge is a log note, not an error.
- `interior`: the largest ground-floor room of 4x4 or more (not a stair) in the densest tile, camera in the corner farthest from the nearest street at eye 1.62, looking at the opposite corner. The standing level is read from the live blocks.
- Repeatable: `--poses FILE` replays a manifest (same `tp`, `rd`, file names); the manifest `commit` is the real short sha (`.fb-commit` shipped by `scripts/remote.sh`, since a remote copy has no `.git`; `-dirty` for an unclean tree). A pose set of the rd10 run is in `docs/review/phase6/flythrough/poses-rd10-71d7b0c5.tsv`.
- Sweeps: sweep-a ranks windows by built volume (a downtown of towers beats a wider suburb) for city and metropolis; sweep-b is the built rim (farthest window from the centre with at least 30 % of the busiest window's buildings) seen from the core side looking out over the taper; hall and temple are lots of that type (not landmarks); a bridge is the longest run of a street over water (city decks use the road role).
- Frame has a per-frame `rd`; street-level frames use 12-16 (nothing far is in them), so they settle in seconds.

Verified: see the report `.claude/reports/p6-fly2-r4.md` (evidence folders, frame review). Lesson: two flythrough runs in one worktree at once corrupt each other (shared output dir and client run dir).
