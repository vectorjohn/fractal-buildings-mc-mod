# 6.25 MAINSTUB: main roads that end at a gate with nothing beyond

Branch `claude/p6-mainstub` (coder), ROADMAP 6.25; spec: architect reviews `.claude/reports/p6-deadport-architect.md` and `playtest1028-qs-architect.md` Q2 (DS-56 clause, proposed).

## Done
- **`ExitTrim`** (settlement package, pure): an exit road with nothing beyond its gate keeps its road only up to the last built lot, plaza or other main road / arterial beside it, plus `KEEP` = 8 columns (one lot depth); a bridge that would be the stub's last stretch goes too (the cut moves to its start). Candidates: an `EXIT` gate no drawn approach leaves from (`SkeletonSource.exitServed`: hashed exits, and the exit of a link the road layer dropped), and a `CELL` gate whose neighbour cell's skeleton has no road at the gate (6.16's known issue). A road with nothing beside it at all (a hamlet's only street) is kept whole and counted as `unserved`.
- **World:** `WorldPlanner.plan(ix, iz)` is now the raw plan with the cut applied to the street pieces of the cut roads (`ExitTrim.apply`; lots, plazas, landmarks, buildings unchanged; same object when no piece is cut). The raw plan (`rawPlan`) is what the road layer reads (`streetLevel`, `onLots`), so the link rule never reads a cut plan: link state reads approaches, approaches read raw plans, the cut reads link state and raw plans. No cycle, nothing depends on which settlement or chunk is asked first. `exitCuts(site)` is memoised per settlement (floor 4096 entries like the link memos).
- **CLI / `CityPlanner.plan`:** same cut from the skeleton approaches (the CLI has no link rule); shortens the settlement roads, removes the cut `EXIT` gates (a `CELL` gate keeps its place and loses its road), and adds the `exit-stub` rule to the violations.
- **`exit-stub` check** (`SettlementChecks.exitStubs`): a gateless exit road runs at most `MAX_STUB` = 16 columns past its last built lot / plaza / road beside it.

## Decisions and surprises
- A junction only counts when the other street goes somewhere (a skeleton road or arterial, or a street with a built lot beside it): cutting past a lattice street that serves a lot would cut that lot off (`roads-connected` failed in `CityRoadsTest`, `RoadLatticeTest`, `CityProgramTest` until then). So w12345 s2_1 g4 is cut by 8 columns, not 84: a lattice street with a built lot crosses it 16 blocks from the gate. s2_1 g1 (187 columns) and s6_6 g13 (193) are the long ones.
- A lane that hung on a cut-off tail goes with it (`ExitTrim.dropOrphans`: streets that reached a road or the tile border before the cut and no longer do).
- A road with a wall gate keeps its road through the gate plus 8 columns; the `WALL` gate stays.
- Only built lots (`status build`) count as frontage; unbuilt ones (`empty:steep`, `empty:bank`) do not (the architect's 84 counted them).
- Per-tile determinism tests (`MultiCellCityTest`, `SettlementDeterminismTest`, `CityPlannerTest`) now plan tiles alone with `CityPlanner.planTileTrimmed`, which plans the other tiles along the cut roads on the way.
- 20.02.18's road is not a stub by this rule (built lots at the gate). Left as is; the architect decides whether a bridge to a small cluster at a hashed exit is wanted.

## Known issues
- No turning head at the cut end (cities): the road simply stops. A plaza record would pull in landmark and ground handling; left for the architect's DS-56 wording.
- The corridor of a dropped link's approach stays lot-free (plans read `approachNodes` before the link rule; releasing it would make plans depend on link state, which depends on plans through `streetLevel`: a cycle).
- `fractal-cli roads` / `city --roads` do not know the link rule, so the CLI never cuts a dropped link's exit (the world does).
